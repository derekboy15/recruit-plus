Stratagems are special rules you pay for with Command Points (CP). Every army can use the core stratagems, and each faction's codex adds its own. This page explains the rules that apply to all of them; the full list of core stratagems, with their wording, is on the [stratagems page](/rules/stratagems).

## Command Points

Command Points are your army's budget for stratagems. You can't use a stratagem unless you have enough CP to pay its cost.

### Starting CP

A **Battle-forged** army starts the battle with a pool of CP. How many depends on the battle size and the mission pack you are playing; see [Battle sizes](/rules/matched-play#battle-sizes). Before the battle you also spend some of these CP on things other than stratagems, such as extra detachments.

An **Unbound** army starts with no CP. It can still spend CP that it gains from other rules, such as mission rules.

### Gaining CP during the battle

- **Battle-forged bonus:** if your army is Battle-forged, you gain 1 CP at the start of your Command phase.
- **Grand Tournament missions:** in the War Zone Nephilim and Arks of Omen mission packs, both players gain 1 CP in **every** Command phase, yours and your opponent's.
- **Other rules:** some abilities, stratagems and mission rules give you extra CP, or refund CP you spent.

### The CP cap

You can't gain or be refunded more than **1 CP per battle round** from rules. These don't count towards that limit:

- the Battle-forged bonus;
- CP gained in the Command phase from mission rules;
- CP gained from a stratagem used during a phase.

CP you spend on stratagems before the battle can **never** be refunded.

## Using a stratagem

Each stratagem says when you can use it, for example "at the start of your Shooting phase" or "after an enemy unit has fought". To use one:

1. Check that the moment it describes has come, and that you have a unit that meets its conditions.
2. Pay its CP cost.
3. Resolve its effect.

You can use a stratagem in your opponent's turn as well as your own, if its timing allows. Fire Overwatch, for example, is used in your opponent's Charge phase.

Some stratagems are used **before the battle**, while you muster your army. These include the Requisition stratagems below and many codex "upgrade" stratagems. Record them on your army roster before the game.

## Once per phase

You can use the same stratagem many times in a battle, but **not more than once in the same phase**.

- It is why only one of your units can usually fire Overwatch in each enemy Charge phase: Fire Overwatch is a stratagem, so you can use it only once per phase.
- Stratagems that aren't used **during a phase**, such as ones used before the battle or at the end of a battle round, aren't limited by this rule.

Some stratagems have tighter limits written on them, such as "once per battle". Insane Bravery is one of these.

## Stratagems with the same name

Two stratagems with **identical names** count as the same stratagem, even if they come from different books. So if your codex and a supplement both have a stratagem with the same name, you can still only use that name once per phase between them.

## Limits on what stratagems can affect

### Out-of-phase rules

Some rules let a unit act outside its normal phase, for example "shoot as if it were the Shooting phase". Those actions follow that phase's normal rules, but **stratagems that are used in a particular phase can't be used on them**. A "use in your Shooting phase" stratagem, for example, can't be used on a unit that is shooting out of phase.

### Embarked units

Units embarked inside a TRANSPORT can't be affected by stratagems, unless the stratagem says otherwise. Emergency Disembarkation is written to work when the transport is destroyed.

### Re-rolls

Command Re-roll follows the normal re-roll rules: a dice can never be re-rolled more than once. You can't use Command Re-roll on a dice that has already been re-rolled by another rule.

## Changing a stratagem's cost

Some rules change how many CP a stratagem costs. Since the June 2021 FAQ, apply them in this order:

1. First, rules that **set** the cost to a fixed number.
2. Then, rules that **increase or decrease** the cost.

## The core stratagems

Every army can use these seven stratagems. Their costs never changed during 9th edition. The [stratagems page](/rules/stratagems) has the full wording of each.

| Stratagem | CP | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Command Re-roll** | 1 | After you make certain rolls, such as a hit, wound, save or charge roll |
| **Counter-offensive** | 2 | After an enemy unit has fought in the Fight phase |
| **Insane Bravery** | 2 | Before one of your units takes a Morale test (once per battle) |
| **Fire Overwatch** | 1 | After an enemy unit declares a charge against one of your units |
| **Cut Them Down** | 1 | When an enemy unit Falls Back, before it moves |
| **Desperate Breakout** | 2 | In your Movement phase, for a unit that is within Engagement Range and hasn't moved |
| **Emergency Disembarkation** | 1 | When one of your TRANSPORT models is destroyed |

See also the [Fight phase](/rules/fight-phase#stratagems-in-the-fight-phase) for Counter-offensive and the [Morale phase](/rules/morale-phase#insane-bravery) for Insane Bravery.

## Requisition stratagems

The War Zone Nephilim (2022) and Arks of Omen (2023) Grand Tournament mission packs added **Requisition stratagems**. You use them before the battle, while mustering your army, and each can be used only once per army.

| Stratagem | CP | Mission packs | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warlord Trait** | 1 | Nephilim, Arks of Omen | Gives your CHARACTER Warlord a Warlord Trait |
| **Relic** | 1 | Nephilim, Arks of Omen | Gives one CHARACTER that isn't a named character a Relic |
| **Heroic Support** | 1 | Arks of Omen | Lets you take up to two of a unit that a detachment rule limits to one per detachment (Gene-sect limits excluded) |

In these packs your Warlord doesn't get a free Warlord Trait or Relic; you buy them with these stratagems. Codex stratagems that give extra Warlord Traits or Relics still work on top of them.

## Stratagems from other game modes

In the **Boarding Actions** game mode from the Arks of Omen books, you can only use three core stratagems: Command Re-roll, Counter-offensive and Insane Bravery, at their normal costs.

## Not core stratagems in 9th edition

If you have seen 10th edition material, you may expect more core stratagems. These were **not** core stratagems in 9th edition:

- **Grenade** and **Tank Shock**. Some 9th edition codexes had their own faction stratagems that did similar things.
- **Heroic Intervention**. In 9th edition it is a free rule for CHARACTER units in the enemy Charge phase; see the [Charge phase](/rules/charge-phase).
- **Rapid Ingress**, **Smokescreen**, **Go to Ground** and **Epic Challenge**.
