The Command phase is the first phase of every turn. It is short: you gain Command Points, use any rules that happen in this phase and, in most matched play missions, score your primary objective.

## What happens in the Command phase

Your Command phase runs in this order:

1. **Gain Command Points.** At the very start of the phase, you gain your CP for the turn. See [Gaining Command Points](#gaining-command-points).
2. **Use Command-phase rules.** Resolve any abilities, Stratagems and mission rules that say they happen in the Command phase. See [Command-phase abilities](#command-phase-abilities).
3. **Score objectives.** In most matched play missions, primary objectives are scored at the end of your Command phase. See [Scoring at the end of the phase](#scoring-at-the-end-of-the-phase).

Units don't move, shoot or fight in this phase. Once everything above is done, move on to your Movement phase.

> **Tip:** Older 8th edition material has no Command phase: turns started with the Movement phase. In 9th edition every turn starts here.

## Gaining Command Points

**Command Points (CP)** are the currency you spend on Stratagems and on some choices you make before the battle. You start the battle with a pool of CP set by the battle size; see [Battle sizes](/rules/matched-play#battle-sizes).

### The Battle-forged CP bonus

If your army is **Battle-forged** (organised into detachments), you gain **1 CP** at the very start of each of your Command phases. This is the Battle-forged CP bonus. An army that isn't Battle-forged doesn't get it.

Under the core rules, you only gain this CP in **your own** Command phase. That means the player going second is one CP behind during the first player's turn of every battle round.

### Matched play missions: both players gain CP every Command phase

The matched play mission packs from War Zone Nephilim (2022) onward, including the final Arks of Omen pack (2023), changed this. In those missions:

- **Both players** gain **1 CP** at the start of **every** Command phase: yours and your opponent's.
- Over a 5-round game, that is 10 CP from Command phases instead of 5.
- Those packs also give you fewer CP at the start of a Strike Force battle: 6 instead of 12.

> **Tip:** If you play a mission from the Core Book or an earlier mission pack, only the player whose turn it is gains the CP. Check which mission pack you are using.

## The CP gain cap

You can't stack up CP endlessly from abilities. In each battle round, a player can't **gain** or be **refunded** more than **1 CP** from rules, in total. Any extra CP that would be gained or refunded that round are lost.

These don't count towards the cap:

- the Battle-forged CP bonus;
- CP that a mission gives you in the Command phase;
- Stratagems that themselves give you CP when you use them during a phase.

CP you spend on Stratagems before the battle can never be refunded.

For example, if two of your abilities would each refund 1 CP in the same battle round, only the first refund happens. The CP you gain at the start of your Command phase is still yours, because it's exempt.

## Spending Command Points

You spend CP on **Stratagems**: special rules you can use when their conditions are met, in any phase they name. You can't use a Stratagem if you don't have enough CP to pay for it.

Two limits always apply:

- You can't use the same Stratagem more than once in the same phase. Stratagems with identical names from different books count as the same Stratagem.
- A Stratagem that names a phase can't be used when a unit acts "as if it were" that phase.

The core Stratagems that every army can use, such as Command Re-roll, are listed on the [Stratagems](/rules/stratagems) page.

Before the battle, CP can also pay for:

- detachments in your army;
- units you place in [Strategic Reserves](/rules/movement-phase#strategic-reserves);
- pre-battle Requisition Stratagems, such as giving a CHARACTER a Warlord Trait or a Relic, in matched play mission packs that use them.

In the Arks of Omen mission pack, detachments and Strategic Reserves cost 0 CP, so you spend your CP only on Stratagems.

## Command-phase abilities

Many army rules happen in the Command phase. Codexes written for 9th edition moved a lot of choices here. For example:

- some CHARACTERS choose or activate abilities that last for the turn, such as a Space Marine Chaplain's litanies;
- some armies pick a rule that affects the whole army this turn, such as the Necron Command Protocols;
- some missions give out CP or other rewards in this phase.

Your datasheets, codex and mission tell you exactly which rules are used in the Command phase. Resolve them after you gain your CP. If several of your rules happen at the same time, you choose the order, because it is your turn.

## Scoring at the end of the phase

In most matched play missions, **primary objectives** are scored at the **end of your Command phase**, starting in battle round 2. Usually you score for holding objective markers. Each mission's briefing explains what you score for, and some missions also score part of their primary objective at other times.

Because you score before you move, what counts is the objectives you hold at the start of your turn.

In the Grand Tournament mission packs (including Arks of Omen), there is one exception in the **last battle round (round 5)**:

- The player who goes first scores their primary objective in their Command phase, as normal.
- The player who goes second scores their primary objective at the **end of their turn** instead.

This gives the second player a full final turn to take objectives before scoring.

Secondary objectives have their own scoring times, written on each one. See [Secondary objectives](/rules/matched-play#secondary-objectives).

## Example

You are playing a Strike Force game from the Arks of Omen mission pack, and you are going second. It is battle round 2.

1. Your opponent's turn comes first. At the start of their Command phase, you **both** gain 1 CP, because this mission pack gives CP to both players in every Command phase.
2. Your turn begins. At the start of your Command phase, you both gain 1 CP again.
3. You then resolve your Command-phase abilities, such as choosing which litany your Chaplain will chant this turn.
4. At the end of your Command phase, you score your primary objective for the objective markers you control.
5. Earlier this battle round, an ability refunded you 1 CP after you used a Stratagem. If a second ability tries to refund you a CP before the round ends, it can't: you have hit the CP gain cap. The CP from the Command phases don't count towards that cap.

Now move on to your Movement phase.
