## INFANTRY

A keyword for soldiers on foot. INFANTRY models get cover from Obstacles and Area Terrain, can move through walls and floors of some terrain, and take `-1` to hit with Heavy weapons if their unit moved. Each INFANTRY unit in a detachment lets you take one Dedicated Transport. See [Terrain](/rules/terrain) and [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## BEAST

A keyword for animals and creatures. BEAST models share INFANTRY's terrain benefits: cover from Obstacles and Area Terrain, and moving through walls and floors. They don't suffer the Heavy weapon penalty, which only applies to INFANTRY. See [Terrain](/rules/terrain).

## SWARM

A keyword for bases of many small creatures, such as Ripper Swarms or Nurglings. SWARM models get the same terrain benefits as INFANTRY and BEAST models. See [Terrain](/rules/terrain).

## VEHICLE

A keyword for tanks, walkers and other machines. VEHICLE models can shoot while in Engagement Range thanks to [Big Guns Never Tire](#big-guns-never-tire), and VEHICLE models can protect nearby characters through [Look Out, Sir](/rules/shooting-phase#look-out-sir) (a VEHICLE that is itself a CHARACTER needs 10 or more wounds). They don't get cover from Obstacles or Area Terrain, but Dense Cover still helps them. See [Terrain](/rules/terrain).

## MONSTER

A keyword for huge creatures. MONSTER models follow the same Big Guns Never Tire rules as VEHICLE models, and MONSTER models can protect nearby characters through Look Out, Sir (a MONSTER that is itself a CHARACTER needs 10 or more wounds). See [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## TITANIC

A keyword for the very largest models, such as Knights and titanic monsters. A TITANIC unit can still shoot and manifest psychic powers after it Falls Back. If every model in the unit is TITANIC, it ignores Difficult Ground on terrain under `3"` tall. See [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase) and [Terrain](/rules/terrain).

## CHARACTER

A keyword for heroes and leaders. A CHARACTER with 9 or fewer wounds is protected by [Look Out, Sir](/rules/shooting-phase#look-out-sir), and CHARACTERS can make [Heroic Interventions](#heroic-intervention). If your army has any CHARACTERS, your Warlord must be one of them; see [The Warlord](/rules/army-building#the-warlord).

## FLY

A keyword for models that can fly. When a FLY model makes a Normal Move, Advance or Fall Back, it can move over other models and ignores vertical distance, but it can't end on top of a model or within Engagement Range of an enemy. When it charges, it can pass over models but must climb terrain normally. See [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase).

## AIRCRAFT

A keyword for planes and other fast flyers. AIRCRAFT have a minimum and maximum move, can't control objectives and can't perform actions. In matched play you can only take 1, 2 or 3 AIRCRAFT models depending on battle size, and they start the battle in Strategic Reserves. See [Aircraft](/rules/matched-play#aircraft) and [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase).

## TRANSPORT

A keyword for models that can carry other units. A unit embarks by ending a move with every model within `3"` of the transport, and disembarks by being set up wholly within `3"` of it before it moves. If the transport is destroyed, you roll a `D6` for each model inside, and each 1 kills one of them. See [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase).

## PSYKER

A keyword for psychic models. A PSYKER knows Smite and the other powers on its datasheet, can try to deny enemy powers within `24"`, and can perform psychic actions. See [Psychic phase](/rules/psychic-phase) and [Smite](/rules/psychic-phase#smite).

## CAVALRY, BIKER and JUMP PACK

Keywords for mounted troops, bikes and jump infantry. The core rules don't give them special treatment, but because they aren't INFANTRY they don't get cover from Obstacles or Area Terrain and can't move through walls and floors. Codexes and missions often refer to them. See [Terrain](/rules/terrain).

## BUILDING and Fortifications

Buildings are units, not terrain features: they can be attacked, but models can't move through them. Fortifications can't be your Warlord, never control objectives, never perform actions and never go into Strategic Reserves. See [Building a legal army](/rules/army-building#other-matched-play-limits).

## WARLORD

The keyword the model you choose as your army's leader gains. Your Warlord can have a Warlord Trait, and some detachments and secondary objectives depend on it. See [The Warlord](/rules/army-building#the-warlord).

## PRIMARCH, DAEMON PRIMARCH and SUPREME COMMANDER

Keywords for the mightiest leaders. In Core Book games they can be taken in a Supreme Command Detachment, where they must be your Warlord. In Arks of Omen games your army can include only one SUPREME COMMANDER. See [Building a legal army](/rules/army-building#core-book-detachments).

## UNALIGNED

A keyword for units that belong to no faction, such as generic Fortifications. UNALIGNED units don't have to share your Army Faction. See [Battle-forged armies](/rules/army-building#battle-forged-armies).

## AGENT OF THE IMPERIUM and AGENT OF CHAOS

Keywords for specialist units that can join other armies, such as Inquisitors and assassins. An AGENT OF THE IMPERIUM can join an IMPERIUM detachment without using a slot and without switching off that detachment's faction rules. See [Agents of the Imperium](/rules/army-building#agents-of-the-imperium).

## CORE

A keyword that marks the main fighting units of an army, added with the first 9th edition codexes in late 2020. Many character auras and Stratagems only affect CORE units, so big vehicles, monsters and elite units often can't be buffed by them. Check each datasheet: it isn't limited to Troops or INFANTRY. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## The Most Important Rule

If you're not sure how a situation should be resolved, agree a sensible answer with your opponent. If you can't agree, roll off and the winner decides. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Unit

One or more models from the same datasheet, which move and fight together. "Friendly" units are in your army and "enemy" units are in your opponent's. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Unit coherency

Each model in a unit must be within `2"` horizontally and `5"` vertically of at least one other model in its unit. A unit of 6 or more models needs each model to be that close to two others. See [Unit coherency](/rules/basics#unit-coherency).

## Engagement Range

A model is within Engagement Range of an enemy model when it is within `1"` horizontally and `5"` vertically of it. Units in Engagement Range are locked in combat. See [Engagement Range](/rules/basics#engagement-range).

## Within and wholly within

A unit is **within** a distance if any part of any of its models is inside it. It is **wholly within** only if every part of every model is inside. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Hull

The part of a model without a base that you measure to. In 9th edition the hull is any part of the model, including turrets and sponsons. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Starting Strength

The number of models in a unit at the start of the battle. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Below Half-strength

A unit is below Half-strength when it has fewer than half its Starting Strength models left. A unit with exactly half its models left is not below Half-strength. See [Morale phase](/rules/morale-phase).

## Destroyed

A unit is destroyed when its last model is removed. It only counts as destroyed by you if your attack, your mortal wounds or your "destroy" effect killed that last model, or if it fled in the Morale phase. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Battle round, turn and phase

In each **battle round**, both players take one **turn**. Each turn is made of seven **phases** in a fixed order: Command, Movement, Psychic, Shooting, Charge, Fight and Morale. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Command phase

The first phase of each turn. It is when you gain your Battle-forged CP bonus, use command abilities and, in many missions, score primary objectives. See [Command phase](/rules/command-phase).

## Battle-forged and Unbound

A **Battle-forged** army has all its units organised into detachments and gets Command Points. An **Unbound** army doesn't use detachments and gets no starting CP. Matched play armies must be Battle-forged. See [Battle-forged armies](/rules/army-building#battle-forged-armies).

## Battle-forged CP bonus

A Battle-forged army gains 1 CP at the start of its own Command phase. In Arks of Omen games, both players gain 1 CP at the start of every Command phase. See [Command phase](/rules/command-phase).

## Command Points (CP)

The currency you spend on Stratagems, Requisitions and, in Core Book games, on detachments and Strategic Reserves. Your starting CP depends on the battle size. See [Detachments and Command Points](/rules/army-building#detachments-and-command-points).

## Stratagem

A special rule you pay CP to use. You can use each Stratagem only once per phase, and Stratagems with the same name count as the same Stratagem. See [How stratagems work](/rules/core-stratagems).

## Requisition Stratagem

A Stratagem you buy while mustering your army, before the battle. The Arks of Omen Grand Tournament pack gives every army three: Warlord Trait, Relic and Heroic Support. See [Requisition stratagems](/rules/army-building#requisition-stratagems).

## Detachment

An organisation chart that your units are placed into, with compulsory and optional slots for each Battlefield Role. In Core Book games each detachment has a CP cost. See [Detachments and Command Points](/rules/army-building#detachments-and-command-points).

## Detachment abilities

Faction rules, such as Chapter Tactics or Objective Secured on Troops, that a detachment gains when every unit in it shares the right Faction keyword. Some detachments, such as Auxiliary Support, never gain them. See [Detachments and Command Points](/rules/army-building#detachments-and-command-points).

## Army of Renown

An optional themed army from a codex or supplement, with its own restrictions and bonus rules. You must choose it explicitly, it must be Battle-forged, and it can't use Specialist Detachments. See [Building a legal army](/rules/army-building).

## Specialist Detachment

An older kind of detachment upgrade, bought with a Stratagem, that gave extra Warlord Traits, Relics and Stratagems. The GT packs ban them. See [Other matched play limits](/rules/army-building#other-matched-play-limits).

## Power Rating and Power Level

A **Power Rating** is a rough value for a unit, printed on its datasheet. Your army's **Power Level** is the total of its Power Ratings. Power Level is used mainly in open and narrative play, and to price Strategic Reserves in Core Book games. See [Battle-forged armies](/rules/army-building#battle-forged-armies).

## Points and the Munitorum Field Manual

Points are the detailed costs of each model and its wargear, used to build matched play armies. They were published in the Munitorum Field Manual, which Games Workshop updated regularly. See [Battle-forged armies](/rules/army-building#battle-forged-armies).

## Understrength unit

A unit with fewer models than its datasheet's minimum size. Understrength units can't go in standard detachments and aren't allowed in matched play. See [Other matched play limits](/rules/army-building#other-matched-play-limits).

## Reinforcement points

Points you keep back when building your army, to pay for units added during the battle, such as summoned units. See [Your army roster](/rules/army-building#your-army-roster).

## Reinforcement unit

Any unit that starts the battle somewhere other than the battlefield. It arrives in the Reinforcements step of your Movement phase and counts as having moved its full Move characteristic. If it hasn't arrived by the end of the battle, it counts as destroyed. See [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase).

## Strategic Reserves

The core rules for keeping units off the table at the start of the battle. They arrive from battle round 2, within `6"` of a battlefield edge other than your opponent's and more than `9"` from enemy models. In Core Book games they cost CP; in Arks of Omen games they are free. See [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase) and [Reserve limits](/rules/matched-play#reserve-limits).

## Normal Move, Advance, Fall Back and Remain Stationary

The four things a unit can do when you select it in your [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase): a **Normal Move** goes up to its Move characteristic, and a unit that **Remains Stationary** doesn't move. An **Advance** adds a `D6` to the move but stops the unit shooting (except with Assault weapons) or charging. A **Fall Back** leaves Engagement Range but stops the unit charging, and shooting or manifesting psychic powers unless it is TITANIC.

## Charge move and charge roll

To charge, roll `2D6`: that's the furthest each model can move. The charge only works if the unit can end within Engagement Range of every unit it declared as a target. See [Charging](/rules/charge-phase#charging).

## Heroic Intervention

After your opponent's charges, a CHARACTER within `3"` horizontally and `5"` vertically of an enemy unit can move up to `3"` towards the closest enemy model. See [Charge phase](/rules/charge-phase).

## Pile in and Consolidate

Short moves a unit makes when it fights. It piles in up to `3"` before attacking and consolidates up to `3"` after, each time ending closer to the closest enemy model. See [Fight phase](/rules/fight-phase).

## Fights first and fights last

Units that charged this turn fight first. If a unit is affected by both a fights-first and a fights-last rule, they cancel out and it fights as normal. See [Fight phase](/rules/fight-phase).

## Overwatch

Shooting at an enemy unit that has declared a charge against yours, before it makes its charge roll. Overwatch only hits on unmodified rolls of 6. You normally do it with the 1 CP Fire Overwatch Stratagem. See [Charge phase](/rules/charge-phase) and [Stratagems](/rules/stratagems).

## Big Guns Never Tire

VEHICLE and MONSTER models can shoot while their unit is in Engagement Range, but only at enemy units in Engagement Range of their unit. Heavy weapons fired this way take `-1` to hit. See [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## Look Out, Sir

A rule that stops enemies shooting your CHARACTERS with 9 or fewer wounds while they are near certain friendly units, unless the character is the closest visible target. See [Look Out, Sir](/rules/shooting-phase#look-out-sir).

## Blast

A weapon ability. A Blast weapon always makes at least 3 attacks against a unit of 6 to 10 models, and its maximum number of attacks against a unit of 11 or more. It can never shoot at a unit within Engagement Range of the firing unit. See [Blast](/rules/shooting-phase#blast).

## Mortal wound

A wound that causes 1 damage with no wound roll and no saving throw, not even an invulnerable save. Unlike normal damage, extra mortal wounds carry over to the next model. See [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## Invulnerable save

A saving throw that AP never changes. If a model has one, you choose whether to use it or the normal armour save. Cover never improves it. See [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## Ignoring wounds (Feel No Pain)

Rules that let you roll to ignore each wound a model would lose. Players often call them "Feel No Pain". A model can only use one such rule for each wound. See [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## Re-roll

Rolling a dice again. Re-rolls happen before modifiers, and you can never re-roll the same dice more than once. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Unmodified roll

A dice result after any re-rolls but before any modifiers. Many rules trigger on an unmodified 6. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Modifier cap

Hit rolls and wound rolls can never be modified by more than `-1` or `+1` in total. Saving throws have no such cap. See [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## Aura ability

An ability that affects units within a set range of the model that has it. Auras with the same name don't stack, and a CHARACTER performing an action can't use its auras. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Action

A task your unit spends its turn on, often to score one of your [secondary objectives](/rules/matched-play#secondary-objectives). A unit can't start an action if it's in Engagement Range or it Advanced or Fell Back this turn, and AIRCRAFT and Fortifications can't do them at all. The action fails if the unit moves, shoots, charges, Heroically Intervenes, attempts a psychic power or is destroyed before it finishes. Each unit can do one action per battle round.

## Psychic Action

An action a PSYKER performs in your Psychic phase instead of manifesting powers. It needs a Psychic test, can cause Perils of the Warp and can be denied like a psychic power. See [Psychic phase](/rules/psychic-phase) and [Perils of the Warp](/rules/psychic-phase#perils-of-the-warp).

## Objective marker

A 40mm round marker on the battlefield that missions ask you to control. A model is in range of it when within `3"` horizontally and `5"` vertically. See [Objective markers](/rules/matched-play#objective-markers).

## Controlling an objective

You control an objective marker if you have more models in range of it than your opponent. Each model counts for only one marker per turn, and AIRCRAFT and Fortifications never count. See [Objective markers](/rules/matched-play#objective-markers).

## Objective Secured

An ability, usually on Troops, that lets you control an objective marker even when your opponent has more models in range, unless they also have Objective Secured models there. A rule that removes Objective Secured beats one that grants it. See [Objective markers](/rules/matched-play#objective-markers).

## Primary and secondary objectives

How you score victory points in a mission. Primary objectives, usually about holding objective markers, give up to 45 VP. Each player picks 3 secondary objectives worth up to 15 VP each, and a painted army adds 10 VP, for a maximum of 100. See [Primary objectives](/rules/matched-play#primary-objectives) and [Secondary objectives](/rules/matched-play#secondary-objectives).

## Out-of-phase rules

Rules that let a unit act "as if it were" another phase, such as shooting in the Fight phase. They use that phase's normal rules, but you can't use that phase's Stratagems on them. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Rare Rules

A section of the core rules, expanded by FAQs, that settles unusual situations, such as fights-first and fights-last effects or mortal wounds mixed with normal damage. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Redeploying units

Some abilities let you move a unit again after deployment. Unless the ability says otherwise, it can only put the unit back on the battlefield, not into reserves. See [The mission sequence](/rules/matched-play#the-mission-sequence).

## Damage table

A table on the datasheet of a large model whose characteristics, marked with an asterisk on its profile, get worse as it loses wounds. See [The basics](/rules/basics).

## Explodes

A common vehicle ability. When the model is destroyed, roll a dice, and on a 6 nearby units suffer mortal wounds; the datasheet gives the distance and amount. Units embarked inside it aren't affected by the explosion, but they roll for the destroyed transport instead. See [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase).

## Crusade

9th edition's narrative campaign system. Your army has an Order of Battle and gains experience, Battle Honours and Battle Scars from game to game. It uses Power Level, not points. See [Ways to play](/rules/matched-play#ways-to-play).

## Boarding Actions

A 500-point close-quarters variant from 2023, fought on special Boarding Actions terrain. Each player starts with 1 CP, and only the Command Re-roll, Counter-offensive and Insane Bravery core Stratagems can be used. See [Ways to play](/rules/matched-play#ways-to-play).

## Deep Strike

A name for abilities that let a unit start off the battlefield and arrive later, set up anywhere more than `9"` from enemy models. The exact rule is on each datasheet. In matched play these units can't arrive in battle round 1 and must arrive by the end of battle round 3. See [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase) and [Reserve limits](/rules/matched-play#reserve-limits).

## Smoke Launchers

A common vehicle ability, usually once per battle, that makes the vehicle harder to hit until your next Shooting phase. The details are on each datasheet. See [Shooting phase](/rules/shooting-phase).

## Inspiring Leader

The generic Warlord Trait any Warlord can take: friendly units within `6"` of your Warlord add `+1` to their Leadership. See [The Warlord](/rules/army-building#the-warlord) and [Morale phase](/rules/morale-phase).
