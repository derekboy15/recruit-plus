Before a matched play game, you pick your units, organise them into one or more detachments, choose a Warlord and write it all down on an army roster. This article covers the rules that make that army legal.

## Battle-forged armies

In matched play your army must be **Battle-forged**. That means every unit in it belongs to a **detachment**: an organisation chart with slots for each Battlefield Role.

A Battle-forged army:
- starts the game with a pool of Command Points (CP) set by the battle size;
- gains the **Battle-forged CP bonus** of `+1` CP in the Command phase (see [Detachments and Command Points](#detachments-and-command-points));
- may use Strategic Reserves;
- must have an **Army Faction**: every unit, except UNALIGNED units, shares at least one Faction keyword, such as IMPERIUM or CHAOS.

An army that isn't organised into detachments is **Unbound**. It gets no starting CP and can't use Strategic Reserves. Unbound armies aren't allowed in matched play.

Matched play armies are measured in **points**. Each unit's cost, and the cost of its wargear, comes from the Munitorum Field Manual. Open and narrative play usually use **Power Level** instead. The points limit for each battle size is in [Battle sizes](/rules/matched-play#battle-sizes).

## Which rules your army uses

The army-building rules depend on the battle size you play.

| Battle size | Points | Army-building rules |
|---|---|---|
| Combat Patrol | up to 500 | Core Book: exactly one Patrol Detachment |
| Incursion | up to 1,000 | Arks of Omen GT: one Arks of Omen Detachment, plus one optional allied detachment |
| Strike Force | up to 2,000 | Arks of Omen GT: one Arks of Omen Detachment, plus one optional allied detachment |
| Onslaught | up to 3,000 | Core Book: up to 4 detachments from the Core Book table |

The Arks of Omen Grand Tournament pack (January 2023) was the last matched play pack of 9th edition. It only covers Incursion and Strike Force games. Combat Patrol and Onslaught games use the Core Book's Eternal War matched play rules, with the Core Book detachments.

In a Combat Patrol game, IMPERIAL KNIGHTS and CHAOS KNIGHTS armies use one Super-heavy Detachment instead of a Patrol.

> **Tip:** Older articles and videos often show armies built from two or three Battalions or other Core Book detachments at 2,000 points. Those rules were replaced at Incursion and Strike Force by the Arks of Omen Detachment.

## Battlefield roles

Every datasheet has exactly one **Battlefield Role**. The role decides which detachment slot the unit fills.

| Role | What it usually covers |
|---|---|
| HQ | Commanders and leaders, such as Captains, Warbosses and Overlords |
| Troops | Line infantry, such as Intercessors, Boyz and Guardsmen |
| Elites | Specialists, many characters, Dreadnoughts and Terminators |
| Fast Attack | Bikes, jump infantry and fast skimmers |
| Heavy Support | Tanks, artillery and heavy weapon squads |
| Flyers | AIRCRAFT |
| Dedicated Transport | A transport bought to carry one of your units, such as a Rhino |
| Lords of War | Super-heavy vehicles, Knights, Primarchs and titanic monsters |
| Fortifications | Buildings and defences bought as part of your army |

Troops usually gain **Objective Secured** from their codex's detachment rules. That makes them very good at holding objectives (see [Objective markers](/rules/matched-play#objective-markers)).

## Detachments and Command Points

Each detachment has a **Command Cost**, paid in CP, and a list of slots. Red, compulsory slots must be filled. Optional slots can't be exceeded.

When you build a Core Book army:
1. Start with the CP for your battle size: 3 at Combat Patrol, 18 at Onslaught.
2. Pay each detachment's Command Cost from that pool.
3. Add any **Command Benefit**: for example, a Patrol, Battalion or Brigade refunds its whole cost if it contains your Warlord.
4. Whatever is left is your starting CP for the battle, for Stratagems and Strategic Reserves.

For example, at Onslaught you start with 18 CP. A Battalion with your Warlord in it costs 3 CP and refunds 3 CP. Adding a Spearhead costs another 3 CP, so you begin the battle with 15 CP.

In an Arks of Omen game every detachment costs 0 CP and gives no Command Benefit. You start with 6 CP and spend them only on Stratagems, including the [Requisition stratagems](#requisition-stratagems).

General rules for every detachment:
- A unit can only belong to one detachment.
- A detachment can include at most one Dedicated Transport for each INFANTRY unit in it.
- **Detachment abilities**, such as Chapter Tactics or Objective Secured, only apply if every unit in the detachment shares the Faction keyword the ability needs. Auxiliary Support, Super-heavy Auxiliary and Fortification Network detachments never gain detachment abilities.
- Standard detachments need all their units to come from the same Faction, and can't include Understrength units.

**The Battle-forged CP bonus.** In Core Book games, you gain 1 CP at the start of your own Command phase. In Arks of Omen games, **both** players gain 1 CP at the start of **every** Command phase, yours and your opponent's. See [Command phase](/rules/command-phase).

## Core Book detachments

Use this table for Combat Patrol and Onslaught games. Combat Patrol allows only one detachment, a Patrol. Onslaught allows up to 4.

| Detachment | Command Cost | Command Benefit | Slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patrol | 2 CP | `+2` CP if it contains your Warlord | HQ 1–2, Troops 1–3, Elites 0–2, Fast Attack 0–2, Heavy Support 0–2, Flyers 0–2 |
| Battalion | 3 CP | `+3` CP if it contains your Warlord | HQ 2–3, Troops 3–6, Elites 0–6, Fast Attack 0–3, Heavy Support 0–3, Flyers 0–2 |
| Brigade | 4 CP | `+4` CP if it contains your Warlord | HQ 3–5, Troops 6–12, Elites 3–8, Fast Attack 3–5, Heavy Support 3–5, Flyers 0–2 |
| Vanguard | 3 CP | none | HQ 1–2, Troops 0–3, Elites 3–6, Fast Attack 0–2, Heavy Support 0–2, Flyers 0–2 |
| Spearhead | 3 CP | none | HQ 1–2, Troops 0–3, Elites 0–2, Fast Attack 0–2, Heavy Support 3–6, Flyers 0–2 |
| Outrider | 3 CP | none | HQ 1–2, Troops 0–3, Elites 0–2, Fast Attack 3–6, Heavy Support 0–2, Flyers 0–2 |
| Supreme Command | 0 CP | Pick one: `+4` CP if your army has a Brigade, `+3` if a Battalion, `+2` if a Patrol | Exactly 1 HQ or Lord of War that is a PRIMARCH, DAEMON PRIMARCH or SUPREME COMMANDER, and it must be your Warlord. Max one per army. |
| Super-heavy | 3 CP, or 6 CP with TITANIC units | none | Lords of War 3–5, all from the same Faction |
| Super-heavy Auxiliary | 3 CP | none | Exactly 1 Lord of War |
| Fortification Network | 1 CP | `+1` CP if every unit shares a Faction with your Warlord's detachment | Fortifications 1–3. Max one per army; your Warlord can't be in it. |
| Auxiliary Support | 2 CP | none | Exactly 1 unit of any role except Lords of War and Fortifications |

You can take the same detachment more than once, as long as you stay within the battle size's detachment limit.

## The Arks of Omen Detachment

In Incursion and Strike Force games, your army is built around **exactly one** Arks of Omen Detachment. You may add **one** allied detachment (see [Allied detachments](#allied-detachments)) and no other detachments.

The Arks of Omen Detachment costs 0 CP and has no Command Benefit.

**Compulsory slots.** You must include:
- 1 HQ unit (IMPERIAL KNIGHTS and CHAOS KNIGHTS detachments don't need one); and
- one block of 3 units from a single role you choose: 3 Troops, 3 Elites, 3 Fast Attack, 3 Heavy Support or 3 Lords of War.

These compulsory units are **in addition to** the optional slots below.

| Role | Optional slots |
|---|---|
| HQ | 0–3 |
| Troops | 0–9 |
| Elites | 0–3 |
| Elites (CHARACTER units only) | 0–3 more |
| Fast Attack | 0–3 |
| Heavy Support | 0–3 |
| Lords of War | 0–3 |
| Flyers | 0–2 |
| Fortifications | 0–3 |
| Dedicated Transports | 1 per INFANTRY unit |

The Aircraft cap still applies: at Incursion you can include only 1 AIRCRAFT model, even though the detachment has 2 Flyers slots (see [Aircraft](/rules/matched-play#aircraft)).

So if you pick Elites as your block of 3, you can take up to 3 compulsory Elites, 3 optional Elites and 3 more Elites that are CHARACTERS.

**Restrictions:**
- Every unit must share a Faction keyword other than IMPERIUM, CHAOS, AELDARI, YNNARI or TYRANIDS.
- No Understrength units.
- Your whole army can include only one SUPREME COMMANDER unit, and it must be in the Arks of Omen Detachment.

**Named exceptions.** Some leaders can join a detachment of a different sub-faction without breaking the other units' detachment rules: Abaddon with any Legion, the Silent King with any Dynasty, Commander Shadowsun with any Sept, Belisarius Cawl with any Forge World, and a Space Marine Primarch with any Chapter.

## Allied detachments

In an Arks of Omen game you may add **one** allied detachment. It costs 0 CP and gives no Command Benefit. It must be a Patrol or Super-heavy Auxiliary (or an Auxiliary Support, where the list below says so), and only these pairings, called **Battle Brothers**, are allowed:

| Your Arks of Omen Detachment | Allowed allied detachment |
|---|---|
| Any IMPERIUM faction | One Agents of the Imperium Auxiliary Support or Patrol; or one Imperial Knights Super-heavy Auxiliary (a single Freeblade); or one Leagues of Votann Patrol |
| Asuryani or Drukhari | A Harlequins Patrol |
| Astra Militarum | A Patrol made only of Militarum Tempestus units |
| Genestealer Cults | A Brood Brothers Patrol, Auxiliary Support or Super-heavy Auxiliary |
| Any CHAOS faction | A Chaos Knights Super-heavy Auxiliary (a single Dreadblade) |
| World Eaters, Thousand Sons, Death Guard or Emperor's Children | A Legiones Daemonica Patrol of the matching god: Khorne, Tzeentch, Nurgle or Slaanesh |
| Other Chaos Space Marine Legions | Any Legiones Daemonica Patrol |
| Disciples of Be'lakor (Chaos Daemons Army of Renown) | A Traitoris Astartes Patrol **and** a House Korvax Chaos Knights Super-heavy Auxiliary |
| A Drukhari Kabal, Wych Cult or Coven | A Drukhari Patrol of a different type |

Disciples of Be'lakor is the only case where you may take two allied detachments.

In Combat Patrol you can't take allies, because your army is a single Patrol. In Onslaught, allies are simply extra Core Book detachments that share your Army Faction, such as IMPERIUM.

## Agents of the Imperium

AGENT OF THE IMPERIUM units, such as Inquisitors and Officio Assassinorum assassins, have their own way into an army. Under the final rules (April 2023), a Battle-forged army can include one AGENT OF THE IMPERIUM unit in each IMPERIUM Patrol, Boarding Patrol, Battalion, Brigade or Arks of Omen Detachment, without it using a slot. This doesn't work for FALLEN detachments.

An Agent included this way:
- doesn't stop the rest of the detachment gaining its detachment abilities;
- doesn't break rules that need every unit in your army to share a keyword, such as Combat Doctrines;
- is ignored when checking shared Faction keywords and your Army Faction.

Agents can never join a Brood Brothers detachment. In an Arks of Omen game you can also bring Agents as your allied detachment (see [Allied detachments](#allied-detachments)).

## The Warlord

When you build your army, pick one model to be your **Warlord**. It gains the WARLORD keyword.
- If your army has any CHARACTER models, your Warlord must be a CHARACTER.
- A Fortification can't be your Warlord.

A CHARACTER Warlord can have one **Warlord Trait**. Named characters usually have fixed traits. Any Warlord can use the generic **Inspiring Leader** trait: friendly units within `6"` of it add `+1` to their Leadership.

How you get the trait depends on the rules your game uses:
- **Core Book games (Combat Patrol, Onslaught):** your CHARACTER Warlord gets one Warlord Trait for free.
- **Arks of Omen games (Incursion, Strike Force):** your Warlord only gets a trait if you pay 1 CP for the Warlord Trait Requisition stratagem.

In Core Book games, where your Warlord is matters: putting it in a Patrol, Battalion or Brigade refunds that detachment's cost.

## Requisition stratagems

Many Stratagems are used **before the battle**, while you muster your army (these are *pre-battle* Stratagems). They upgrade units, hand out extra Relics or Warlord Traits, or set units up off the battlefield. You record every one of them on your army roster. **Requisition stratagems** are a separate group of 3 core pre-battle Stratagems from the Arks of Omen Grand Tournament pack: Warlord Trait, Relic and Heroic Support (see [Core stratagems](/rules/stratagems)).

CP spent before the battle can **never be refunded**. Rules that refund CP only work on Stratagems used during a phase.

In Arks of Omen games, these three Requisition stratagems are available to every army. Each can be used once per army.

| Stratagem | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Warlord Trait | 1 CP | Your CHARACTER Warlord gets a Warlord Trait it could have. A named character with several listed traits gets all of them. |
| Relic | 1 CP | One CHARACTER that isn't a named character gets one Relic. |
| Heroic Support | 1 CP | If a detachment rule limits a unit to 1 per detachment, you can take up to 2 instead. Genestealer Cults' Gene-sect rule is excluded. |

Codex Stratagems that give **extra** Relics or Warlord Traits still work alongside these. For how Stratagems work in general, see [How stratagems work](/rules/core-stratagems).

## Rule of Two and Rule of Three

You can't include the same datasheet too many times:

| Battle size | Max copies of one datasheet |
|---|---|
| Combat Patrol | 3 |
| Incursion | 2 (the Rule of Two) |
| Strike Force | 3 (the Rule of Three) |
| Onslaught | 3 |

These units don't count towards the limit:
- Troops units;
- Dedicated Transports;
- units added during the battle with Reinforcement points.

All Daemon Prince datasheets count as one datasheet for this rule.

> **Tip:** In Arks of Omen games, a Dedicated Transport that starts the battle empty can't be deployed and counts as destroyed (see [Other matched play limits](#other-matched-play-limits)). This stops players using empty transports to get around the Rule of Three.

## Other matched play limits

These rules apply in every matched play game:
- **No Understrength units.** You can't field a unit smaller than its minimum size.
- **Shared Faction in each detachment.** Every unit in a detachment must share a Faction keyword that isn't IMPERIUM, CHAOS, AELDARI, YNNARI or TYRANIDS. Fortification Networks are exempt.

These extra rules apply in Arks of Omen games (Incursion and Strike Force):
- **One sub-faction choice** (from War Zone Nachmund onward, including Arks of Omen). Whenever you choose a keyword in angle brackets, such as `<CHAPTER>` or `<HIVE FLEET>`, you must make the same choice across your whole army. Exceptions include `<MARK OF CHAOS>`, `<ALLEGIANCE>`, Freeblades, Dreadblades and Ork Specialist Mobs.
- **No Specialist Detachments** (from War Zone Nachmund onward, including Arks of Omen).
- **Dedicated Transports start full.** Each Dedicated Transport must start the battle with at least one unit embarked. If it doesn't, it can't be deployed and counts as destroyed.
- **Strategic Reserves are free.** You don't pay CP to put units into Strategic Reserves.

**Fortifications** have special rules in every game:
- They can't be set up within `3"` of other terrain, except hills. If there is no room, the Fortification isn't deployed and counts as destroyed.
- In GT missions they are deployed before your other units. To make room, you may remove one Obstacle or Area Terrain piece from your own deployment zone.
- They never go into Strategic Reserves, never control objectives and never perform Actions.

The Aircraft limits for each battle size are in [Aircraft](/rules/matched-play#aircraft).

## Your army roster

Before the game you write an **army roster** and share it with your opponent. You can't change it to suit your opponent after that. It lists:
- your name, army name and Army Faction;
- each detachment and which units are in it;
- each unit's Battlefield Role, model count, wargear and points;
- any keywords you chose, such as `<CHAPTER>`;
- the psychic powers your PSYKERS know and other pre-selected abilities;
- Warlord Traits, Relics and pre-battle Stratagem upgrades;
- which model is your Warlord;
- your total points, including any Reinforcement points;
- your starting CP after paying for detachments.

**Reinforcement points** are points you keep back to pay for units added during the battle, such as summoned units. Units added this way are never part of a detachment, so they never gain detachment abilities.
