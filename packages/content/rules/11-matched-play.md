Matched play is the balanced, competitive way to play: both armies are the same size in points, and you win by scoring victory points (VP) from objectives. This article covers how a matched play game is set up, scored and won.

## Ways to play

9th edition has three styles of play:
- **Open play** has almost no restrictions. Armies don't have to be equal in size.
- **Narrative play** tells a story. Its main form is **Crusade**, a campaign system where your army gains experience, Battle Honours and Battle Scars from game to game. Crusade uses Power Level instead of points.
- **Matched play** is balanced play: Battle-forged armies, equal points limits, and missions scored out of 100 VP.

The rest of this article is about matched play. **Boarding Actions** is a separate 500-point variant from 2023, fought on special close-quarters terrain; it isn't covered here.

To build an army for matched play, see [Building a legal army](/rules/army-building).

## Battle sizes

Before anything else, agree a battle size with your opponent. It sets the points limit, your starting Command Points (CP) and the table size.

| Battle size | Points limit (each side) | Starting CP (Core Book) | Starting CP (Arks of Omen GT) | Minimum battlefield | Game length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Combat Patrol | 500 | 3 | not covered | 44" x 30" | up to 1 hour |
| Incursion | 1,000 | 6 | 6 | 44" x 30" | up to 2 hours |
| Strike Force | 2,000 | 12 | 6 | 44" x 60" | up to 3 hours |
| Onslaught | 3,000 | 18 | not covered | 44" x 90" | up to 4 hours |

Which column you use depends on the mission pack:
- **Combat Patrol and Onslaught** games use the Core Book's Eternal War missions. You start with 3 CP (Combat Patrol) or 18 CP (Onslaught), pay for detachments from that pool, and gain 1 CP at the start of each of your own Command phases.
- **Incursion and Strike Force** games use the Arks of Omen Grand Tournament pack. Each player starts with 6 CP at either size. Detachments cost nothing, and **both** players gain 1 CP at the start of **every** Command phase.

Strike Force (2,000 points) was the standard tournament size for the whole edition. Incursion (1,000 points) was the second Grand Tournament size.

> **Tip:** You'll often see 6 CP for Incursion and 12 CP for Strike Force. Those are the Core Book values, which the Grand Tournament packs used until mid-2022. The final pack, Arks of Omen, uses 6 CP at both sizes.

## Mission packs

A **mission pack** gives you the missions, the scoring rules and any extra army restrictions.

- **Eternal War** is the matched play pack in the Core Book (July 2020). It has missions for all four battle sizes, chosen by agreement or a `D6` roll. Its Combat Patrol and Onslaught missions were the only official matched play missions at those sizes for the whole edition.
- The **Grand Tournament (GT) packs** replaced Eternal War for organised play. They only cover Incursion and Strike Force, with nine missions at each size. You pick a mission by agreement or roll a `D33`: roll two `D3`s and read the first as tens and the second as units, giving 11 to 33.

There were five GT packs: GT 2020, GT 2021, War Zone Nachmund (early 2022), War Zone Nephilim (mid-2022) and **Arks of Omen** (January 2023). Each one replaced the last. Arks of Omen was the final 9th edition pack, so its rules are the ones to use for Incursion and Strike Force.

## The mission sequence

Every matched play game is set up in the same order. These are the Core Book's 16 steps:

1. **Select battle size.**
2. **Muster armies.** Build your Battle-forged army, write your roster and use any Stratagems that upgrade units.
3. **Determine the mission.**
4. **Read the mission briefing:** its primary objective, any mission rules, and (in Eternal War) its mission secondary.
5. **Place objective markers** as the mission map shows.
6. **Create the battlefield.** Set up terrain, but never on top of an objective marker.
7. **Select secondary objectives.** Each player secretly picks 3 (see [Secondary objectives](#secondary-objectives)).
8. **Determine Attacker and Defender.** Roll off; the winner chooses.
9. **Choose deployment zone.** The Defender chooses.
10. **Declare reserves and transports.** Note which units start in reserve and which start embarked (see [Reserve limits](#reserve-limits)).
11. **Deploy armies.** Take turns setting up one unit at a time, starting with the Defender. A model too big to fit in your deployment zone is set up touching your battlefield edge and can't act in battle round 1.
12. **Determine first turn.**
13. **Resolve pre-battle abilities.** Take turns, starting with the player who goes first.
14. **Begin the battle.**
15. **End the battle** after 5 battle rounds.
16. **Determine the victor.**

The GT packs change a few steps:
- In step 12 you roll off, and the **winner takes the first turn**. In the Core Book version, the winner chooses who goes first.
- You choose secondary objectives **after** deployment zones have been chosen.
- Fortifications are deployed before any other units.

## Reserve limits

Some units start the battle off the table, in Strategic Reserves or as other reinforcements such as deep strikers. Matched play limits this:
- No more than half of your units, and less than half of your points, can start off the table.
- Reserve units can't arrive in battle round 1.
- Any reserve unit that hasn't arrived by the end of battle round 3 counts as destroyed. This doesn't apply to units that went into reserve after the first battle round started.

These limits come from the Core Book's Eternal War rules, and every GT pack repeats them. How Strategic Reserves arrive is covered in [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase).

In Core Book games, putting units into Strategic Reserves costs CP based on their Power Rating. In Arks of Omen games it costs nothing.

## Objective markers

Most missions are about controlling **objective markers**: 40mm round markers placed as the mission map shows.

- A model is **in range** of a marker if it is within `3"` horizontally and `5"` vertically of it.
- You **control** a marker if you have more models in range of it than your opponent.
- Each model only counts towards one marker per turn.
- AIRCRAFT and Fortifications never count towards controlling a marker.

**Objective Secured.** Some units, usually Troops, have Objective Secured. You control a marker if any of your Objective Secured models are in range of it, even if your opponent has more models there. If both players have Objective Secured models in range, the normal model count decides. A rule that removes Objective Secured always beats a rule that grants it.

Some missions and codex rules make objectives **sticky**: you keep control of the marker after your models leave it, until your opponent takes it.

## Primary objectives

Every mission has a **primary objective**, worth up to 45 VP over the game. Primaries are about holding objective markers.

**Eternal War missions (Combat Patrol and Onslaught).** You score at the end of your Command phase, from battle round 2 onwards. Each condition you meet is worth 5 VP, up to 15 VP per turn.

| Primary | Conditions (5 VP each) |
|---|---|
| Take and Hold | You hold 1 or more objectives; you hold 2 or more; you hold more than your opponent |
| Domination | You hold 2 or more objectives; you hold 3 or more; you hold more than your opponent |
| Unified Advance | You hold 1 or more objectives; you hold at least one "A" and one "C" objective; you hold at least one each of "A", "B" and "C" |

**Arks of Omen missions (Incursion and Strike Force).** Each mission has one primary objective with two parts:
- A **hold** part, using the Take and Hold or Domination conditions above but worth 4 VP each, up to 12 VP per turn. In battle rounds 2 to 4 you score it at the end of your Command phase. In battle round 5 the player who went first scores in their Command phase, and the player going second scores at the **end of their turn**.
- A **mission-specific** part, such as destroying units, completing an action or holding a particular objective. Your mission tells you how and when it scores.

The two parts together can't give you more than 45 VP.

> **Tip:** Because Eternal War scores at the start of your turn, going first is a real advantage there. The later GT packs let the second player score at the end of their last turn to even this out.

## Secondary objectives

Before the battle, each player secretly picks **3 secondary objectives**, with **no more than one from each category**. Each one can score up to 15 VP, so secondaries are worth up to 45 VP in total. VP over the 15 cap is lost.

There are five categories:

| Category | What it rewards |
|---|---|
| Purge the Enemy | Destroying particular kinds of enemy units, such as characters or big vehicles and monsters |
| No Mercy, No Respite | Destroying lots of enemy units or models overall |
| Battlefield Supremacy | Getting your units into table quarters or the enemy deployment zone |
| Shadow Operations | Completing **actions** with your units |
| Warpcraft | Completing **psychic actions** with your PSYKERS |

A secondary is either **Progressive** (scored during the game, at the times it says) or **End Game** (scored once, when the battle ends).

**Core Book secondaries (Combat Patrol and Onslaught).** The Eternal War pack has 17 universal secondaries that any army can pick. Each Eternal War mission also has its own mission secondary, which can replace one of your category picks.

| Category | Core Book secondaries |
|---|---|
| Purge the Enemy | Assassinate; Bring It Down; Titan Slayers; Slay the Warlord |
| No Mercy, No Respite | Thin Their Ranks; Attrition; While We Stand, We Fight; First Strike |
| Battlefield Supremacy | Engage on All Fronts; Linebreaker; Domination |
| Shadow Operations | Investigate Sites; Repair Teleport Homer; Raise the Banners High |
| Warpcraft | Mental Interrogation; Psychic Ritual; Abhor the Witch |

**Arks of Omen secondaries (Incursion and Strike Force).** The Arks of Omen pack has 11 universal secondaries:

| Category | Arks of Omen secondaries |
|---|---|
| Purge the Enemy | Assassination; Bring It Down; Abhor the Witch |
| No Mercy, No Respite | No Prisoners; Grind Them Down |
| Battlefield Supremacy | Engage on All Fronts; Behind Enemy Lines |
| Shadow Operations | Raise the Banners High; Retrieve Battlefield Data |
| Warpcraft | Psychic Interrogation; Warp Ritual |

In Arks of Omen games:
- **Faction secondaries** for every army are printed in the pack, and you may pick up to 3 of them, still only one per category. Which faction's set you can use depends on your Arks of Omen Detachment. Secondaries printed in codexes can't be used.
- If you don't choose, you get the **default secondaries**: Assassination, Grind Them Down and Behind Enemy Lines.
- Assassination, Grind Them Down and Behind Enemy Lines can also give you `+1` CP when your Troops (or Armiger-class or War Dog-class Knights) do the work.
- You can't pick **Abhor the Witch** if your army includes any PSYKER.

Each secondary's exact scoring is in the mission pack. As a rough guide, scoring about 10 VP from a secondary is a good result, and 15 is rare.

## Scoring and winning

A matched play game is scored out of 100 VP:

| Source | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Primary objectives | 45 VP |
| Secondary objectives (3 x 15) | 45 VP |
| Battle Ready bonus for a fully painted army | 10 VP |
| **Total** | **100 VP** |

After 5 battle rounds, the player with the most VP wins. If you are tied, the game is a draw.

**Conceding.** You and your opponent can agree to stop early and count up your VP. If one player concedes alone, they remove their army and their opponent wins.

## Aircraft

In matched play you can only include a limited number of AIRCRAFT. The limit counts **models**, not units:

| Battle size | Max AIRCRAFT models |
|---|---|
| Combat Patrol | 1 |
| Incursion | 1 |
| Strike Force | 2 |
| Onslaught | 3 |

These caps aren't in the Core Book. They came from the first Balance Dataslate (9 Nov 2021) and stayed in force to the end of 9th edition.

The Arks of Omen dataslate (January 2023) added two more rules:
- All your AIRCRAFT units must **start the battle in Strategic Reserves**. In Arks of Omen games this costs no CP.
- Abilities that trigger when an Aircraft **moves over** enemy units, such as bombing runs, only work if the Aircraft is still on the battlefield after its move.

> **Not confirmed:** One library source says that if your Aircraft make up more than half of your army's points, no other units can start in reserve. This couldn't be checked against another source.

How Aircraft move is covered in [Movement phase](/rules/movement-phase).

## Universal Matched Play Rules

Some rules apply to every matched play game but aren't in the Core Book. Games Workshop added them later in Balance Dataslates. Together with the Core Book's own matched play limits, these are the rules every matched play army follows:

| Rule | What it does | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Aircraft limits | 1, 2 or 3 AIRCRAFT models by battle size (see [Aircraft](#aircraft)) | First Balance Dataslate, 9 Nov 2021 |
| Aircraft start in reserve | All AIRCRAFT start in Strategic Reserves | Arks of Omen dataslate, January 2023 |
| Indirect Fire | When you shoot a target you can't see, the firing model's Ballistic Skill is worsened by 1 (`-1`), and the target gets `+1` to its armour save (see [Indirect Fire](/rules/shooting-phase#indirect-fire)) | Balance Dataslate, 14 Apr 2022; Astra Militarum lost their exemption in January 2023 |
| Bodyguard abilities | Rules that protect a character work through Look Out, Sir instead of making it impossible to target (see [Look Out, Sir](/rules/shooting-phase#look-out-sir)) | Balance Dataslate, 14 Apr 2022 |
| Rule of Two and Rule of Three | Limits on copies of one datasheet (see [Rule of Two and Rule of Three](/rules/army-building#rule-of-two-and-rule-of-three)) | Core Book Eternal War (Rule of Three); GT 2021 pack (Rule of Two at Incursion) |
| Reserve limits | Half your units, under half your points, no arrivals in battle round 1 (see [Reserve limits](#reserve-limits)) | Core Book Eternal War |

> **Tip:** Older guides from before 2022 don't mention the Indirect Fire penalty or the Aircraft rules. The rules above are the final versions, as they stood at the last Balance Dataslate (13 Apr 2023).
