**Stratagems** are special rules you pay for with Command Points (CP). Each one says when you can use it and what it does.

## Using a Stratagem

Spend the CP and resolve its effect. You can't use a Stratagem you can't afford. See [using a stratagem](/rules/core-stratagems#using-a-stratagem).

You can use each Stratagem only once per phase. That is why only one of your units can usually fire Overwatch in each of your opponent's Charge phases. See [once per phase](/rules/core-stratagems#once-per-phase).

## The core Stratagems

Every army can use these:

| Stratagem | CP | When |
|---|---|---|
| Command Re-roll | 1 | After a roll such as a hit, wound, save or charge roll |
| Counter-offensive | 2 | After an enemy unit has fought |
| Insane Bravery | 2 | Before a Morale test (once per battle) |
| Fire Overwatch | 1 | After an enemy unit declares a charge against your unit |
| Cut Them Down | 1 | When an enemy unit Falls Back |
| Desperate Breakout | 2 | Your Movement phase, for a trapped unit |
| Emergency Disembarkation | 1 | When your TRANSPORT is destroyed |

Each army has many more Stratagems of its own. See [the core Stratagems](/rules/core-stratagems#the-core-stratagems).

## Command Re-roll

Command Re-roll lets you roll a die again. A charge roll is 2D6 rolled together, so you must re-roll both dice: you can't keep one. See [charging](/rules/charge-phase#charging).

## Your drill

A 9" charge needs 8": a hard roll. If it falls short, spend 1 CP on Command Re-roll.
