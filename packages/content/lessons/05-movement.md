In your Movement phase you pick your units one at a time. Each one does exactly one of four things. See [the Move Units step](/rules/movement-phase#the-move-units-step).

## The four choices

- **Normal Move:** each model moves up to its Move (M). It can't move within Engagement Range of an enemy model.
- **Advance:** roll a D6 and add it to M for this move. For the rest of the turn the unit can't charge, and it can only shoot Assault weapons, at -1 to hit. See [Advance](/rules/movement-phase#advance).
- **Fall Back:** only for a unit that starts the phase in combat. Each model moves up to M and must end out of Engagement Range. For the rest of the turn the unit can’t shoot or charge, unless it is TITANIC. See [Fall Back](/rules/movement-phase#fall-back).
- **Remain Stationary:** don't move. Any unit you don't pick stays where it is.

A unit within Engagement Range of an enemy unit can only Fall Back or Remain Stationary.

## Engagement Range

A model is within **Engagement Range** of an enemy model when it is within 1" of it (and 5" vertically). That is how the game decides who is in combat. See [Engagement Range](/rules/basics#engagement-range).

## Unit coherency

A unit must end every move together. Every model must be within 2" of at least one other model from its unit. A unit of 6 or more models needs every model within 2" of at least two others. If a move would break coherency, the unit can't make it. See [unit coherency](/rules/basics#unit-coherency).

## Your drill

Advance one squad and see how far the dice take it. Then pull your other squad out of combat with a Fall Back.
