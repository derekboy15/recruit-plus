In your Shooting phase you pick your units one at a time. Each one picks targets for all of its guns, then rolls the attacks you learned in lesson 3.

## Who can be a target

A target must be **in range** of the weapon and **visible** to the firing model: if you can draw a line from any part of the firing model to any part of a model in the target unit, it can see it. See [choosing targets](/rules/shooting-phase#choosing-targets).

Terrain can block the view. The **Obscuring** trait (ruins, for example) means models can't see through or over the feature if it is at least 5" tall. See [visibility](/rules/basics#visibility).

You can't shoot at an enemy unit that is within Engagement Range of one of your units, except with Pistols and the Big Guns Never Tire rule for VEHICLE and MONSTER models.

## Cover

INFANTRY in Area Terrain such as ruins, woods or craters get its cover. **Light Cover** adds +1 to their armour saves against ranged attacks. It never improves an invulnerable save. See [Light Cover](/rules/terrain#light-cover).

## Blast

Blast weapons fire more shots into big units. Against a unit of 6 to 10 models, a Blast weapon always makes at least 3 attacks. Against 11 or more models, it makes the most it can: 6 for a D6. See [Blast](/rules/shooting-phase#blast).

## Look Out, Sir

A CHARACTER with 9 or fewer wounds can't be shot while it is within 3" of a friendly unit of 3 or more models that has no CHARACTERS, or of a friendly VEHICLE or MONSTER unit (with no CHARACTERS, or with 10 or more wounds). It can still be shot if it is the closest visible enemy unit to the firing model. See [Look Out, Sir](/rules/shooting-phase#look-out-sir).

## Your drill

Three Necron units: Warriors in a crater, more Warriors behind ruins, and an Overlord. Work out what you can shoot.
