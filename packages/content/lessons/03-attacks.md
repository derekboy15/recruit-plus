Shooting and fighting use the same steps for every attack. Learn them once and you can resolve any attack in the game.

## The four rolls

1. **Hit roll.** Roll a D6 for each attack. It hits on the firing model's Ballistic Skill (BS) or more, or its Weapon Skill (WS) in melee.
2. **Wound roll.** Roll a D6 for each hit. The score you need comes from the wound table below.
3. **Saving throw.** Your opponent picks a model in the target unit and rolls a D6 for each wound. The weapon's AP makes it harder: AP -1 takes 1 off the roll. If the result is equal to or more than the model's Save (Sv), the wound is stopped.
4. **Damage.** Each failed save makes the model lose wounds equal to the weapon's Damage (D). A model at 0 wounds is destroyed. Extra damage is lost.

An unmodified 6 always hits and always wounds. An unmodified 1 always fails, for hits, wounds and saves alike. See [making attacks](/rules/shooting-phase#making-attacks).

## The wound table

Compare the weapon's Strength (S) with the target's Toughness (T):

| Strength compared to Toughness | Roll needed |
|---|---|
| S is double T or more | 2+ |
| S is higher than T | 3+ |
| S equals T | 4+ |
| S is lower than T | 5+ |
| S is half T or less | 6+ |

So a bolt rifle (S 4) against a Necron Warrior (T 4) wounds on 4+.

## Modifiers

Rules can add to or take away from a roll. A hit roll or wound roll can never be changed by more than +1 or -1 in total, however many modifiers apply. Saves have no such limit. See [re-rolls and modifiers](/rules/basics#re-rolls-and-modifiers).

## Your drill

Your 5 Intercessors are 12" from the Warriors, so Rapid Fire doubles their bolt rifles: 10 shots. Against the Warriors' 4+ save, AP -1 means they need 5+. Roll, then count your hits and wounds yourself.
