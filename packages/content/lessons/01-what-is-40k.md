Warhammer 40,000 is a battle between two armies of miniatures on a tabletop. You move your models, roll dice to attack, and try to hold objectives better than your opponent.

## The game

Your army is made of **units**: a squad of soldiers, a tank, a lone hero. Each unit has a **datasheet** that tells you how far it moves, how well it fights and how tough it is. See [units, models and datasheets](/rules/basics#units-models-and-datasheets).

The smallest game is a **Combat Patrol**: 500 points of models each, on a battlefield 44" by 30". Everything is measured in inches, and you can measure any distance at any time. See [battle sizes](/rules/matched-play#battle-sizes).

## Turns and phases

A game lasts **5 battle rounds**. In each battle round both players take one turn, and the same player always goes first.

Each turn has 7 phases, always in this order:

1. **Command phase:** gain Command Points.
2. **Movement phase:** move your units.
3. **Psychic phase:** your psykers use their powers.
4. **Shooting phase:** fire ranged weapons.
5. **Charge phase:** rush into close combat.
6. **Fight phase:** units in combat fight.
7. **Morale phase:** units that lost models test their nerve.

The next lessons take these one at a time. See [the battle round](/rules/basics#the-battle-round).

## Winning

You win by scoring more **victory points** (VP) than your opponent. Most VP come from **objective markers** placed on the battlefield.

A model is in range of a marker if it is within 3" of it. You **control** the marker if you have more models in range of it than your opponent. See [objective markers](/rules/matched-play#objective-markers).

## Your first drill

Your Intercessors start 7" from an objective marker, measured from the middle of the squad to the middle of the marker. An Intercessor has a Move of 6", so a Normal Move can take the squad close enough. Move them up and take the objective.
