Charging is how you get into close combat. You charge with your units one at a time.

## Declaring a charge

A unit can charge if it is within 12" of an enemy unit, didn't Advance or Fall Back this turn, and isn't already in Engagement Range of an enemy. Pick one or more enemy units within 12" as its targets. See [charging](/rules/charge-phase#charging).

## Overwatch

Before you roll, your opponent may spend 1 CP on the Fire Overwatch Stratagem. One unit you are charging shoots at your charging unit, but each of its attacks only hits on an unmodified 6. See [Overwatch](/rules/charge-phase#overwatch).

## The charge roll

Roll 2D6: that is how far, in inches, each model can move. The charge only works if the unit ends within Engagement Range (1") of every unit it declared as a target, in unit coherency, and not within Engagement Range of any other enemy unit. If it can't, the charge fails and the unit doesn't move.

So the distance you need is the gap to the enemy, minus 1". A unit that made a charge move fights first in the Fight phase.

## Heroic Interventions

After all your charges, your opponent's CHARACTERS can **Heroically Intervene**. A CHARACTER within 3" of an enemy unit (and not in Engagement Range) moves up to 3", ending closer to the closest enemy model. See [Heroic Interventions](/rules/charge-phase#heroic-interventions).

## Your drill

Your Intercessors are 6" from the Necron Warriors. Declare the charge, face Overwatch, then judge your roll.
