In your Psychic phase, your PSYKER units try to manifest psychic powers. If your army has no psykers, you skip the phase.

## The Psychic test

Pick a psyker and a power it knows, then roll 2D6. If the total is equal to or higher than the power's **warp charge**, the test is passed. See [manifesting a power](/rules/psychic-phase#manifesting-a-power).

Your opponent can then try to stop it with **Deny the Witch**: one of their psykers within 24" rolls 2D6, and must roll higher than your total. See [Deny the Witch](/rules/psychic-phase#deny-the-witch).

## Smite

Every psyker knows Smite. It has a warp charge of 5, plus 1 for each other Smite your army has already attempted this phase. It hits the closest visible enemy unit within 18", which suffers D3 mortal wounds, or D6 if your Psychic test was 11 or more. See [Smite](/rules/psychic-phase#smite).

A **mortal wound** does 1 damage with no wound roll and no saving throw, not even an invulnerable save.

## Perils of the Warp

If your Psychic test shows a double 1 or a double 6, your psyker suffers **Perils of the Warp**: D3 mortal wounds on itself. A double 6 still adds up to 12, so if the psyker survives, the power can still go off. See [Perils of the Warp](/rules/psychic-phase#perils-of-the-warp).

## Your drill

Your Librarian is 10" from the Necron Warriors. Attempt Smite and work out whether it goes off.
