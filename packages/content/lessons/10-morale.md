The Morale phase ends every turn, for both players' units. It checks whether units that took losses hold their nerve.

## Who tests

A unit takes a **Morale test** if one or more of its models were destroyed this turn. Losing wounds without losing a whole model doesn't count. Each unit tests at most once. See [which units test](/rules/morale-phase#which-units-test).

## The Morale test

Roll a D6 and add the number of the unit's models destroyed this turn. Compare the total with the highest Leadership (Ld) in the unit:

- **equal or lower:** passed, nothing happens;
- **higher:** failed, and one model flees (it is removed and counts as destroyed).

An unmodified roll of 1 always passes. See [Morale tests](/rules/morale-phase#morale-tests).

## Combat Attrition

After a failed test, roll a D6 for each model still in the unit. Each 1 means one more model flees. If the unit is below Half-strength (fewer than half the models it started with), subtract 1 from each roll, so 1s and 2s both flee. See [Combat Attrition](/rules/morale-phase#combat-attrition).

The Insane Bravery Stratagem (2 CP, once per battle) passes a Morale test automatically.

## Coherency checks

At the end of the phase, any unit that is out of unit coherency removes models until it is back in one group.

## Your drill

The Necrons destroyed 3 of your 10 Intercessors this turn. Work out which rolls fail, then take the test.
