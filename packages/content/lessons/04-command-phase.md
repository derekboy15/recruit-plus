The Command phase is the first phase of every turn. Nothing moves or attacks in it. It is short, but it matters.

## Command Points

**Command Points (CP)** are what you spend on Stratagems: special tricks such as re-rolling a dice roll. You start the battle with a pool of CP, set by the battle size. In a Combat Patrol game you start with 3 CP.

At the start of each of your Command phases, a Battle-forged army gains 1 CP. In a Core Book mission (Combat Patrol and Onslaught), only the player whose turn it is gains it. In the Arks of Omen missions (Incursion and Strike Force), both players gain 1 CP at the start of every Command phase, and each player starts with 6 CP. See [gaining Command Points](/rules/command-phase#gaining-command-points).

Rules can't give you more than 1 extra CP in a battle round, on top of these. See [the CP gain cap](/rules/command-phase#the-cp-gain-cap).

## Command-phase rules

Some abilities and mission rules happen in the Command phase. Use them after gaining your CP.

## Scoring

In most matched play missions, you score your **primary objective** at the end of your Command phase, starting in battle round 2. In the Core Book's Take and Hold, you score 5 VP for each of these, up to 15 VP a turn:

- you hold 1 or more objectives;
- you hold 2 or more objectives;
- you hold more objectives than your opponent.

You score before you move, so what counts is what you held at the start of your turn. See [scoring at the end of the phase](/rules/command-phase#scoring-at-the-end-of-the-phase).

## Your drill

It is your Command phase in battle round 2. Count your CP, then end the phase and see what you score.
