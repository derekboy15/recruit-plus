Every unit has a datasheet. It tells you everything the unit can do: how far it moves, how well it shoots and fights, and how hard it is to kill.

## The profile

Each model has a row of characteristics. Here is an Intercessor's:

| M | WS | BS | S | T | W | A | Ld | Sv |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6" | 3+ | 3+ | 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 7 | 3+ |

- **M** (Move): an Intercessor has a Move of 6".
- **WS** and **BS** (Weapon Skill and Ballistic Skill): the D6 roll needed to hit in melee and with guns. `3+` means a 3 or more.
- **S** and **T** (Strength and Toughness): compared to work out the roll needed to wound.
- **W** (Wounds): an Intercessor has 2 Wounds, so it takes 2 damage to destroy it.
- **A** (Attacks): how many melee attacks it makes.
- **Ld** (Leadership): used for Morale tests.
- **Sv** (Save): the roll needed to stop a wound. An Intercessor saves on `3+`; a Necron Warrior saves on `4+`.

See [units, models and datasheets](/rules/basics#units-models-and-datasheets) for the full list.

## Weapons

Each weapon has its own profile. The Intercessor's bolt rifle:

| Range | Type | S | AP | D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30" | Rapid Fire 1 | 4 | -1 | 1 |

- **Range**: how far it shoots.
- **Type**: Rapid Fire 1 means 1 shot, doubled when the target is within half range (15" for a bolt rifle).
- **S**: the Strength of each hit. **AP** (armour piercing) makes the target's save worse: AP -1 takes 1 off each save roll. **D** is the damage each failed save does.

The weapon types (Assault, Heavy, Rapid Fire, Grenade and Pistol) each have a rule of their own. See [weapon types](/rules/shooting-phase#weapon-types).

## Keywords and abilities

Below the profiles come the unit's **abilities** and **keywords**, such as INFANTRY or CHARACTER. Rules often say which keywords they affect.

## Your drill

Open each datasheet under the questions and find the answer. Then shoot the bolt rifles you just read about.
