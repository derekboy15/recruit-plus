In the Fight phase, units in combat fight. Both players' units fight in this phase, whoever's turn it is.

## Who fights when

1. **Units that charged this turn fight first.**
2. **Then the players take turns** picking one unit each, starting with the player whose turn it isn't, until every unit that can fight has fought.

A unit can fight if it is within Engagement Range of an enemy unit, or if it charged this turn. Each unit fights once. See [fight order](/rules/fight-phase#fight-order).

## Fighting with a unit

When you pick a unit, it does three things in order:

1. **Pile in:** each model may move up to 3". A model that moves must end closer to the closest enemy model. See [pile in](/rules/fight-phase#pile-in).
2. **Attack:** a model fights if it is within Engagement Range of an enemy unit, or within ½" of a friendly model that is within ½" of an enemy model. Each one makes its Attacks (A) characteristic in close combat attacks, rolled like shooting but hitting on its Weapon Skill (WS). See [which models fight](/rules/fight-phase#which-models-fight).
3. **Consolidate:** each model may move up to 3", again ending closer to the closest enemy model. See [consolidate](/rules/fight-phase#consolidate).

A model with no melee weapon fights with a close combat weapon: its own Strength, AP 0, Damage 1.

## Your drill

Your Intercessors have charged the Necron Warriors. Fight with them, then play the Necrons as they fight back.
