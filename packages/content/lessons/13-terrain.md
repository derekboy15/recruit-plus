Terrain changes what models can see, how well they are protected and how fast they move. Each piece has a **category** (who uses it) and **traits** (what it does). See [categories and traits](/rules/terrain#categories-and-traits).

## Who gets cover

INFANTRY, BEAST and SWARM models on or within an **Area Terrain** feature (ruins, woods, craters) get its cover. Near an **Obstacle** (a barricade or wall) they get cover within 3" of it, unless the attacker has a clear view of the whole model. Vehicles and monsters don't get cover this way.

## Cover traits

- **Light Cover:** +1 to armour saves against ranged attacks.
- **Heavy Cover:** +1 to armour saves against melee attacks, but not for a model that made a charge move this turn.
- **Dense Cover:** if the feature is at least 3" tall, ranged attacks through it take -1 to hit. This works for any target behind it, vehicles and monsters too (but not against AIRCRAFT, or a unit with a model of 18 or more wounds).

See [cover traits](/rules/terrain#cover-traits).

## Seeing and moving

- **Obscuring:** models can't see through or over the feature if it is at least 5" tall.
- **Difficult Ground:** a unit that makes a Normal Move, Advance or Fall Back over it subtracts 2" from its models' Move, and charging over it gives -2 to the charge roll.

See [movement traits](/rules/terrain#movement-traits).

## Your drill

Cross a crater with one squad, then shoot through a wood with the other.
