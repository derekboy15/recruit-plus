Games are won with **victory points** (VP), and most of them come from objective markers.

## Controlling an objective

A model is in range of a marker if it is within 3" horizontally and 5" vertically of it. You **control** a marker if you have more models in range of it than your opponent. Each model only counts towards one marker. AIRCRAFT never count. See [objective markers](/rules/matched-play#objective-markers).

**Objective Secured.** Some units, usually Troops, have Objective Secured. You control a marker if any of your Objective Secured models are in range of it, even if your opponent has more models there. If both players have Objective Secured models in range, the normal model count decides.

## Scoring the primary objective

Every mission has a primary objective. In a Core Book mission it is scored at the end of your Command phase, from battle round 2. Take and Hold gives 5 VP for each of these, up to 15 VP a turn:

- you hold 1 or more objectives;
- you hold 2 or more objectives;
- you hold more objectives than your opponent.

See [primary objectives](/rules/matched-play#primary-objectives). Missions also have secondary objectives that score in other ways.

## Your drill

Take a second objective, predict what you will score, then play on to your next Command phase and see.
