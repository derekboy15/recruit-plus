Before a game, each player builds an army up to the battle size's points limit: 500 points for a Combat Patrol. Every unit and its wargear has a points cost.

## Battle-forged

In matched play your army must be **Battle-forged**: every unit belongs to a **detachment**, an organisation chart with slots for each Battlefield Role (HQ, Troops, Elites, Fast Attack, Heavy Support, Flyers). A Battle-forged army gets Command Points. See [Battle-forged armies](/rules/army-building#battle-forged-armies).

## Detachments

Combat Patrol allows one detachment, a **Patrol**:

- HQ 1–2 and Troops 1–3, plus 0–2 Elites, 0–2 Fast Attack, 0–2 Heavy Support and 0–2 Flyers.
- It costs 2 CP, and you get 2 CP back if it contains your Warlord.

Bigger games use bigger detachments, such as the Battalion. See [Core Book detachments](/rules/army-building#core-book-detachments).

One of your CHARACTERS is your **Warlord**. See [the Warlord](/rules/army-building#the-warlord).

## The Rule of Three

You can include the same datasheet at most 3 times (the Rule of Two allows only 2 at Incursion). Troops units and Dedicated Transports don't count. See [the Rule of Three](/rules/army-building#rule-of-two-and-rule-of-three).
