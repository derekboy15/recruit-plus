Every matched play game is set up the same way. Combat Patrol uses the Core Book's Eternal War missions; Incursion and Strike Force use the Arks of Omen missions. See [mission packs](/rules/matched-play#mission-packs).

## Before deployment

1. Agree the battle size and build your armies.
2. Pick the mission and read its briefing.
3. Place the objective markers and set up terrain, as the mission map shows.
4. Choose secondary objectives.
5. Roll off: the winner chooses who is the **Attacker** and who is the **Defender**. The Defender chooses a deployment zone.

## Deployment

The players take turns setting up one unit at a time, starting with the Defender. Each unit must be set up wholly within your deployment zone. Some units can start in reserve instead: no more than half your units, and less than half your points. See [reserve limits](/rules/matched-play#reserve-limits).

## The first turn

Once both armies are set up, roll off for the first turn. In a Core Book mission the winner chooses who goes first; in an Arks of Omen mission the winner goes first. Then the battle begins, and lasts 5 battle rounds. See [the mission sequence](/rules/matched-play#the-mission-sequence).

## Your drill

Set up a real Combat Patrol mission, Incisive Attack, against the Necrons, then roll off for the first turn.
