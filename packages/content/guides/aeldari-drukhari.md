The Drukhari are raiders from the Dark City of Commorragh who strike fast and grow nastier as the battle goes on.

## How this army plays

A Drukhari army draws on 3 kinds of force. **Kabals** shoot, with poisoned splinter weapons and dark lances. **Wych Cults** are fast, fragile melee fighters boosted by Combat Drugs. **Haemonculus Covens** bring slower, tougher units such as Wracks, Grotesques and the Talos. Most infantry ride in Raiders and Venoms and jump out to strike.

**Strengths.** Speed, plenty of anti-tank weapons, and poison: poisoned weapons wound most non-VEHICLE targets on a fixed roll, so big monsters and elite infantry aren't safe. Power from Pain makes the whole army better every battle round.

**Weaknesses.** Kabalite Warriors and Wyches are Toughness 3 with 1 wound and light armour, and your transports don't take much punishment. Poison doesn't work against VEHICLE units, so you need lances and blasters for tanks. Lose your transports early and your infantry is stranded.

**What to expect.** A game of aggressive trades. Strike first from range or with a charge, keep moving, and save enough units to hold objectives in the later rounds, when Power from Pain is at its strongest.

> **Tip:** The Talos and Cronos lost the CORE keyword from February 2022 until the final April 2023 dataslate, so older articles may say CORE auras don't affect them. They now do.

## Army rules

**Power from Pain.** This only works if every unit in your army is DRUKHARI or UNALIGNED. Its effects build up with the battle round:

- **Round 1+:** a 6+ invulnerable save.
- **Round 2+:** the unit can charge in a turn it Advanced.
- **Round 3+:** +1 to hit with melee attacks; VEHICLE and MONSTER units also ignore the Heavy weapon penalty for shooting at enemies within Engagement Range.
- **Round 4+:** the invulnerable save improves to 5+.
- **Round 5+:** Morale tests are passed automatically, and units with damage tables count as having double their remaining wounds.

**Blade Artists.** An unmodified wound roll of 6 with a melee weapon improves the attack's AP by 1.

**Poisoned Weapon (X+).** Against any unit except VEHICLE or TITANIC units, an unmodified wound roll of X or more always wounds.

**Insensible to Pain.** Coven units roll a D6 each time a model would lose a wound; on a 5+ it isn't lost.

**Combat Drugs.** Before the battle, each unit with this ability picks 1 drug or rolls for 2 different ones at random, such as +1 Strength, +1 Toughness or +2" Move.

**Obsessions.** Your Kabal, Wych Cult or Haemonculus Coven gives its units an extra rule. For example, Kabal of the Black Heart treats the battle round as 1 higher for Power from Pain, and the Cult of Strife fights first when it starts the Fight phase in Engagement Range. A Realspace Raid detachment mixes all 3 kinds of force.

**Stratagems to know.** Lightning-fast Reactions (1 CP) makes attacks -1 to hit against an INFANTRY, VEHICLE or BIKER unit (not Coven units). Never Stationary (2 CP) gives a unit a Normal Move of up to 7" after shooting, but it can't charge that turn. Agents of Vect (0 CP, Black Heart only, once per battle) makes a Stratagem your opponent just used (other than Command Re-roll) cost 1 more CP for the rest of the battle.

## Key units

- [[Archon]] - the Kabal leader; its Overlord aura re-rolls hit rolls of 1 for nearby Kabal CORE and INCUBI units.
- [[Kabalite Warriors]] - cheap Troops with poisoned splinter rifles to fill Raiders and Venoms.
- [[Wyches]] - fast melee Troops with Combat Drugs, a 6+ invulnerable save and a 4+ invulnerable save against melee attacks.
- [[Incubi]] - elite swordsmen whose Tormentors ability can stop an enemy unit fighting until yours have.
- [[Raider]] - the main transport, carrying 11 DRUKHARI INFANTRY models.
- [[Venom]] - a small 6-model transport; ranged attacks against it are -1 to hit and it has a 5+ invulnerable save against them.
- [[Talos]] - a tough Coven monster that hits hard in melee.
- [[Hellions]] - fast skyboard raiders that can charge in a turn they Fell Back.
