## How this army plays

Ultramarines are the all-rounders of the Space Marines. They have no single trick that wins games. Instead you get flexible shooting, plenty of re-rolls and extra Command Points to spend on stratagems. If you want to learn how Space Marines work before picking a specialist chapter, start here.

Your Chapter Tactic lets units Fall Back out of combat and still shoot, so being tied up in melee hurts you less than most armies. Your super-doctrine rewards a steady advance: in Tactical Doctrine, units that make a Normal Move shoot as if they had stayed still, so Heavy weapons fire without a penalty and bolters keep their double shots.

Roboute Guilliman and Marneus Calgar are your centrepieces. Both give extra Command Points as Warlord and hand out hit re-rolls nearby. The catch is that a lot of your power sits in a few expensive models, so keep your key units close to them and expect to spend CP every turn.

The weakness is that you are good at everything but best at nothing. Melee chapters hit harder and gunline chapters shoot better from a standstill. You win by picking the right tool each turn.

## Army rules

### Shared Space Marine rules

Almost every datasheet has **Angels of Death**:

- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** ignore modifiers to Combat Attrition tests.
- **Bolter Discipline:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire double shots at targets within half range, when the firer is INFANTRY (not Centurions) whose unit stayed still, or when the firer is a TERMINATOR or BIKER.
- **Shock Assault:** +1 Attack in a turn your unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Combat Doctrines:** if every unit in your army is ADEPTUS ASTARTES, the active doctrine improves AP by 1: Devastator for Heavy and Grenade weapons, Tactical for Rapid Fire and Assault weapons, Assault for Pistols and melee. You start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move one step on, but you never have to.

Troops have Objective Secured, and an objective they control at the end of your Command phase stays yours until your opponent takes it.

### Ultramarines rules

- **Codex Discipline (Chapter Tactic):** +1 Leadership, and your units can shoot after Falling Back, at -1 to hit.
- **Scions of Guilliman (super-doctrine):** in Tactical Doctrine, a unit that made a Normal Move counts as having stayed still when it shoots.

Signature stratagems:

- **Fall Back and Re-engage:** a unit that Falls Back can shoot and charge, without the Codex Discipline hit penalty.
- **Sons of Guilliman:** an INFANTRY or BIKER unit re-rolls hit rolls of 1 when it shoots or fights; Troops can re-roll any hit roll.
- **Squad Doctrines:** one INFANTRY or BIKER unit picks its own doctrine until your next Movement phase.
- **Tactical Expertise:** once per battle, at the start of your Movement phase while the Tactical Doctrine is active: until the next battle round, unmodified wound rolls of 6 with Rapid Fire and Assault weapons get an extra 1 AP.

> **Tip:** The **Seal of Oath** relic is a strong pick: before the first turn you choose one enemy unit, and ULTRAMARINES CORE and CHARACTER units near the bearer re-roll hit and wound rolls against it all battle.

## Key units

- [[Roboute Guilliman]] - The Primarch: a huge fighter with a big re-roll aura who gives you 3 extra Command Points and can come back after being destroyed.
- [[Marneus Calgar]] - Very hard to kill because his armour halves damage, and he gives you 2 extra Command Points as Warlord.
- [[Victrix Honour Guard]] - Calgar's bodyguards, who keep him safe with Look Out, Sir.
- [[Captain Sicarius]] - Gives Objective Secured to a nearby CORE or CHARACTER unit each battle round.
- [[Chief Librarian Tigurius]] - A reliable psyker who makes one nearby unit harder to hit each battle round.
- [[Intercessor Squad]] - Solid Troops that make the most of Bolter Discipline, Sons of Guilliman and sticky objectives.
