Chaos Titan Legions are traitor war machines the size of buildings, known as God-Engines. This is not a full army: the catalogue holds just three Forge World Titans.

## How this army plays

Each Titan is one giant model. The smallest, the Warhound, costs 2,000 points, which is the size of a whole standard Strike Force game, and the Reaver and Warbringer Nemesis cost even more. So in practice Titans are for very large games, Apocalypse-style battles and narrative or open play, not normal matched play.

- **Strengths:** enormous wounds, a 2+ save, void shields that soak up shooting, and weapons that can delete almost any target.
- **Weaknesses:** everything you have is in one model. It can't hold objectives well, it can be tied up or swarmed, and once its shields are down it is a very big target.
- **For a new player:** this is not a starting army. Titans are best added to a Chaos force you already know, as the centrepiece of a big game.

The Titans have the CHAOS, TITANICUS TRAITORIS and <HERETIC TITAN LEGION> keywords. Each fills a Lord of War slot, and you join them to other Chaos armies through the shared CHAOS keyword.

## Army rules

The catalogue has **no army-wide rules and no Stratagems** for this faction. Everything comes from the datasheets, and all three Titans share these abilities:

- **Void Shields.** Each Titan has a number of void shields, each with 3 shield points: 2 on the Warhound, 4 on the Reaver and 6 on the Warbringer Nemesis. While it has any shields left, it has a 5+ invulnerable save against ranged attacks. When it fails a save against a ranged attack, the damage strips shield points instead of wounds. A shield that has started losing points keeps losing them until it collapses, and any damage left over from the attack that collapses it is lost. Mortal wounds ignore shields. At the start of your Command phase, a damaged shield that still has points is restored to 3.
- **Battle Titan.** The Titan can declare a charge in a turn it Fell Back. When it makes a Normal Move, Advances or Falls Back, it can walk over models other than MONSTERS and VEHICLES, but can't end its move within Engagement Range of them.
- **Reactor Meltdown.** When the Titan is destroyed, roll a D6. On a 6+ it explodes, and every unit within 3D6" suffers 2D6 mortal wounds.
- **Damage table.** Like other huge vehicles, each Titan gets slower and less accurate as it loses wounds.

The Warhound also has **Flank Speed**: when it Advances, it adds a flat 12" to its Move instead of rolling.

> **Tip:** some 9th Edition sources also list a Chaos Warlord Titan. It is not in this catalogue, so you can't add one here.

## Key units

- [[Chaos Warhound Titan]] - The smallest and fastest Titan, with 50 wounds and guns such as the vulcan mega-bolter or the auto-hitting inferno gun.
- [[Chaos Reaver Titan]] - A 70-wound battle Titan with heavy arm weapons and a power fist for crushing other big models.
- [[Chaos Warbringer Nemesis Titan]] - The biggest option here, with 100 wounds, 6 void shields and a quake cannon that can hit targets it can't see.
