Chaos Space Marines are the traitor Legions: power-armoured veterans backed by cultists, daemon-possessed warriors and daemon engines.

## How this army plays

This is a flexible, elite army that leans towards close combat. A typical list is built around a few very tough characters (Abaddon, a Lord Discordant or a Daemon Prince), with Possessed and Terminators doing most of the killing while Legionaries and Cultists hold objectives. Shooting is decent but mostly short-ranged.

- **Strengths:** hard-hitting melee units, durable characters and lots of choice.
- **Weaknesses:** by the end of 9th Edition the codex had fallen behind the strongest armies, and the final balance update of April 2023 gave it no help. Elite infantry is expensive to lose.
- **For a new player:** expect a medium model count. Marines have 2 wounds and Terminators 3, so the army forgives a few mistakes. The hard part is the number of options, so pick one Legion and one or two marks and learn those first.

## Army rules

**Let the Galaxy Burn.** If every unit in your army is TRAITORIS ASTARTES and your <LEGION> units all share one Legion, each battle round your army takes part in a "Wanton act". While it is active, an unmodified hit roll of 6 with certain weapon types scores 1 extra hit:
- Battle round 1, Wanton Destruction: Heavy, Rapid Fire and Grenade weapons.
- Battle round 2, Wanton Massacre: Rapid Fire, Assault and Pistol weapons.
- Battle round 3: you choose Massacre or Slaughter.
- Battle round 4 onwards, Wanton Slaughter: Assault, Pistol and melee weapons.

**Marks of Chaos.** Many units take a Mark, with an extra bonus if they also have the ICON keyword:
- *Khorne:* +1 Strength in melee in a turn the unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- *Tzeentch:* once per turn, the first failed save against the unit has its Damage changed to 0.
- *Nurgle:* attacks whose Strength equals or is at least double the unit's Toughness get -1 to wound.
- *Slaanesh:* the unit fights first if it starts the Fight phase within Engagement Range.

**Other common rules.** *Daemon Engines* have a 5+ invulnerable save and regain 1 lost wound in your Command phase. *Warp Strike* units can start in reserve and arrive more than 9" from enemy models.

**Legions.** A detachment whose <LEGION> units all share a Legion gains that Legion's Trait, Stratagems, Relics and Warlord Traits. The choices are Black Legion, Word Bearers, Night Lords, Iron Warriors, Alpha Legion, Emperor's Children, Red Corsairs and Creations of Bile.

**Signature Stratagems.**
- *Veterans of the Long War* (2 CP): a TRAITORIS ASTARTES INFANTRY or BIKER unit gets +1 to wound when it shoots or fights.
- *Hatred Eternal* (2 CP): a LEGIONARIES unit shoots again at the end of your Shooting phase, or fights again at the end of the Fight phase if it is still within Engagement Range.
- *Wrath of the Chosen* (1 CP): a TERMINATOR or CHOSEN unit gets +1 to hit for the phase.
- *Murderous Perfection* (1 CP): once in the phase, a SLAANESH unit turns one hit, wound or damage roll into a 6.

## Key units

- [[Abaddon the Despoiler]] - Must be your Warlord. He can't lose more than 3 wounds in a phase and makes nearby CHAOS CORE units better at charging and hitting.
- [[Legionaries]] - The backbone Troops: 2-wound marines that hold objectives and can shoot or fight twice with Hatred Eternal.
- [[Possessed]] - Fast, tough daemonic melee infantry. They aren't CORE, so many character auras won't affect them.
- [[Chaos Terminator Squad]] - 3-wound veterans that can Warp Strike in; with the Mark of Slaanesh they fight first.
- [[Lord Discordant on Helstalker]] - A fast Daemon Engine character that damages enemy vehicles or boosts your own.
- [[Cultists Mob]] - Cheap bodies to screen your army and hold objectives.
