## How this army plays

Raven Guard are the stealth and ambush chapter of the Space Marines. You win the game before it starts: redeploying units, moving infantry forward before the first turn, and dropping squads in from ambush where your opponent least wants them.

Your Chapter Tactic makes you hard to shoot from far away, so you can approach in the open and then use terrain to stay safe. Phobos units such as Infiltrators, Incursors and Eliminators fit the style well, and jump pack units led by Kayvaan Shrike give you fast melee strikes. Your super-doctrine helps you hunt enemy characters in Tactical Doctrine.

Expect a tricky, aggressive game that rewards planning. The weakness is that much of your strength is in stratagems and deployment tricks, which cost Command Points and need careful timing. Up close, the Chapter Tactic does nothing, so a unit that ends up in the open within 12" of the enemy is no tougher than any other Marine.

## Army rules

### Shared Space Marine rules

Almost every datasheet has **Angels of Death**:

- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** ignore modifiers to Combat Attrition tests.
- **Bolter Discipline:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire double shots at targets within half range, when the firer is INFANTRY (not Centurions) whose unit stayed still, or when the firer is a TERMINATOR or BIKER.
- **Shock Assault:** +1 Attack in a turn your unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Combat Doctrines:** if every unit in your army is ADEPTUS ASTARTES, the active doctrine improves AP by 1: Devastator for Heavy and Grenade weapons, Tactical for Rapid Fire and Assault weapons, Assault for Pistols and melee. You start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move one step on, but you never have to.

Troops have Objective Secured, and an objective they control at the end of your Command phase stays yours until your opponent takes it.

### Raven Guard rules

- **Shadow Masters (Chapter Tactic):** your units get the benefit of Light Cover against attacks from more than 18" away. INFANTRY wholly on or in a terrain feature get Dense Cover against attacks from more than 12" away.
- **Surgical Strikes (super-doctrine):** in Tactical Doctrine, +1 to hit and +1 to wound against CHARACTER units.
- **Master of Ambush (Warlord Trait):** before the first battle round, redeploy your Warlord and one INFANTRY unit anywhere more than 9" from the enemy.

Signature stratagems:

- **Strike from the Shadows:** set up a RAVEN GUARD INFANTRY unit in ambush, then bring it in at the end of one of your Movement phases more than 9" from all enemy models.
- **Infiltrators:** before the first turn, a RAVEN GUARD INFANTRY unit makes a move, ending more than 9" from enemy models.
- **False Flight:** a unit that Falls Back can still shoot and charge.
- **See, But Remain Unseen:** a unit that made no attacks this turn is -1 to be hit by ranged attacks until your next turn.
- **Stranglehold:** once per battle, if you have SCOUT or PHOBOS units: in the first battle round, each time your opponent uses a stratagem, on a 5+ they must pay 1 extra CP or it has no effect.
- **Ambushing Fire:** once per battle, at the start of your Movement phase while the Tactical Doctrine is active: until the next battle round, unmodified wound rolls of 6 with Rapid Fire and Assault weapons get an extra 1 AP.

## Key units

- [[Kayvaan Shrike]] - A jump pack Chapter Master with hit re-rolls, charge re-rolls for jump pack and Phobos units, and a chance of a mortal wound after he charges.
- [[Infiltrator Squad]] - Phobos Troops that deploy forward with Concealed Positions and keep enemy reinforcements 12" away.
- [[Incursor Squad]] - Phobos Troops that deploy forward and ignore hit roll modifiers when they shoot.
- [[Eliminator Squad]] - Hidden snipers for picking off characters, which Surgical Strikes makes even better.
- [[Aggressor Squad]] - Heavy close-range firepower that is ideal for Strike from the Shadows.
- [[Vanguard Veteran Squad]] - Jump pack melee that strikes fast with Shrike's charge re-rolls.
