## How this army plays

The Astra Militarum, or Imperial Guard, is the Imperium's army of ordinary soldiers. You win with numbers, tanks and artillery. Each Guardsman is weak, but you field a lot of them, and your Officers make them far better with Orders.

Strengths:
- Huge amounts of shooting, from lasguns up to battle tanks and super-heavy tanks.
- Cheap infantry to hold objectives and screen your tanks.
- Tough tanks: a Leman Russ is Toughness 8 with a 2+ save.
- Lots of flexibility, with many units and doctrines to try.

Weaknesses:
- Infantry dies quickly and is poor in melee, apart from Ogryns and Bullgryns.
- Orders have short ranges, so your army must stay organised around its Officers.
- Every Command phase has a lot of decisions, which slows down new players.

Expect to spend your Command phase giving Orders, then shoot hard. A good starting army is a few squads of Cadian Shock Troops, a Command Squad, and two or three tanks.

> **Tip:** the Guard got a new codex at the very end of 9th Edition. Rules such as Hammer of the Emperor and free Infantry Squad wargear came from earlier dataslates and no longer apply.

## Army rules

**Voice of Command (Orders).** In your Command phase, each OFFICER can issue Orders; its datasheet says which types it knows and how many it can issue. There are three types:
- Regimental and Prefectus Orders go to a PLATOON unit within 6" of the Officer.
- Mechanised Orders go to a SQUADRON unit within 12".

A unit can only have one Order at a time, and a new one replaces the old one. The same Officer can't issue the same Order twice in one battle round. Popular Orders include Take Aim! (better hit rolls and AP when shooting), First Rank, Fire! Second Rank, Fire! (more lasgun shots) and Take Cover!.

**Regimental Tactics.** If every unit in your army is ASTRA MILITARUM (apart from Agents of the Imperium and Unaligned units), an Order can spread. When you give a Regimental or Prefectus Order to a unit with this ability, other PLATOON units within 6" of it can get the same Order. Mechanised Orders spread to SQUADRON units within 6" in the same way.

**Regimental Doctrines.** Your army either uses Born Soldiers, or builds a custom doctrine from two options. Born Soldiers makes unmodified hit rolls of 6 with ranged attacks wound automatically. The custom options include Mechanised Infantry, Recon Operators, Armoured Superiority, Expert Bombardiers and Veteran Guerrillas. Kasrkin add one extra doctrine that no other unit in your army has.

**Regiment abilities.** Some datasheets have their own rules. Cadian Shock Troops score an extra hit on unmodified 6s with lasguns and laspistols. Tempestus units are not REGIMENTAL; instead they score an extra hit on every unmodified 6 to hit.

**Turret Weapons.** Many tank guns get +1 to hit. The tank can also fire them while in Engagement Range, at enemy units that are not engaged with it, but the usual -1 to hit for being engaged still applies.

**Key Stratagems.** Cadia Stands! makes wound rolls of 1-3 fail against a CADIAN INFANTRY unit for a phase. Ingrained Precision lets a BORN SOLDIERS unit auto-wound on unmodified hits of 5+. Overcharged Las-cells adds mortal wounds to hot-shot weapons on unmodified wound rolls of 6, up to 6 per unit each phase.

## Key units

- [[Lord Solar Leontus]] - Your best leader, with all three Order types and a re-roll aura for CORE units.
- [[Cadian Command Squad]] - A cheap Officer squad that gives Regimental Orders.
- [[Cadian Shock Troops]] - Your core Troops, protected by Cadia Stands!.
- [[Kasrkin]] - Elite storm troopers with hot-shot guns and an extra doctrine.
- [[Bullgryns]] - Tough Abhumans who hold objectives and win fights.
- [[Leman Russ Battle Tanks]] - The classic battle tank, with many turret options.
- [[Rogal Dorn Battle Tank]] - A bigger, tougher tank with heavy firepower.
- [[Heavy Weapons Squad]] - Cheap mortar teams that shoot at targets out of sight.
