## How this army plays

Daemons are a fast, aggressive melee army that appears from the warp where your opponent least wants it. Every unit belongs to one god: Khorne hits hard in combat, Tzeentch shoots and casts powers, Nurgle is slow and tough, and Slaanesh is very fast. Most armies mix gods, pairing a few Greater Daemons with cheap, quick objective grabbers.

- **Medium model count:** a few big monsters plus infantry units and beast packs.
- **Moderately forgiving.** Arriving from reserve lets you fix a bad deployment, but your units have modest saves and die quickly to massed attacks.
- **Strengths:** speed, flexible deployment, strong melee and tough monsters.
- **Weaknesses:** little shooting outside Tzeentch, and your saves never get better from cover.

Daemons ended the edition as one of the strongest factions, untouched by the final April 2023 dataslate. Popular builds paired Be'lakor, a Bloodthirster and a Lord of Change with Fiends, Flamers and Flesh Hounds.

## Army rules

Every LEGIONES DAEMONICA unit has these **Daemonic** rules:

- **Daemonic Save:** instead of a normal Save, each datasheet has two values: the first against melee attacks, the second against ranged attacks (Bloodletters have 5+/4+). This save can't be modified in any way, so AP doesn't worsen it and cover doesn't improve it.
- **Manifestation:** you can deploy a unit in the warp and bring it in during the Reinforcements step of one of your Movement phases. It must arrive more than 9" from enemy models, or more than 6" away if it is wholly within 6" of one of your WARP LOCUS models that was already on the battlefield when the turn began (a WARP LOCUS with a god keyword only helps units of that god). If every unit in your army is LEGIONES DAEMONICA, it can instead arrive wholly within your deployment zone more than 3" from enemies, or outside both deployment zones and farther from each enemy unit than that unit's Leadership in inches (at least 3", at most 9").
- **Daemonic Terror:** enemy units within 6" get -1 Leadership and -1 to their Combat Attrition tests.
- **Warp Storm:** if every unit in your army is LEGIONES DAEMONICA, you gain Warp Storm points at the start of each battle round (roll eight D6; each 4+ is one point) and spend them on effects, such as extra attacks for Khorne units or fighting first for Slaanesh units.
- **Malefic weapons:** a weapon marked Malefic plus a number gives its bearer exactly that many extra attacks with it every time it fights. Unless a rule says otherwise, other extra-attack effects and characteristic changes don't apply to them.

Each god has its own Warlord Traits, Relics and stratagems, for example:

- *Frenetic Bloodlust* (Khorne, 1 CP, or 2 CP to move): at the start of the Fight phase, a BLOODLETTERS CORE unit not in combat makes a Normal Move of up to 6" ending closer to the closest visible enemy unit; one already in combat piles in instead.
- *Warp Portal* (Tzeentch, 1 CP for a HERALD, otherwise 2 CP): instead of a Normal Move, a TZEENTCH CHARACTER is set up anywhere more than 9" from enemy models.
- *Swarming Flies* (Nurgle, 2 CP): in your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, attacks against one NURGLE unit get -1 to hit.
- *Impossible Elegance* (Slaanesh, 1 CP): at the start of the Fight phase, pick a SLAANESH CHARACTER that isn't a VEHICLE; until the end of the phase, unmodified melee hit rolls of 1-3 against it always fail.

## Key units

- [[Be'lakor]] - The Dark Master: very hard to hurt, a psyker and a WARP LOCUS that leads mixed-god armies.
- [[Bloodthirster]] - Khorne's Greater Daemon, a fast flying monster that can kill almost anything it charges.
- [[Lord of Change]] - Tzeentch's Greater Daemon: a powerful psyker with a 3+ ranged save.
- [[Flamers]] - Flying shooters with plenty of AP -2 shots, one of your few good ranged units.
- [[Fiends]] - Quick, hard-hitting beasts that make enemies in combat with them worse at hitting.
- [[Flesh Hounds]] - Cheap, very fast Khorne hounds for grabbing far objectives.
- [[Nurglings]] - Hard-to-hit swarms that can deploy well forward to screen and hold objectives.
