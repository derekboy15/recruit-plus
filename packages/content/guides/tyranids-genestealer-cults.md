## How this army plays

Genestealer Cults are an uprising from below. Your army is mostly fragile human-looking hybrids with mining tools, autoguns and demolition charges, led by a few scary characters and backed by bikes and industrial vehicles. Your power comes from choosing where and when the fight starts.

Much of your army can begin hidden. You place ambush markers instead of models and reveal what is under them in the first battle round, or keep units underground and bring them in later. Your opponent has to guess where the real threats are, while you pick off key targets and swarm objectives. Your units also cooperate: once one unit has shot at a target, the rest of the cult hits it more easily.

Strengths: board control, cheap bodies for objectives and actions, character snipers, and alpha strikes with demolition charges. Weaknesses: low Toughness and weak saves, so you lose trades in the open, and a bad reveal is hard to undo.

As a new player, expect a thinking game. Plan your reveals before the battle, and use Neophytes to hold ground while your characters and elite units do the killing.

## Army rules

- **Cult Ambush:** units with *Conceal* can be set up in ambush instead of on the battlefield. INFANTRY and BIKER units can instead be set up underground. For each ambushing unit you put an ambush marker wholly within your deployment zone, and enemy models can't be set up or moved within 9" of a marker. In the first battle round you reveal the markers and set the units up there. Underground units arrive later as Reinforcements, away from the enemy.
- **Crossfire:** when an enemy unit takes a lot of hits from one of your units (or a hit from a weapon with Damage higher than 1), it gains a crossfire marker. Your other units then get +1 to hit it. If the target is also Exposed and within 12", you get +1 to wound too and it loses the benefit of cover. Crossfire only works if every unit in your army is GENESTEALER CULTS from a single cult, apart from UNALIGNED units and a limited share of BROOD BROTHERS.
- **Unquestioning Loyalty:** when one of your <CULT> CHARACTER models fails a save, you can pick another of your models with this ability within 3" of it and roll a D6, adding 1 for a PATRIARCH. On a 4+, that model dies instead and your character takes no damage.
- **Cult Creeds:** your cult choice gives your <CULT> units a bonus, plus its own Warlord Trait, Relic, power and stratagem. For example, Twisted Helix makes you stronger, Rusted Claw blunts enemy AP and Pauper Princes hit harder in melee.
- **Brood Brothers:** for each GENESTEALER CULTS Detachment you can add 1 ASTRA MILITARUM Detachment. Those units become BROOD BROTHERS: they can't be your WARLORD or take Relics, get +2 Leadership, and their INFANTRY gain Unquestioning Loyalty. Keep them to 25% of your army or less if you want Crossfire.
- **Key stratagems:** *Return to the Shadows* (1CP) lifts a GENESTEALER CULTS INFANTRY unit more than 6" from the enemy off the board, to come back next turn more than 9" away. *Meticulous Uprising* (2CP) moves ambush markers before you reveal them. *Lurk in the Shadows* (2CP) stops enemy models shooting one of your INFANTRY units unless it's the closest eligible target or within 12" of them.

## Key units

- [[Patriarch]] - Your deadliest melee character and a psyker. Your models get +1 when taking Unquestioning Loyalty tests for him.
- [[Kelermorph]] - A BS 2+ gunslinger that earns extra shots and picks off enemy characters.
- [[Neophyte Hybrids]] - Cheap Troops with heavy mining weapons. Your objective holders.
- [[Acolyte Hybrids]] - Tougher melee Troops with rock saws and demolition charges.
- [[Atalan Jackals]] - Fast bikes with 14" moves that can still shoot and charge after Falling Back.
- [[Purestrain Genestealers]] - WS 2+ hunters with a 4+ invulnerable save.
- [[Aberrants]] - Tough brutes with heavy power weapons that take 1 less damage per attack.
- [[Goliath Rockgrinder]] - A tough transport with a drilldozer blade that delivers hybrids.
