## How this army plays

The Adeptus Astra Telepathica are the Imperium's sanctioned psykers: humans trained to use their dangerous mental powers in the Emperor's service. In 9th Edition this "faction" holds only two datasheets, the Primaris Psyker and the Astropath. Both are ASTRA MILITARUM units, so in practice you add them to an Astra Militarum army rather than building an army from them alone.

Think of them as support pieces. A Primaris Psyker sits behind your lines, shielding your troops, slowing or scaring the enemy, and blasting nearby units with Smite. An Astropath is a small add-on that joins a Command Squad and, if its psychic action works, earns you an extra Command Point.

Strengths:
- Psychic support for an Astra Militarum army: protection, debuffs and mortal wounds.
- The Astropath's psychic action can earn 1 extra CP in your Psychic phase.
- They can use the Astra Militarum stratagems, because they carry the ASTRA MILITARUM keyword.

Weaknesses:
- Both models are fragile humans with a 5+ save: the Primaris Psyker has 4 wounds and the Astropath only 1.
- Psychic powers can fail, and a bad roll can cause Perils of the Warp.
- Two datasheets can't make a legal army on their own.

## Army rules

These units have no army-wide rule of their own. What matters is on their datasheets:

- **The Primaris Psyker** can try to manifest 2 psychic powers in your Psychic phase and deny 1 in your opponent's. It knows Smite and 2 powers from the Psykana discipline. Those powers include giving a friendly ASTRA MILITARUM unit a 5+ invulnerable save, slowing an enemy unit's Move, Advance and charge rolls, and dealing mortal wounds to the closest enemy unit.
- **The Astropath** can manifest 1 power and deny 1. It knows Smite and 1 Psykana power (the research library says only powers 1–3 of the discipline).
- **Divination** is the Astropath's psychic action, with a warp charge of 7. If it succeeds in your Psychic phase, you gain 1 CP. Only one ASTROPATH model in your army can attempt it each Psychic phase.

See the [Psychic phase](/rules/psychic-phase) for how psychic tests, Deny the Witch and Perils of the Warp work.
