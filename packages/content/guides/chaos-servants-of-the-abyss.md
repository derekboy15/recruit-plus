The Black Legion warband of Obsidius Mallex from Blackstone Fortress: a Chaos Lord, a pair of Black Legionnaires and a rabble of cultists, mutants and traitor guardsmen.

## How this army plays

This is a tiny, themed warband rather than a full army. Every unit in it adds up to only a few hundred points, so it suits small narrative games, or a small extra detachment beside a bigger Chaos force.

**Strengths.** Obsidius Mallex is a capable melee character with a thunder hammer and a 4+ invulnerable save, and he helps the whole warband hit more reliably. The Negavolt Cultists and Chaos Beastmen hit surprisingly hard on the charge, and the cheap cultists and guardsmen give you bodies to screen and hold ground.

**Weaknesses.** Apart from Mallex and the Black Legionnaires, everything is fragile. The whole warband must deploy around Mallex, so you can't spread out, and your opponent knows exactly where to aim. If Mallex dies, the rest lose their best support. The Traitor Commissar and Chaos Ogryn have no points values, so you can only use them in games that use Power Level.

**For a new player** it is a simple, low-model-count force with clear jobs: stick together, screen Mallex, and send him into the enemy's best infantry.

> **Not confirmed:** this catalogue still gives Mallex a points cost, but the research library lists him as a Legends unit and records that most Blackstone Fortress characters lost their matched-play points. Check with your opponent or event before using this warband in matched play.

## Army rules

**Servants of the Abyss.** This rule only matters if your army includes OBSIDIUS MALLEX. Units with it can't be set up before him. When you set him up during deployment, they are all set up at the same time. The first time he is set up on the battlefield, each of them must be within 6" of him (or inside the same TRANSPORT). Any that can't be placed count as destroyed.

**Mallex's support.** His Lord of Chaos ability lets friendly BLACK LEGION and SERVANTS OF THE ABYSS units within 6" of him re-roll hit rolls of 1. The Black Legionnaires can only be taken in a detachment that includes him, and neither they nor the Chaos Beastmen use up slots in that detachment.

**Stratagems.** The catalogue gives this warband the Chaos Space Marines and Black Legion Stratagems, but most of them need TRAITORIS ASTARTES, CORE or LEGIONARIES units, and none of these datasheets has those keywords. Check the keywords before you spend CP. A few name BLACK LEGION units, which Mallex and the Black Legionnaires are, for example:
- **Unrelenting Onslaught (2 CP):** when you move a BLACK LEGION unit, it ignores modifiers to its Move, Advance and charge rolls, and can shoot and charge even after Falling Back, until your next Movement phase.
- **Confluence of Traitors (1 CP):** once per battle, give a BLACK LEGION HERETIC ASTARTES unit one Legion Trait until your next Command phase.
- **Vengeful Skies (3 CP):** once per battle, while a BLACK LEGION CHARACTER is on the battlefield, mark a line across the table over two turns. Each unit it crosses suffers D3 mortal wounds on a 3+ (easier against big units, harder against CHARACTERS).

## Key units

- [[Obsidius Mallex]] - Your leader and only real heavy hitter. Everything deploys around him and his re-roll aura keeps the warband effective.
- [[Negavolt Cultists]] - Tough for cultists, with a 5+ invulnerable save and a chance to ignore wounds, and their electro-goads score extra hits on 6s.
- [[Chaos Beastmen]] - Cheap, Strength 4 melee models that get +1 Strength and +1 Attack when they charge.
- [[Black Legionnaires]] - Two power-armoured marines who double their boltgun shots more easily than normal.
- [[Cultists of the Abyss]] - Cheap screening infantry with a heavy stubber or grenade launcher for support.
- [[Traitor Guardsmen]] - Lasgun-armed guardsmen that a Traitor Commissar can push forward faster.
