The Thousand Sons are Tzeentch's sorcerer legion: powerful psykers leading squads of soulless, armoured Rubric Marines.

## How this army plays

This is a psychic army first. Every Rubric Marine and Scarab Occult Terminator squad is led by a sorcerer, so almost every unit is a PSYKER, and the Psychic phase is where you deal much of your damage, with mortal wounds that ignore saves. Your infantry back that up with steady shooting and good protection against small arms.

- **Low model count.** Rubrics and Scarabs are expensive, so every loss matters.
- **Not forgiving.** You track Cabal points, choose rituals, order your psychic powers and plan around Deny the Witch. The army rewards practice more than most.
- **Strengths:** mortal-wound damage at range, infantry that shrug off Damage 1 weapons, and tricks for moving units across the table.
- **Weaknesses:** little dedicated anti-tank, heavy reliance on the Psychic phase, and trouble against armies that resist psychic powers.

By the end of the edition Thousand Sons were one of the weakest armies in competitive play, and the April 2023 dataslate did little for them.

## Army rules

- **Brotherhood of Sorcerers:** in a Battle-forged army, THOUSAND SONS units in THOUSAND SONS Detachments add 1 to their Psychic tests, and ARCANA ASTARTES and TZAANGOR units get a 5+ invulnerable save.
- **Cabal of Sorcerers:** if every unit in your army (apart from UNALIGNED units) is THOUSAND SONS, you gain Cabal points at the start of your Psychic phase. Magnus the Red makes 4; Ahriman, an Exalted Sorcerer or a Thousand Sons Daemon Prince make 3; a Sorcerer, Sorcerer in Terminator Armour or Infernal Master makes 2; an Aspiring Sorcerer, Scarab Occult Sorcerer or Tzaangor Shaman makes 1. Spend them that phase on **Cabbalistic Rituals**; unspent points are lost. Each ritual is normally used once per phase, and each unit can only benefit from one ritual. Examples:
  - *Kindred Sorcerers* (5 points): add 1 to a Psychic test after rolling it. *Wrath of the Immaterium* (9 points) adds 2 instead.
  - *Malevolent Charge* (4 points): after a power deals mortal wounds, one of the units it hit suffers D3 more.
  - *Cabbalistic Focus* (8 points): a power that passed its Psychic test can't be denied.
- **All is Dust:** Rubric Marines and Scarab Occult Terminators add 1 to armour saves against Damage 1 attacks, and don't suffer the hit penalty for moving and firing Heavy weapons. They also automatically pass Morale tests.
- **Detachment limits:** a detachment can't have more Cultist or BRAY units than Rubric and Scarab units, and can have only one DAEMON PRINCE. Each detachment can follow one of the Great Cults, which adds a psychic power, a Warlord Trait and a Relic.

Signature stratagems:

- *Malefic Scroll* (1 CP): a Smite from a THOUSAND SONS PSYKER (not Magnus) deals exactly 3 mortal wounds.
- *Great Sorcerer* (1 CP): a THOUSAND SONS PSYKER can try to manifest one extra power this turn.
- *Unwavering Phalanx* (1 CP for 5 or fewer models, otherwise 3 CP): in your opponent's Shooting phase, attacks against one Rubric Marines or Scarab Occult Terminators unit lose 1 Damage, to a minimum of 1.
- *Webway Infiltration* (2 CP for one THOUSAND SONS INFANTRY unit, 3 CP for two; once per battle): set them up off the table, then bring them in during a later Movement phase more than 9" from any enemy models.

## Key units

- [[Rubric Marines]] - Core troops: tough against light weapons, armed with AP -2 inferno boltguns and led by a psyker.
- [[Scarab Occult Terminators]] - Your hardest-hitting infantry, able to teleport in and very hard to remove with Damage 1 weapons.
- [[Exalted Sorcerer]] - An efficient source of Cabal points and psychic damage, and fast on a Disc of Tzeentch.
- [[Ahriman]] - A master sorcerer who can re-roll his Psychic tests, so his key powers almost always go off.
- [[Magnus the Red]] - The Daemon Primarch: a flying psychic monster who powers the whole Cabal.
- [[Chaos Spawn]] - Cheap, fast models for screening and grabbing objectives so your sorcerers don't have to.
