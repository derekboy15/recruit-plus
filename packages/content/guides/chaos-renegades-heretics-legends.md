Traitor Guardsmen, mutants, cultists and stolen Imperial tanks, rising up against the Emperor. This is a **Warhammer Legends** list: fine for casual and narrative games, but not for matched play or tournaments.

## How this army plays

Renegades & Heretics is a horde army. Big squads of cheap, fragile infantry (Toughness 3, 6+ saves) hold the ground while captured Imperial tanks and artillery do the heavy killing. Expect a high model count and a lot of dice.

**Strengths.** Cheap Troops squads can cover the whole table, and the tank and artillery options give you plenty of firepower. Units charging into combat get a little stronger, so your mobs can tie up enemy units.

**Weaknesses.** The infantry die quickly and have poor Leadership, so Morale and Combat Attrition will cost you models. There are no army-wide rules, no Stratagems of its own and no psychic discipline beyond Smite, so the army has very few tricks.

**What to expect.** Because it is Legends, Games Workshop no longer updated or balanced these datasheets, and most events banned them. Agree with your opponent before you bring it. If you want traitor guardsmen in matched play, the Chaos Space Marines codex has Cultists, Traitor Guardsmen and the Dark Commune instead.

> **Tip:** The catalogue also offers Inquisitors, Assassins and other Agents of the Imperium. They are IMPERIUM units and don't belong in this army, so ignore them.

## Army rules

This list has no faction rules and no Stratagems of its own. What it has is a set of abilities shared across its datasheets.

- **Covenant of Chaos:** when one of these units fights in a turn in which it charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention, its models get +1 Strength for that fight. Most infantry and characters have it.
- **Minions of Chaos:** Renegade Cultists, Renegade Militia and Renegade Mutant Rabble gain Objective Secured if your army is Battle-forged and every model in their detachment is RENEGADES AND HERETICS or UNALIGNED.
- **Leaders and banners:** a Renegade Enforcer's Baleful Judge aura gives +2 Leadership to RENEGADES AND HERETICS CORE units within 6". A Renegade Command Squad can carry the Banner of Hate (re-roll charges for units within 6") or the Banner of the Apostate (units within 6" can make Heroic Interventions as if they were CHARACTERS).
- **Psykers:** the Rogue Psyker Coven can add D3 to a Psychic test, then suffers D3 mortal wounds afterwards. The Malefic Lord has a 4+ invulnerable save and turns into a strong melee fighter if he survives Perils of the Warp.

> **Tip:** The borrowed tanks carry the Astra Militarum Regimental Tactics ability, but it only works if every model in your army has the ASTRA MILITARUM keyword, so it does nothing here.

## Key units

- [[Renegade Cultists]] - Cheap bodies in squads of up to 30 that can hold objectives with Objective Secured.
- [[Renegade Militia Squad]] - Your line infantry, with heavy weapon teams and Objective Secured.
- [[Renegade Command Squad]] - Its banners make nearby mobs better at charging and counter-charging.
- [[Rogue Psyker Coven]] - A risky but powerful source of Smite mortal wounds.
- [[Renegade Ogryn Brutes]] - Tough melee hitters with extra Strength and Attacks when they charge.
- [[Leman Russ Battle Tanks]] - The workhorse battle tank, giving the army the heavy firepower its infantry lacks.
