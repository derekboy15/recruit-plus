The Leagues of Votann are stocky, cybernetic Kin from the galactic core.

## How this army plays

Votann are an elite, durable shooting army. Hearthkyn Warriors and Hernkyn Pioneers hold objectives, while Hearthguard, Thunderkyn and the Hekaton Land Fortress deal the damage. Enemies that destroy your units, complete actions or hold objectives get Judgement tokens, and your attacks against them wound far more easily.

**Strengths.** Void Armour makes every unit resilient, and characters hand out re-rolls.

**Weaknesses.** Your infantry is slow (Move 5"), you have few models, and every loss hurts. Tokens are strongest on units that have already hurt you, so you often trade first.

**What to expect.** You track a lot of tokens, then focus your fire on the units that carry them.

## Army rules

**Eye of the Ancestors.** If every unit is VOTANN from one League (ignoring UNALIGNED models), enemy units gain Judgement tokens, up to 3 each. A unit gains one when it destroys one of your units or completes an action or psychic action, and at the end of each of your opponent's turns you can give one to an enemy unit within range of an objective marker your opponent controls. Your attacks (except by COG models) against a unit with tokens wound automatically on an unmodified hit roll of 6 (1 token), 5+ (2 tokens) or 4+ (3 tokens). That automatic wound never counts as an unmodified wound roll of 6. Tokens stay until the unit is destroyed.

**Grim Efficiency.** In each of your Command phases, if every unit is from the same League (ignoring UNALIGNED models), a Kâhl can give one visible enemy unit a Judgement token.

**Void Armour.** Your opponent can't re-roll wound rolls or damage rolls against these units, and each attack allocated to them has its Armour Penetration reduced by 1, to a minimum of 0.

**Steady Advance.** These units ignore modifiers to their Move characteristic and to Advance and charge rolls. When they Advance, they don't roll: they add 3" to their Move instead, or 6" if they are ACCELERATED.

**Weapon types.** Beam weapons draw a line to the target and also roll to wound against other eligible units the line crosses. Magna-rail weapons ignore invulnerable saves, and on an unmodified wound roll of 6 their excess damage carries over to other models in the unit.

**Leagues.** A mono-League army gains its League's customs and its Ancestral Judgement bonus, which ties into Judgement tokens. Greater Thurian League models count double for holding objectives, and Ymyr Conglomerate units gain range and an invulnerable save. You can also build a custom League.

**Signature stratagems.** Ancestral Sentence (1 CP) lets a VOTANN unit re-roll hit rolls against targets with 1 or more Judgement tokens in the Shooting or Fight phase. Huntr's Mark (1 CP) makes one shooting attack against such a target hit automatically. Reactive Reprisal (1 CP for HEARTHKYN WARRIORS, 2 CP otherwise) lets a VOTANN CORE unit shoot straight back at a unit with tokens that just shot it, but it can't shoot in your next Shooting phase.

> **Tip:** Some early reviews say token auto-wounds count as unmodified 6s to wound. Games Workshop changed that on 29 September 2022, before most of the range was on sale.

## Key units

- [[Kâhl]] - adds a Judgement token every Command phase, and re-rolls hit rolls of 1 for nearby CORE units.
- [[Ûthar the Destined]] - a Greater Thurian League hero who takes only 1 damage from each attack and can turn one nearby roll into a 6 each battle round.
- [[Grimnyr]] - your psyker, with a 4+ invulnerable save.
- [[Hearthkyn Warriors w/ ion blasters]] - your Troops: steady, flexible objective holders.
- [[Hernkyn Pioneers]] - fast, Toughness 5 bikes with a 12" pre-game move, great for scoring.
- [[Cthonian Beserks w/ concussion mauls]] - hard-hitting melee that ignores wounds on a 5+ (4+ against Damage 1) and fights on when killed.
- [[Brôkhyr Thunderkyn w/ graviton blast cannons]] - heavy weapons that deny dense cover.
- [[Hekaton Land Fortress]] - a Toughness 8 transport for 12 models carrying huge guns.
