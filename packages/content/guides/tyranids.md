## How this army plays

Tyranids are an alien swarm with one mind. You mix cheap bugs that you happily lose by the dozen with huge monsters that soak up damage and hit very hard. Almost everything wants to get close. Your few shooting units support the rush rather than replace it.

The army is built around **SYNAPSE** units: Hive Tyrants, Tyranid Warriors, Zoanthropes, Tervigons and similar creatures. They keep your swarm from fleeing and power your Synaptic Imperatives, so keep your little bugs near them. If your opponent kills your Synapse first, your army loses much of its edge.

Strengths: you bring more bodies than almost anyone, your monsters are tough, and you have burrowers, psychic mortal wounds and models you can bring back. Weaknesses: your cheap models die easily to blast and flamer weapons, your big monsters can be focused down one at a time, and you have few ways to fix a bad position once you're committed.

As a new player, expect to move a lot of models every turn. Start with a simple plan: screen with Termagants, push Hormagaunts forward to tie enemies up, and let a Hive Tyrant and a unit of Warriors anchor the middle of the table.

## Army rules

- **Synapse:** while one of your <HIVE FLEET> units is within 6" of a friendly SYNAPSE unit, it auto-passes Morale tests.
- **Synaptic Link:** many command abilities can target any of your units within 12" of a SYNAPSE unit. That range chains through other SYNAPSE units that are within 12" of each other.
- **Shadow in the Warp:** enemy PSYKER units within 18" of one of your units with this aura (most SYNAPSE units have it) take -1 to Psychic tests, and they suffer 1 extra mortal wound when they suffer Perils of the Warp.
- **Synaptic Imperatives:** if every unit in your army is HIVE TENDRIL (UNALIGNED units excepted) and shares one hive fleet (LIVING ARTILLERY excepted), then at the start of each battle round you pick one Imperative offered by one of your SYNAPSE units on the battlefield. Each Imperative works once per battle, for your units near your SYNAPSE units. For example, the Hive Tyrant's lets your units charge after Falling Back.
- **Hive Fleets:** your hive fleet gives an Adaptation and an Adaptive bonus. Once you know who goes first, you can swap the Adaptive bonus for a Hyper-adaptation. Leviathan makes Synapse monsters hard to wound, Kraken is fast, Behemoth is strong on the charge, Kronos boosts shooting, and Gorgon is poisonous.
- **Swarming Masses:** Termagants, Hormagaunts and Gargoyles can fight with models up to 2½" from the enemy, so far more than the front rank swings.
- **Key stratagems:** *Endless Swarm* (1CP) returns up to D3+3 destroyed models to an ENDLESS MULTITUDE unit. *Overrun* (1CP) lets a HIVE TENDRIL CORE unit that charged make a Normal Move at the end of the Fight phase if it's no longer engaged. *Adrenal Surge* (1/2CP) adds attacks to a charging ADRENAL GLANDS unit.

> **Tip:** Older articles say a Hive Tyrant had to be your Warlord and Imperatives needed your Warlord alive. The final dataslate (April 2023) removed both limits.

## Key units

- [[The Swarmlord]] - A tough melee monster and SYNAPSE leader who gives a CORE unit Objective Secured.
- [[Hive Tyrant]] - T8 with a 2+ save and a 4+ invulnerable save, a psyker, and lets a CORE unit re-roll hit rolls of 1.
- [[Tyranid Warriors]] - CORE INFANTRY with SYNAPSE, 3 wounds each, and many weapon options. They hold objectives.
- [[Termagants]] - Cheap ENDLESS MULTITUDE screens with 18" fleshborers.
- [[Hormagaunts]] - Fast melee swarm that piles in up to 3" further and drowns enemies in attacks.
- [[Zoanthropes]] - SYNAPSE psykers with a 4+ invulnerable save that deal mortal wounds.
- [[Maleceptor]] - A psychic monster that deals extra mortal wounds when it casts with a high Psychic test.
- [[Exocrine]] - Your most reliable shooting monster, and its targets lose cover if it barely moves.
