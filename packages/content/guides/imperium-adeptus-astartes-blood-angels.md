## How this army plays

Blood Angels are the jump-pack chapter. Your army flies across the table, drops in from the sky and wins the game in the Fight phase. You have the whole Space Marine range plus your own angelic heroes, the berserk Death Company and the golden Sanguinary Guard.

Your strengths are speed and melee damage. Your chapter rules make every charge hit harder, and you have many ways to arrive close to the enemy. Your characters are among the best fighters in the game.

Your weakness is that most of your power only works after you charge. If the enemy shoots you off the table first, or screens you so you can't reach good targets, you lose much of your damage. Your shooting is ordinary Marine shooting.

Expect to spend the first turn hiding and setting up, then strike hard in turn 2 and 3. Hide your jump-pack units out of sight, keep some in reserve, and pick your charges carefully. A Blood Angels charge that lands on the right target wins games.

## Army rules

Your units have the usual **Angels of Death** rules. Combat Doctrines start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move on one step (Devastator → Tactical → Assault), but you never have to. Your Troops keep objectives they captured until the enemy takes them back.

**The Red Thirst** is your Chapter Tactic. Add 1 to Advance and charge rolls for these units. In a turn in which the unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention, add 1 to the wound roll for its melee attacks.

**Savage Echoes** is your super-doctrine. If every unit in your army (other than UNALIGNED units) is BLOOD ANGELS, then while the Assault Doctrine is active, a unit that charges, is charged or makes a Heroic Intervention gets +1 Attack until the end of the turn. This adds to Shock Assault.

**The Black Rage** is on every DEATH COMPANY unit. The unit can't Fall Back and can't perform actions. It gets +1 Attack when it fights in a turn in which it charged or made a Heroic Intervention, and it ignores each lost wound on a 6.

> **Tip:** A Sanguinary Priest's **Blood Chalice** lets one nearby unit count the Assault Doctrine as active for its attacks while your army is still in Devastator or Tactical. Keep a Priest close to your main assault unit.

Signature stratagems:

- **Upon Wings of Fire** (1CP): remove one of your CORE JUMP PACK units and set it up again in your next Reinforcements step, more than 9" from enemy models.
- **Descent of Angels** (1CP): a CORE JUMP PACK unit that arrived from Reinforcements this phase ignores charge roll modifiers and gets +1 to hit until the end of the turn.
- **Forlorn Fury** (1 or 2CP, once per battle): before the first turn, one DEATH COMPANY unit moves up to 12", ending more than 9" from enemy models.
- **Angel's Sacrifice** (1CP): enemies fighting one of your non-VEHICLE characters must attack it.

## Key units

- [[Commander Dante]] - a fast, hard-to-hit Chapter Master who re-rolls one nearby unit's hit rolls.
- [[The Sanguinor]] - can appear in combat when the enemy charges your units, and gives nearby units extra attacks.
- [[Chief Librarian Mephiston (Primaris)]] - a fearsome psyker who is also a melee monster.
- [[Sanguinary Guard]] - elite jump-pack warriors with a 2+ save, hard to hit in melee.
- [[Death Company Intercessors]] - cheap Primaris berserkers with huge damage on the charge.
- [[Death Company Marines]] - berserkers who can take jump packs to reach the enemy fast.
- [[Sanguinary Priest]] - heals, and switches on the Assault Doctrine early for one unit.
