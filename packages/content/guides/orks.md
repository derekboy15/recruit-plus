Orks are a loud, numerous army of brawlers, beasts and ramshackle vehicles.

## How this army plays

Orks win by flooding the board and charging. Boyz are cheap Toughness 5 bodies, Beast Snagga units hunt vehicles and monsters, and Kill Rigs and Squighog Boyz hit fast. Shooting is plentiful but inaccurate: most Orks shoot on a 5+. Everything builds towards one huge turn, the Waaagh!.

**Strengths.** Lots of models, durable Boyz, re-rollable charges and a Waaagh! turn that makes the whole army stronger and harder to kill.

**Weaknesses.** Poor armour saves and low Leadership mean you lose a lot of models. The Waaagh! is once per battle, so calling it at the wrong time wastes your best turn.

**What to expect.** Big model counts and long turns. Plan your Waaagh! turn before the game and don't be afraid to trade Boyz for board control.

## Army rules

**Waaagh!** If your WARLORD is a WARBOSS, once per battle in your Command phase you can call a Waaagh!, as long as that WARBOSS is on the battlefield or embarked in a TRANSPORT that is. It has two stages:

- **Stage 1** (until your next Command phase): ORKS CORE and ORKS CHARACTER units can charge even after Advancing, and ORKS models get +1 Strength, +1 Attack and a 5+ invulnerable save.
- **Stage 2** (until the Command phase after that): ORKS models keep +1 Strength and +1 Attack, with a 6+ invulnerable save.

A SPEEDBOSS WARLORD calls a **Speedwaaagh!** instead. In stage 1, ORKS models take no hit penalty for Assault weapons after Advancing, and ORKS VEHICLE and ORKS BIKER models make 1 extra attack with each Dakka weapon. In both stages, ranged attacks by ORKS VEHICLE and ORKS BIKER units improve their Armour Penetration by 1. If GHAZGHKULL THRAKA is your WARLORD, he calls a **Great Waaagh!**, which counts as both at once.

**'Ere We Go.** Almost every Ork unit can re-roll its charge rolls.

**Dakka weapons.** These have two Attacks values; use the higher one if the target is within half range.

**Mob Rule.** While a unit is within 6" of a friendly <CLAN> MOB unit that isn't below Half-strength, it never counts as below Half-strength.

**Clan Kulturs.** In an ORKS Detachment, <CLAN> units (except GRETCHIN) gain their clan's kultur, and Troops (except GRETCHIN) gain Objective Secured. Goffs score extra hits in melee, Evil Sunz move faster, Bad Moons shoot further with Dakka and Heavy weapons, and Deathskulls can re-roll one hit or wound roll each time they shoot or fight.

**Signature stratagems.** Get Stuck In Ladz! (1 CP, or 2 CP for more than 10 models) lets BOYZ or BEAST SNAGGA BOYZ pile in and consolidate up to an extra 3". Tellyporta (2 CP) keeps a non-MONSTER unit of Power Rating 20 or less off the table to arrive later, more than 9" from enemy models.

> **Tip:** Older articles describe a weaker Waaagh!. The June 2022 Balance Dataslate added the invulnerable saves, so use the version above.

## Key units

- [[Ghazghkull Thraka]] - calls the Great Waaagh!, can't lose more than 4 wounds in a phase, and lets nearby GOFF units re-roll melee hit rolls.
- [[Warboss]] - the usual way to call a Waaagh!, and gives nearby <CLAN> CORE and CHARACTER units +1 to hit in melee.
- [[Boyz]] - cheap Objective Secured Troops with Toughness 5 and 2 attacks each.
- [[Beast Snagga Boyz]] - tougher Troops with a 6+ invulnerable save and +1 to hit against VEHICLE and MONSTER units.
- [[Kommandos]] - can set up anywhere more than 9" from the enemy deployment zone and enemy models.
- [[Squighog Boyz]] - fast, tough cavalry that hit hard on the charge.
- [[Kill Rig]] - a big Toughness 8 Beast Snagga chariot that shoots, transports and fights.
- [[Meganobz]] - 2+ save, 3-wound elite infantry that escort your bosses.
