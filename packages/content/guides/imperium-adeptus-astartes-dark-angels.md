## How this army plays

Dark Angels are three armies in one. The **Deathwing** is your 1st Company: tough Terminators that teleport in and refuse to die. The **Ravenwing** is your 2nd Company: fast bikes, speeders and jets. The rest of the chapter uses the normal Space Marine range. You can mix all three or build around one wing.

Your strengths are staying power and solid shooting. Your Chapter Tactic rewards units that hold their ground, so a gunline sitting on objectives hits more often. Terminators with storm shields are very hard to shift, and your Ravenwing has invulnerable saves against shooting while it keeps moving.

Your weaknesses are cost and numbers. Terminators and bikes are expensive, so you have few models and every loss hurts.

Expect a patient game. Hold the middle with your toughest units, shoot from position, and use the Ravenwing or teleporting Terminators to take objectives late.

## Army rules

Your units have the usual **Angels of Death** rules. Combat Doctrines start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move on one step (Devastator → Tactical → Assault), but you never have to. Your Troops keep objectives they captured until the enemy takes them back.

**Grim Resolve** is your Chapter Tactic. Your attacks get +1 to hit unless the unit has moved this turn (pile-in and consolidation moves don't count). Combat Attrition tests for these units are passed automatically.

**Sons of the Lion** gives each doctrine a bonus for one wing:

- **Devastator, Speed of the Raven:** RAVENWING models get +3" Move and can shoot after Advancing, with the same hit penalty as Assault weapons.
- **Tactical, Fire Discipline:** INFANTRY other than DEATHWING can fire Rapid Fire and Assault weapons (not Blast) at enemies they are in combat with, using a Ballistic Skill of 5+.
- **Assault, Implacable:** DEATHWING INFANTRY and DREADNOUGHTS re-roll melee wound rolls against CHARACTERS and units containing a model with 8 or more wounds.

**Inner Circle** (DEATHWING and INNER CIRCLE units) means the unit always passes Morale tests. To Fall Back it must roll 2D6 equal to or under its Leadership (VEHICLE, RAVENWING and CHAPTER MASTER units skip this roll), and it can't Fall Back from FALLEN units at all.

> **Tip:** Older guides say Inner Circle Terminators ignore wound rolls of 1-3. That protection was removed by the final Balance Dataslate in April 2023, so it no longer applies.

**Jink** (RAVENWING units) gives a 5+ invulnerable save against ranged attacks, improving to 4+ until your next turn after the unit Advances. If the unit Remains Stationary it loses the save until your next Movement phase.

**1st Company** and **2nd Company:** in an all-Dark Angels army, a Vanguard Detachment of only DEATHWING or INNER CIRCLE units gives its Terminator squads Objective Secured. An Outrider Detachment of only RAVENWING units does the same for its Bike Squads and Outrider Squads.

Signature stratagems:

- **Tactical Appraisal** (1CP): one unit within 6" of your WARLORD uses the doctrine of your choice until your next Command phase.
- **Line Unbreakable** (1CP): one INFANTRY unit can only be attacked in melee by models that are actually within Engagement Range of it.
- **Weapons from the Dark Age** (2CP): one unit's plasma weapons get +1 Damage this phase.

## Key units

- [[Azrael]] - your Chapter Master; gives extra CP as Warlord and nearby INFANTRY and BIKER units a 4+ invulnerable save against shooting.
- [[Belial]] - the Deathwing's leader, letting one Deathwing unit re-roll all hit rolls.
- [[Ezekiel]] - a psyker whose aura gives nearby units an extra attack.
- [[Lion El'Jonson]] - your Primarch: fights first and can arrive from the shadowed paths.
- [[Deathwing Terminator Squad]] - the core of a Deathwing army: tough and flexible.
- [[Deathwing Knights]] - the toughest melee Terminators, with storm shields and maces.
- [[Ravenwing Black Knights]] - fast, hard-hitting bikes with plasma talons.
- [[Ravenwing Talonmaster]] - a fast speeder character who strips cover from a chosen enemy for your Ravenwing.
