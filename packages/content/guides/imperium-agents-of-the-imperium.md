## How this army plays

Agents of the Imperium are specialists you add to another Imperium army: Inquisitors and their retinues, the four Officio Assassinorum Assassins, Rogue Traders and Navy crews, and Adeptus Arbites squads. Most players use them as a bolt-on. You take one Agent in each qualifying detachment, for free, and it fills a gap your main army has.

Strengths:
- Free to include. An Agent doesn't take a Battlefield Role slot and doesn't switch off your army's own rules.
- Each Assassin is a specialist. The Vindicare snipes characters, the Eversor shreds infantry, the Culexus shuts down psykers and the Callidus taxes command points.
- Inquisitors bring psychic powers, a Leadership aura and re-rolls against their chosen enemy.

Weaknesses:
- Agents are small, fragile units. They can't win a game alone.
- There are no Agents stratagems, Warlord Traits or secondary objectives. You use the ones from your main army.
- A whole army of Agents is possible, but it is a narrow, unusual way to play.

What to expect: start by adding one Agent to an army you already play. Pick the one that fixes your biggest problem, such as a sniper for enemy characters or an anti-psyker unit.

## Army rules

These rules come from the units' own datasheets. The faction has no army-wide rules or stratagems of its own.

- **Agent of the Imperium:** in a Battle-forged army, each IMPERIUM Patrol, Boarding Patrol, Battalion, Brigade or Arks of Omen detachment can include 1 AGENT OF THE IMPERIUM unit without using a Battlefield Role slot. FALLEN detachments don't qualify. The Agent doesn't stop the rest of the detachment from using its detachment abilities, such as Chapter Tactics. It also doesn't break abilities that need every model in your army to have them, such as Combat Doctrines. It is ignored when checking shared Faction keywords and your Army Faction. An Agent can never join a BROOD BROTHERS detachment.
- **Authority of the Inquisition:** your INQUISITION INFANTRY can ride in any IMPERIUM transport, as long as its other limits allow it. A detachment made only of Inquisition units can include just 1 INQUISITOR.
- **Quarry:** each Ordo hunts a different foe. Its units re-roll hit rolls and wound rolls against it:
  - ORDO MALLEUS: CHAOS or DAEMON units.
  - ORDO HERETICUS: CHAOS or PSYKER units.
  - ORDO XENOS: units that are not CHAOS, IMPERIUM or UNALIGNED.
  - ORDO MINORIS: CHARACTER units.
- **Unquestionable Wisdom (Aura):** your IMPERIUM units within 6" of an Inquisitor can use its Leadership.
- **Assassins:** each has a 4+ invulnerable save and can never have a Warlord Trait. **Independent Operative** lets an Assassin start off the table and arrive in a Reinforcements step more than 9" from every enemy model. **Execution Force** lets you put Assassins in a Vanguard detachment with no HQ, as long as your Warlord is IMPERIUM. That detachment has no Command Benefits and can't repeat a datasheet.
- **Navy and Arbites Troops:** Breachers, Voidsmen and Arbites squads can't fill a compulsory slot, such as the only Troops in a Patrol. Arbites squads get **Brutal Judgement**: unmodified hit rolls of 6 wound automatically against targets within half range.

## Key units

- [[Inquisitor]] - A cheap HQ that can be a psyker. It brings Quarry re-rolls and a Leadership aura.
- [[Inquisitor Coteaz]] - Shoots enemy units that arrive near him, and once per battle makes an enemy stratagem cost 1 extra CP.
- [[Vindicare Assassin]] - A sniper who ignores Look Out, Sir. Use him to pick off enemy characters.
- [[Callidus Assassin]] - In the first battle round she can make enemy stratagems cost 1 extra CP, or fail.
- [[Culexus Assassin]] - Immune to psychic powers. Enemy PSYKERS within 18" take -2 to cast and deny.
- [[Eversor Assassin]] - A melee killer who rolls 3D6 to charge and gets extra attacks as models die.
- [[Imperial Navy Breacher]] - Tough Navy Troops whose Void Armour worsens the AP of incoming attacks by 1.
