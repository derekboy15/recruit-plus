The Harlequins are warrior-performers of the Laughing God, a small, fast army that dances through enemy lines.

## How this army plays

Harlequins are a fast melee army with a short list of units. The core is the Troupe: Players who ride in Starweaver transports, jump out, shoot with fusion pistols and charge. Characters such as the Troupe Master and Solitaire add killing power, the Shadowseer brings psychic tricks, and Skyweaver jetbikes and Voidweaver skimmers give you ranged firepower.

**Strengths.** Everything is quick, and your infantry can move through models and terrain, so little is out of reach. You can Advance and still charge, or Fall Back and still shoot and charge, so your units are hard to pin down.

**Weaknesses.** Players are Toughness 3 with 1 wound and rely on a 5+ invulnerable save. You have few models and few datasheets, so every loss hurts, and big, tough targets need fusion pistols or haywire weapons.

**What to expect.** You decide where and when the fight happens. Stay hidden in transports, pick a target you can wipe out, then use your speed to get clear.

> **Tip:** Older articles describe Harlequins with a 4+ invulnerable save. Since January 2023 most of their invulnerable saves are 1 worse, usually 5+.

## Army rules

**Harlequin's Panoply.** Models in units with this rule have a 5+ invulnerable save. When they make a Normal Move, Advance, Fall Back or charge move, they can move horizontally through models and terrain (but can't end on top of a model). Melee attacks against them are -1 to hit.

**Rising Crescendo.** These units can declare a charge in a turn they Advanced, and can shoot and declare a charge in a turn they Fell Back.

**Saedaths.** Each unit performs a saedath, which gives it a bonus:

- **Light:** attacks against the unit from more than 18" away fail on an unmodified hit roll of 1-3. A unit that made a Normal Move or Advanced in your Movement phase counts as having Remained Stationary in your next Shooting phase, unless it is embarked.
- **Dark:** melee attacks get +1 AP. When one of your models is destroyed by a melee attack, roll a D6; on a 4+ the attacking unit suffers 1 mortal wound once it has finished attacking (up to 6 per phase).
- **Twilight:** +1 Attack in a fight after charging, being charged or making a Heroic Intervention, and an extra 2" on pile-in and consolidation moves.

**Luck of the Laughing God.** If every unit is HARLEQUINS performing the same saedath (SOLITAIRE and UNALIGNED units don't count), you start each battle round with 0 Luck re-rolls, but you can make a Luck of the Laughing God roll: roll up to 6 dice, and if every result is different you gain that many Luck re-rolls. Each one re-rolls a hit roll, wound roll, damage roll, saving throw or Advance roll for one of your units with this rule. Unused re-rolls are lost at the end of the battle round.

**Stratagems to know.** War Dancers (3 CP) lets a TROUPE still in Engagement Range fight again at the end of the Fight phase, once per battle round. Murderous Spectacle (2 CP) adds 1 to the Damage of a charging HARLEQUINS INFANTRY unit's melee weapons (to a maximum of 3). Virtuosos of the Webway (1 CP) pulls a unit not in Engagement Range off the table into Strategic Reserves.

## Key units

- [[Troupe]] - the backbone of the army: fast Troops with fusion pistols and Harlequin melee weapons.
- [[Starweaver]] - a fast transport for 6 HARLEQUINS INFANTRY models that keeps Troupes safe until they strike.
- [[Troupe Master]] - a melee leader with 6 attacks who unlocks Stratagems such as Heroes' Path.
- [[Shadowseer]] - your PSYKER, with Phantasmancy powers to protect and trick.
- [[Death Jester]] - a shrieker cannon that can ignore Look Out, Sir to snipe characters.
- [[Solitaire]] - a Movement 12" melee killer with 8 attacks and a 4+ invulnerable save.
- [[Voidweavers]] - fast skimmers with haywire or prismatic cannons for anti-tank fire.
