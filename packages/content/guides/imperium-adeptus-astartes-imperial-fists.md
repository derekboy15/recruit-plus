## How this army plays

Imperial Fists are the siege specialists of the Space Marines. You hold ground, dig in and shoot. Bolters are your bread and butter: your Chapter Tactic gives bolt weapons extra hits, and your stratagems stack more on top. Heavy weapons pointed at tanks and buildings are your other strength.

Expect a patient, defensive game. You set up a firing line, sit on objectives and make your opponent come to you. Because you can stay in Devastator Doctrine all game, your Heavy weapons keep their AP bonus from start to finish. Stratagems such as **Bolster Defences** and **The Shield Unwavering** make units in cover or on objectives tougher.

The weakness is speed and melee. Fists have few tricks for reaching distant objectives or for winning big fights. Their super-doctrine only helps Strength 7+ Heavy weapons against vehicles and buildings, so it does nothing against infantry hordes. This was one of the weaker Marine chapters in competitive play, so it rewards careful positioning.

The catalogue also covers the **Crimson Fists**, a successor chapter with its own tactic and Pedro Kantor.

## Army rules

### Shared Space Marine rules

Almost every datasheet has **Angels of Death**:

- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** ignore modifiers to Combat Attrition tests.
- **Bolter Discipline:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire double shots at targets within half range, when the firer is INFANTRY (not Centurions) whose unit stayed still, or when the firer is a TERMINATOR or BIKER.
- **Shock Assault:** +1 Attack in a turn your unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Combat Doctrines:** if every unit in your army is ADEPTUS ASTARTES, the active doctrine improves AP by 1: Devastator for Heavy and Grenade weapons, Tactical for Rapid Fire and Assault weapons, Assault for Pistols and melee. You start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move one step on, but you never have to.

Troops have Objective Secured, and an objective they control at the end of your Command phase stays yours until your opponent takes it.

### Imperial Fists rules

- **Siege Masters (Chapter Tactic):** your ranged attacks ignore Light Cover, and an unmodified hit roll of 6 with a bolt weapon scores 1 extra hit.
- **Legacy of Dorn (super-doctrine):** in Devastator Doctrine, Heavy weapons with Strength 7 or more get +1 Damage against VEHICLE and BUILDING units.
- **No Matter the Odds (Crimson Fists tactic):** +1 to hit with ranged attacks against units with at least 5 more models than yours, and bolt weapons score an extra hit on an unmodified 6 to hit.

Signature stratagems:

- **Bolter Drill:** a CORE or CHARACTER unit's bolt weapons score 1 extra hit on an unmodified hit roll of 6. This stacks with Siege Masters.
- **Close-range Bolter Fire:** a CORE or CHARACTER unit's bolt weapons count as Pistols this phase.
- **Tank Hunters:** +1 to wound against one enemy VEHICLE unit.
- **Bolster Defences:** once per battle, a unit in cover adds 1 more to its armour saves until it moves.
- **Praetorian's Wrath:** once per battle, at the start of your Movement phase while the Devastator Doctrine is active: until your next Movement phase, unmodified wound rolls of 6 with your Heavy and Grenade weapons get an extra 1 AP.

## Key units

- [[Captain Lysander]] - A Terminator Captain with a 3+ invulnerable save who keeps nearby units steady; as your WARLORD, unmodified wound rolls of 1-3 against him always fail.
- [[Tor Garadon]] - Gives one nearby unit +1 to hit with ranged attacks and hits vehicles and buildings extra hard.
- [[Pedro Kantor]] - The Crimson Fists Chapter Master, with hit re-rolls and an aura of extra attacks.
- [[Intercessor Squad]] - Bolt rifle Troops that get the most out of Siege Masters, Bolter Drill and sticky objectives.
- [[Centurion Devastator Squad]] - Slow, tough heavy-weapon platforms for your firing line.
- [[Gladiator Lancer]] - A Strength 10 tank hunter that makes full use of Legacy of Dorn.
- [[Eradicator Squad]] - Melta infantry that delete vehicles at close range.
