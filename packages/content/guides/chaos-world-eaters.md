The World Eaters are Khorne's berserkers: a Legion that lives to charge, kill and take skulls for the Blood God.

## How this army plays

World Eaters run across the table and win in close combat. There are no psykers and almost no shooting, so every turn is about getting your units into the right fight.

- **Strengths:** huge melee damage, fast units (Eightbound can arrive from reserves in the first battle round), and a Blood Tithe engine that grows stronger as the battle gets bloodier. The final balance update of April 2023 left the army alone, and it stayed a strong tournament army to the end of 9th Edition.
- **Weaknesses:** few tools against invulnerable saves or enemies you can't reach, and melee armies such as Chaos Daemons can be a hard match.
- **For a new player:** the plan is simple, which makes it a good first army, but it punishes charging in one unit at a time. Expect a medium model count: small squads of Berzerkers, a few Eightbound and some cheap Jakhals and Spawn.

## Army rules

**Relentless Rage.** Every unit has this. At the start of the Fight phase, if the unit made a charge move, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention this turn, its models get +1 Strength and +1 Attacks until the end of the phase.

**The Blood Tithe.** If every unit in your army is WORLD EATERS (models with AGENT OF CHAOS or UNALIGNED don't count), you collect Blood Tithe points (BTP) during the battle:
- 1 BTP each time any unit is destroyed, whether it is yours or the enemy's.
- 1 extra BTP at the end of a phase in which a CHARACTER, MONSTER or VEHICLE unit was destroyed, and 1 more if a TITANIC unit was.

At the end of each phase you can spend BTP on one Blessing of the Blood God. Blessings last for the rest of the battle, and each can only be bought once, except Reborn in Blood. Examples:
- *Spiteful Nullification* (2 BTP): a 5+ roll to ignore mortal wounds.
- *Warp Blades* (4 BTP): +1 AP in melee.
- *Total Carnage* (6 BTP): unmodified melee hit rolls of 6 wound automatically.
- *Reborn in Blood* (6 BTP): a destroyed ANGRON goes back into reserve with 8 wounds and can return as if it had Warp Strike.

**Sub-factions.** Choose *World Eaters*, which uses the whole roster, or *Disciples of the Red Angel*, which must have Angron as Warlord and is built mostly from KHORNE DAEMON units such as Eightbound.

**Signature Stratagems.**
- *Red Butchers* (2 CP): a TERMINATORS or EIGHTBOUND unit adds 1 to the Damage of its attacks when it fights.
- *Blood Frenzy* (1 CP for Troops, 2 CP otherwise): models in a CORE unit killed in melee before they have fought get to fight before they are removed.
- *Gory Dismemberment* (1 CP): unmodified wound rolls of 6 in melee also deal 1 mortal wound, up to 6 per use.
- *Skulls for the Skull Throne!* (1 or 2 CP): when your CHARACTER kills the enemy WARLORD in melee, gain 2 BTP and/or +1 to that model's Advance and charge rolls.
- *Bloodscent* (1 CP, Disciples): when an enemy Falls Back from EIGHTBOUND, they consolidate up to 6".

## Key units

- [[Khorne Berserkers]] - Objective Secured Troops with brutal chainblades. When shooting kills some of them, the rest can Blood Surge towards the enemy.
- [[Eightbound]] - Fast daemonic killers that can arrive from Strategic Reserves in the first battle round.
- [[Exalted Eightbound]] - The scariest melee unit in the book: chainfists, a 4+ invulnerable save, Warp Strike, and an aura that can stop enemies Falling Back.
- [[Lord Invocatus]] - A Juggernaut-riding hero whose weapon ignores invulnerable saves and who speeds up nearby CORE units.
- [[Angron]] - A huge Daemon Primarch who shreds anything in melee and can come back through Reborn in Blood.
- [[Jakhals]] - Cheap Objective Secured cultists that hold ground and feed the Blood Tithe.
