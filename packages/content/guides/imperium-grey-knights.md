## How this army plays

The Grey Knights are an elite army of psychic daemon hunters. Every Knight is a PSYKER in power or Terminator armour, armed with a storm bolter and a Nemesis force weapon. Almost the whole army can teleport onto the battlefield, so you decide where the fight happens.

Strengths:
- Teleporting. Most units can start off the table and drop in where they are needed.
- Psychic power. You cast a lot of powers each turn, including many Smites for steady mortal wounds.
- Good melee. Nemesis weapons have strong AP and 2 damage on many profiles.
- Nemesis Dreadknights are big, fast and tough, and they hit hard.

Weaknesses:
- Small model count. Each model is expensive, so losses hurt.
- Limited long-range anti-tank. You often have to get close to kill big targets.
- Missions that reward killing PSYKERS can give your opponent easy points.

What to expect: plan your deployment. Decide which units start on the table and which teleport in later. Learn to count your psychic powers each turn, because they do a lot of your damage.

## Army rules

- **Teleport Strike:** during deployment, a unit with this rule can wait in a teleportarium chamber. At the end of any of your Movement phases it arrives anywhere more than 9" from every enemy model.
- **Knights of Titan:** your units have And They Shall Know No Fear, Bolter Discipline and Masters of the Warp.
- **Masters of the Warp:** at the start of the first battle round, you choose which Tide of the Warp is dominant. Your PSYKER units gain its bonus. The Warp Shaping power changes the Tide later. In a Battle-forged army, this only works if every unit in your army has the ability. Servitors don't count. The Tides:
  - Convergence: longer range for psi weapons, and extra mortal wounds from Nemesis melee weapons on wound rolls of 6.
  - Celerity: Advance and charge dice of 1 or 2 count as 3.
  - Shadows: cover against long-range shooting.
  - Escalation: more of your units can cast their Brotherhood's power in the same phase.
  - Banishment: hurts enemy DAEMONS nearby.
- **The Aegis:** psykers in your Grey Knights detachments add 1 to Deny the Witch tests. Your units also ignore mortal wounds on a 5+.
- **Brotherhoods:** each detachment comes from one of 8 Brotherhoods, such as Swordbearers or Prescient Brethren. If every unit in it shares that Brotherhood, its psykers learn the Brotherhood's own power. It also unlocks that Brotherhood's Warlord Trait and Stratagem. HONOURED KNIGHTS, such as Paladins, Purifiers and Draigo, have no Brotherhood and don't break this.
- **Psychic disciplines:** characters use the Dominus discipline. Squads know a fixed Sanctic power, such as Hammerhand for Strike Squads.
- **Key stratagems:** *Truesilver Armour* makes unmodified wound rolls of 1-3 against a CORE INFANTRY unit fail. It costs 2CP for 5 or fewer models and 3CP for more. *Teleportation Shunt* (2CP) lifts a TELEPORTER unit off the table and sets it back up more than 9" from enemies, once per unit per battle. *Psybolt Ammunition* makes bolt weapons better against armour.

## Key units

- [[Grand Master in Nemesis Dreadknight]] - Your star leader. A fast, tough walker with a 4+ invulnerable save and a huge sword.
- [[Nemesis Dreadknight]] - The workhorse. Tough, CORE, and armed with heavy psycannons, psilencers or a greatsword.
- [[Strike Squad]] - Your Troops. Teleporting psykers with storm bolters and force weapons.
- [[Interceptor Squad]] - Fast Attack Knights with personal teleporters. Great for grabbing far objectives.
- [[Kaldor Draigo]] - The Supreme Grand Master. He gives a unit full hit re-rolls and has a 3+ invulnerable save.
- [[Purifier Squad]] - HONOURED KNIGHTS who burn enemies with Purifying Flame and incinerators.
- [[Brotherhood Librarian]] - A strong psyker with 2 casts a turn and access to both disciplines.
