## How this army plays

The Legion of the Damned is not really an army on its own. It is a single elite unit, the Damned Legionnaires: ghostly Space Marines who appear out of nowhere to help an IMPERIUM force in its darkest hour. Most players add one or two units of them to a Space Marine army, rather than building a whole list around them.

On the table, Legionnaires are a tough, flexible strike team. Every model has a 3+ armour save **and** a 3+ invulnerable save, so even the heaviest weapons struggle to remove them. They don't start on the battlefield: you hold them back and bring them in later, more than 9" from the enemy, wherever they are needed most. Their guns ignore the bonus that cover gives to enemy saves, so they are good at clearing enemy infantry out of ruins and woods.

Strengths:
- Very hard to kill for their points, thanks to the 3+ invulnerable save.
- Arrive where you need them, at the end of any of your Movement phases.
- Ignore cover with their shooting.

Weaknesses:
- Only one kind of unit, so on its own this faction can't fill a legal army.
- Each model has only 1 wound, so mortal wounds and lots of small attacks still hurt.
- The unit is expensive, so losing it costs you a large share of your points.

## Army rules

Damned Legionnaires carry the usual Space Marine rules (they have the ADEPTUS ASTARTES keyword), plus a few of their own:

- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** they ignore modifiers to their Combat Attrition tests.
- **Bolter Discipline and Shock Assault:** the standard Space Marine rules. Their Rapid Fire bolt weapons make double attacks in more situations than usual (for example, at any range if the unit didn't move), and each model gets 1 extra attack in a fight after it charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Aid Unlooked For:** they can be set up in reserve during deployment, then arrive at the end of any of your Movement phases more than 9" from every enemy model.
- **Flaming Projectiles:** enemy units don't get the cover bonus to their saves against this unit's shooting.
- **Unyielding Spectres:** every model has a 3+ invulnerable save.

> **Not confirmed:** the army data also carries an older rule, Saviours From Beyond, that let the unit join a Vanguard Detachment with no HQ. The final datasheet doesn't print it, so agree with your opponent before relying on it.

Because they are ADEPTUS ASTARTES, they can use the general Space Marine stratagems that don't need keywords they lack. Many of those stratagems need a CHARACTER, PRIMARIS or PHOBOS unit, which the Legion doesn't have, so read each one's requirements before you plan around it.

## Key units

- [[Damned Legionnaires]] - The whole faction: a 5 to 10-model squad with a 3+ invulnerable save that drops in from reserve and shoots enemies out of cover.
