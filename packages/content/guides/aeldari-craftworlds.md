The Asuryani of the craftworlds fight with a scalpel, not a hammer: almost every unit is a specialist that does one job very well.

## How this army plays

Craftworlds is a fast, elite army that mixes accurate shooting with sharp melee units. Each Aspect Warrior shrine has a narrow role: Dire Avengers pour out shuriken fire, Fire Dragons melt tanks, and Howling Banshees and Striking Scorpions cut down infantry. Farseers and Warlocks add psychic support, and grav-tanks such as the Wave Serpent carry your fragile infantry around.

**Strengths.** Speed is everywhere: jetbikes, skimmers and Battle Focus let you strike and then step back out of sight. Lightning Fast Reactions and the Strands of Fate dice help you survive a bad turn, and you have a specialist for every kind of target.

**Weaknesses.** Most infantry are Toughness 3 with 1 wound, and every model is expensive. You will be outnumbered, and a positioning mistake often costs a whole unit.

**What to expect.** Plan each turn: which unit kills which target, and where it hides afterwards. Use terrain and transports to stay out of sight until you strike, and trade units only for something worth more.

## Army rules

**Battle Focus.** A unit that Advanced can still shoot its Assault and Pistol weapons, counting as having Remained Stationary for those attacks. Or, if it didn't Advance or Fall Back, then after shooting in your Shooting phase it can make a Normal Move of D6". It can't embark at the end of that move or charge that turn, can't do it the turn it arrived as Reinforcements, and gets only one per turn. Moving over Area Terrain makes the move 3" shorter.

**Strands of Fate.** If every unit is ASURYANI from the same craftworld (PHOENIX LORD, ANHRATHE and UNALIGNED units don't count), roll 6 dice at the start of each battle round and keep 4 in a Strike Force game (2, 3 or 5 in Combat Patrol, Incursion or Onslaught). Each kept result fixes one roll of a matching type that round: 1 an Advance roll, 2 a charge roll, 3 a Psychic test, 4 a hit roll, 5 a wound roll, 6 a saving throw. Spend the die before rolling and that roll (or one of its dice) counts as an unmodified 6.

**Shuriken weapons.** An unmodified wound roll of 6 improves the attack's AP by 2.

**Craftworld attributes.** Your craftworld gives its units an attribute. Ulthwé, the usual pick, lets a unit re-roll one wound roll each time it shoots or fights, adds 1 to a PSYKER's first Psychic test each turn, and gives its models a 6+ invulnerable save and a 5+ roll to ignore mortal wounds. Its Fate Reader Warlord trait keeps 1 extra Strands of Fate die while that Warlord is on the battlefield.

**Stratagems to know.** Fire and Fade (2 CP) lets a unit make a Normal Move of up to 7" after shooting; it then can't embark, charge or make a Battle Focus move that turn. Lightning Fast Reactions (1 CP) makes attacks against one of your non-MONSTER units -1 to hit for the phase. Webway Strike (1 CP for 1 INFANTRY or BIKER unit, 3 CP for 2), once per battle, holds units back to arrive more than 9" from enemy models.

## Key units

- [[Farseer]] - your key PSYKER: re-rolls Strands of Fate dice and unlocks Stratagems such as Unparalleled Mastery.
- [[Dire Avengers]] - dependable shuriken firepower; Avengers of Asuryan (2 CP) lets a unit below its Starting Strength shoot again.
- [[Rangers]] - cheap Troops whose long rifles can ignore Look Out, Sir.
- [[Howling Banshees]] - their masks stop Overwatch and Set to Defend, and enemies they charge fight after your units.
- [[Fire Dragons]] - short-ranged tank hunters; in the Fight phase, Fusion Charges (1 CP) gives one model a single attack that deals 2D3 mortal wounds to a VEHICLE if it hits.
- [[Wave Serpent]] - a fast transport whose shield gives a 5+ invulnerable save against shooting and makes wound rolls of 1-3 fail.
- [[Wraithlord]] - a Toughness 8 walker with 9 wounds that can hold an objective.
- [[Shroud Runners]] - fast scouts whose Wireweave Grenades inflict mortal wounds and slow an enemy unit.
