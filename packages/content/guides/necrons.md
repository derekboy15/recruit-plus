Necrons are ancient robot legions that refuse to stay dead.

## How this army plays

Necrons are a tough, mid-range army that wins by holding ground. Necron Warriors and Immortals sit on objectives, characters buff them, and destroyed models climb back to their feet. Destroyers, C'tan Shards and big vehicles deal with whatever threatens that line.

**Strengths.** Your Troops are very hard to shift, and the army has plenty of healing and resurrection. Skorpekh Destroyers and Canoptek Wraiths are excellent in melee, and command protocols let you plan your buffs for the whole game.

**Weaknesses.** Most units are slow and there are no psykers. Warriors only do real damage at short range, so you have to get close and stay there. Reanimation only helps a unit that survives: a squad wiped out in one go gets nothing back.

**What to expect.** Your first games are about positioning. Keep characters near the units they buff, decide your protocol order before deployment, and learn which enemy units can delete a whole squad at once.

## Army rules

**Reanimation Protocols.** When an enemy unit shoots or fights and kills some, but not all, of one of these units, the dead models try to get back up once the attacks are finished. Roll one D6 per wound the destroyed models had between them and pool every 5+. While the pool holds at least as many dice as a destroyed model's Wounds, bring that model back with full wounds and spend that many dice. Leftover dice are lost. These rolls can never be modified by more than +1 or -1.

**Living Metal.** At the start of your Command phase, each model with this rule regains 1 lost wound.

**Command Protocols.** If every unit is from one dynasty (ignoring DYNASTIC AGENT, C'TAN SHARD and UNALIGNED units) and your WARLORD is a NECRONS CHARACTER, you secretly give a different protocol to each of battle rounds 1 to 5 after deployment. At the start of each round, if a NECRONS CHARACTER from your army is on the battlefield, that round's protocol activates: reveal it and pick one of its two directives for every eligible unit. The protocol you didn't assign is also active every round. If it is the one named in your dynasty's code, you get both of its directives.

**Dynastic Codes.** In a NECRONS Detachment, Troops gain Objective Secured and <DYNASTY> units gain their dynasty's code. Nihilakh, for example, gives its units Objective Secured, while Novokh adds 1 to charge rolls and improves melee AP by 1 in a turn the unit charged, was charged or Heroically Intervened. You can also build a custom dynasty.

**Signature stratagems.** Techno-oracular Targeting (1 CP) makes one shooting attack wound automatically. Dimensional Corridor (1 CP), used at the start of your Movement phase, removes a <DYNASTY> CORE INFANTRY unit and, in the Reinforcements step of that phase, sets it back up wholly within 3" of a friendly <DYNASTY> MONOLITH and more than 9" away from any enemy models. Resurrection Protocols (1 CP) gives a slain NECRONS INFANTRY NOBLE or CRYPTEK a 4+ roll to return with D3 wounds.

> **Tip:** Older 2022 lists often pair the Eternal Conquerors custom code with a Circumstance of Awakening. Since January 2023 that combination isn't allowed.

## Key units

- [[Necron Warriors]] - Objective Secured Troops that re-roll Reanimation Protocol rolls of 1 and are very hard to shift.
- [[Technomancer]] - each Command phase, returns a destroyed model to a nearby CORE unit (D3 models for Necron Warriors).
- [[Overlord]] - My Will Be Done gives a CORE unit within 9" +1 to hit, and the Overlord has a 4+ invulnerable save.
- [[Skorpekh Destroyers]] - fast, durable melee blenders that re-roll hit rolls of 1.
- [[Lokhust Heavy Destroyers]] - long-range anti-tank that can move without the Heavy weapon penalty.
- [[Canoptek Wraiths]] - a 4+ invulnerable save and moving through models and terrain make them great objective fighters.
- [[The Silent King]] - gives 3 extra Command points as your WARLORD plus re-roll auras for nearby CORE units.
