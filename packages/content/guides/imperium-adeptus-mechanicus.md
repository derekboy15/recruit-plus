## How this army plays

The Adeptus Mechanicus is a shooting army of cyborg soldiers, battle priests and walking machines. Your SKITARII do most of the work: good guns on cheap bodies, with Tech-Priests stacking buffs on top. Each battle round you pick a Doctrina Imperative and a Canticle, and choosing well is how you win.

Strengths:
- Lots of accurate shooting, with arc weapons that punish VEHICLES.
- Fast units that move up the board before the first turn.
- Every SKITARII and Kataphron model has a 5+ invulnerable save.

Weaknesses:
- Most models are fragile. Your foot Skitarii are Toughness 3.
- Many buffs only reach CORE units close to a character, so keep the army together.
- The keyword web (SKITARII, CULT MECHANICUS, CORE, DATA-TETHER) takes time to learn.

Expect slow first games while you learn which unit gets which buff. Plan your Imperatives for the whole battle before you deploy.

> **Tip:** older articles say Enriched Rounds works on hits of 5+ and Galvanic Volley Fire makes rifles Heavy 3. Those were temporary changes. The final rules are below.

## Army rules

**Doctrina Imperatives.** At the start of each battle round, if a DOCTRINA ASSEMBLER (a Tech-Priest or Skitarii Marshal) is on the battlefield, pick one Imperative for your SKITARII. Each can be used once per battle, and each has a bonus and a drawback:
- Protector: BS improves by 1, WS gets worse by 1.
- Conqueror: WS improves by 1, BS gets worse by 1.
- Bulwark: Save improves by 1 (best 2+), Move drops by 3".
- Aggressor: Move goes up by 3", Save gets worse by 1.

**Canticles of the Omnissiah.** Each battle round, pick one Canticle; each can be used once per battle. CULT MECHANICUS units (and SKITARII from Mars) get its effect:
- Shroudpsalm: the unit counts as being in Light Cover.
- Chant of the Remorseless Fist: +1 Strength on melee attacks.
- Incantation of the Iron Soul: ignore Combat Attrition modifiers, and ignore wounds lost to mortal wounds on a 5+.
- Benediction of the Omnissiah: when the unit shoots, re-roll one hit roll, one wound roll and one damage roll.
- Invocation of Machine Vengeance: roll an extra D6 for Advance and charge rolls and drop the lowest.
- Litany of the Electromancer: melee attacks against the unit get -1 to hit.

Both rules need your whole army to be ADEPTUS MECHANICUS, apart from a few allowed allies.

**Forge World dogmas.** A detachment shares one Forge World and its dogma:
- Mars: SKITARII gain Canticles; re-roll one hit roll when a unit shoots or fights.
- Lucius: +1 to armour saves against Damage 1 attacks; +3" weapon range.
- Agripinaa: better AP at half range; you can Hold Steady or Set to Defend when charged.
- Graia: auto-pass Combat Attrition; ignore wounds from mortal wounds on a 5+.
- Stygies VIII: Dense Cover against distant shooters; units you charge can't Hold Steady or Set to Defend.
- Ryza: +1 to charge rolls; +1 to wound in melee after charging, being charged or Heroically Intervening.
- Metalica: no hit penalty for Assault weapons after Advancing or Heavy weapons after moving.

**Key Stratagems.** Enriched Rounds makes radium weapons auto-wound non-VEHICLE targets on unmodified hits of 4+. Galvanic Volley Fire makes Rangers' rifles Rapid Fire 2.

## Key units

- [[Skitarii Marshal]] - Re-rolls wound rolls of 1 for nearby SKITARII CORE and can cancel an Imperative's drawback.
- [[Tech-Priest Manipulus]] - Gives one CORE unit +6" range and better AP on galvanic, arc and radium weapons.
- [[Skitarii Rangers]] - Long-ranged Troops that get Light Cover while they stay still.
- [[Serberys Raiders]] - Fast cavalry that move before the first turn and ignore Look Out, Sir.
- [[Sicarian Ruststalkers]] - Quick melee hunters that ignore Advance and charge modifiers.
- [[Kataphron Destroyers]] - Tough CORE heavy-weapon platforms.
- [[Ironstrider Ballistarii]] - Fast walkers with twin lascannons or autocannons.
- [[Belisarius Cawl]] - Your strongest HQ, with a re-roll aura for nearby ADEPTUS MECHANICUS CORE units.
