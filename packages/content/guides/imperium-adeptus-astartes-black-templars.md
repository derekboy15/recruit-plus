## How this army plays

Black Templars are the most aggressive Space Marine chapter. Your plan is to get across the table fast, crash into the enemy and win the fight. You use the whole Space Marine range except Librarians, which the chapter refuses to field.

Your strengths are early speed, durable melee units and some of the best melee characters in the game. Re-rolled charges mean your charges land more often than other armies'. Chaplains are the heart of the army, so plan to bring at least one.

Your weakness is shooting. You have the normal Marine guns, but a Templar army that hangs back and trades fire is wasting its rules. Expect opponents to screen your charges with cheap units.

Pick your vow for the enemy you face, push your Crusaders onto objectives, and hunt the enemy's leaders with your characters.

## Army rules

Your units have the usual **Angels of Death** rules. Combat Doctrines start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move on one step (Devastator → Tactical → Assault), but you never have to. Your Troops keep objectives they captured until the enemy takes them back.

**Righteous Zeal** is your Chapter Tactic. You can re-roll Advance and charge rolls for these units, and each time one of their models would lose a wound to a mortal wound, roll a D6: on a 5+ it is not lost.

**Templar Vows** replace a super-doctrine. If every unit in your army (other than UNALIGNED units) is BLACK TEMPLARS, at the end of the Read Mission Briefing step you can choose one vow for the whole battle. Each vow gives a benefit and a drawback called a Passion:

| Vow | Benefit | Passion |
|---|---|---|
| Suffer Not the Unclean to Live | Unmodified hit rolls of 6 in melee against non-VEHICLE units wound automatically | When you charge, you must include the closest enemy unit within 12" (not AIRCRAFT, and not already fighting your Templars) |
| Uphold the Honour of the Emperor | A 5+ invulnerable save, and wound rolls of 1-2 against the unit always fail | The unit never gets the benefit of cover |
| Abhor the Witch, Destroy the Witch | Once, in your first Movement phase, +3" Move if the enemy has psykers; re-roll wound rolls of 1 in melee against PSYKERS | No actions while an enemy psyker is within 18" |
| Accept Any Challenge, No Matter the Odds | Assault Doctrine while in Engagement Range, and +1 attack when fighting (not cumulative with Shock Assault) | The unit can't Fall Back |

> **Tip:** Uphold the Honour of the Emperor is the easy choice for a first game. A 5+ invulnerable save on everything, even tanks and Dreadnoughts, always helps.

Your Chaplains can use the **Litanies of the Devout** instead of the usual litanies. The favourite, Litany of Divine Protection, lets one nearby unit ignore lost wounds on a 5+.

Signature stratagems:

- **Devout Push** (1CP): at the start of the Fight phase, one CORE or CHARACTER unit moves up to 3" towards the closest enemy unit or objective marker, or piles in if already in combat.
- **Strength of Conviction** (1CP): in your Command phase, one CORE unit gains Objective Secured until your next Command phase.

## Key units

- [[High Marshal Helbrecht]] - a top melee character who adds 1 to the Strength of nearby BLACK TEMPLARS CORE units.
- [[Chaplain Grimaldus]] - a Chaplain who can stand back up once, guarded by Cenobyte Servitors.
- [[The Emperor's Champion]] - a cheap character-hunter.
- [[Primaris Crusader Squad]] - large, cheap Troops that hold objectives and fight well.
- [[Crusader Squad]] - firstborn Troops mixing tough Initiates with cheap Neophytes.
- [[Primaris Sword Brethren Squad]] - melee veterans who ignore your vow's Passion.
- [[Land Raider Crusader]] - a heavy assault transport for your best fighters.
