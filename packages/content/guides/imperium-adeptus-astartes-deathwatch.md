## How this army plays

The Deathwatch are alien hunters: veterans drawn from many chapters and formed into small, specialised **Kill Teams**. You use most of the Space Marine range, but your signature units are Kill Teams that mix different kinds of Marines in one squad and count as Troops.

Your strengths are flexibility and precision. Your bolt weapons fire special ammunition chosen for each target, and you choose your Combat Doctrine each battle round rather than following the usual order. Your Chapter Tactic can be aimed at the type of unit the enemy relies on most. Against xenos armies, several stratagems give you extra tools.

Your weakness is complexity. Mixed squads, ammunition choices and doctrine picks give you a lot to track, and a mistake costs you. Your units are elite and expensive, so you have few of them.

Expect a careful, thinking game. Plan your doctrine for each round, pick the right ammunition every time you shoot, and use Kill Teams to put tough, Objective Secured squads on the objectives.

## Army rules

Your units have the usual **Angels of Death** rules from Codex: Space Marines. Your Troops keep objectives they captured until the enemy takes them back.

**Xenos Hunters** is your Chapter Tactic. Melee attacks against TYRANIDS, AELDARI, ORK, NECRONS or T'AU EMPIRE units re-roll hit rolls of 1. After both armies are deployed, you also pick one Battlefield Role: for the rest of the battle, attacks against enemy units with that role re-roll wound rolls of 1.

**Mission Tactics** replaces the usual doctrine order. If every unit in your army (other than UNALIGNED units) is DEATHWATCH, you pick the Devastator, Tactical or Assault Doctrine at the start of each battle round, and it stays active until the end of that round.

> **Tip:** The printed rule limits how often you can pick each doctrine. The final Balance Dataslate (April 2023) removed those limits, so you can pick any of the three every round.

**Special-issue Ammunition** is on Deathwatch bolt weapons. Each time the unit shoots, each such weapon can pick one ammunition type for the phase:

| Ammunition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Dragonfire Bolts | The target gets no benefit of cover |
| Hellfire Rounds | +1 to wound against units that aren't VEHICLE or TITANIC |
| Kraken Bolts | +6" range and AP improved by 1 (adds to Combat Doctrines) |
| Vengeance Rounds | +1 Damage |

**Mixed Unit:** if a unit has models with different Toughness, attacks against it use the Toughness of the majority. If there is a tie, you choose between the tied values.

**Kill Teams** come in several types: Proteus (veterans, bikers, jump packs and Terminators), Fortis (Intercessors), Indomitor (Gravis) and Spectrus (Phobos). Each model keeps its own wargear and most of its abilities, but the whole squad is one Troops unit with Objective Secured.

Signature stratagems:

- **Priority Doctrine Adoption** (1CP): in an all-DEATHWATCH army, one unit uses the doctrine of your choice until your next Command phase.
- **Brotherhood of Veterans** (2CP): in your Command phase, one unit swaps Xenos Hunters for another Chapter Tactic or Successor Tactic until the end of the turn.
- **Teleportarium** (1CP): during deployment, one INFANTRY, DREADNOUGHT or BIKER unit gains Teleport Strike.

## Key units

- [[Watch Master]] - your leader, letting one unit re-roll all its hit rolls.
- [[Watch Captain Artemis]] - a tough named Captain with a deadly flamer.
- [[Proteus Kill Team]] - the most flexible Kill Team, able to mix veterans, bikers, jump packs and Terminators.
- [[Indomitor Kill Team]] - tough Gravis Troops that can bring Eradicators or Inceptors.
- [[Spectrus Kill Team]] - Phobos scouts; while it has Infiltrators, enemy reinforcements can't arrive within 12" of it.
- [[Fortis Kill Team]] - Intercessor-based Troops that can include Hellblasters or Outriders.
- [[Deathwatch Veterans]] - versatile veterans with special weapons and ammunition.
