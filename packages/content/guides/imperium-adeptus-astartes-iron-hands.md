## How this army plays

Iron Hands are the machine-loving gunline of the Space Marines. Your army leans on Dreadnoughts, tanks and heavy weapons, backed by Techmarines who repair them. Every unit shrugs off some damage, and vehicles keep fighting at full strength for longer than they should.

You will usually stay in Devastator Doctrine all game. That keeps your super-doctrine switched on, so your Heavy weapons can move and fire without a penalty and re-roll hit rolls of 1. Crucially this works for vehicles that are not CORE, which most Marine buffs can't reach. Late in the edition, Iron Hands and their successors were among the most successful Marine armies in tournaments.

Expect a slow, grinding game. You advance steadily, trade shots well and soak up return fire. The weakness is mobility and melee: your best units are not fast, and an opponent who swarms objectives or ties up your guns in combat can outpace you. Keep a few cheap infantry units to hold and screen objectives.

## Army rules

### Shared Space Marine rules

Almost every datasheet has **Angels of Death**:

- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** ignore modifiers to Combat Attrition tests.
- **Bolter Discipline:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire double shots at targets within half range, when the firer is INFANTRY (not Centurions) whose unit stayed still, or when the firer is a TERMINATOR or BIKER.
- **Shock Assault:** +1 Attack in a turn your unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Combat Doctrines:** if every unit in your army is ADEPTUS ASTARTES, the active doctrine improves AP by 1: Devastator for Heavy and Grenade weapons, Tactical for Rapid Fire and Assault weapons, Assault for Pistols and melee. You start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move one step on, but you never have to.

Troops have Objective Secured, and an objective they control at the end of your Command phase stays yours until your opponent takes it.

### Iron Hands rules

- **The Flesh is Weak (Chapter Tactic):** each time a model would lose a wound, it ignores it on a 6+. Models with a damage table count as having double their remaining wounds when you check which row to use.
- **Calculated Fury (super-doctrine):** in Devastator Doctrine, Heavy weapons suffer no penalty for moving, and they re-roll hit rolls of 1.

Signature stratagems:

- **March of the Ancients:** once per battle, one IRON HANDS DREADNOUGHT gains the CHARACTER keyword and +1 Attacks and Leadership, after you have picked your Warlord, so it can carry a relic.
- **Wrathful Machine Spirit:** a VEHICLE model re-rolls its hit rolls for a phase.
- **Methodical Firepower:** when Devastator Doctrine is not active, one unit also gets its bonus until your next Movement phase.
- **Reject the Flesh, Embrace the Machine:** an INFANTRY unit ignores lost wounds on a 5+ for a phase.
- **Machine Empathy:** a Techmarine repairs a second time.
- **Engine Purge:** once per battle, at the start of your Movement phase while the Devastator Doctrine is active: until the next battle round, unmodified wound rolls of 6 with Heavy and Grenade weapons get an extra 1 AP.

> **Tip:** The **Armour of Contempt** stratagem is not the old army-wide AP rule of that name, which ended in January 2023. The stratagem lets an ADEPTUS ASTARTES VEHICLE ignore wounds from mortal wounds on a 5+ for a phase.

## Key units

- [[Iron Father Feirros]] - A tough Techmarine who repairs vehicles and gives nearby INFANTRY a 5+ invulnerable save.
- [[Primaris Techmarine]] - A cheaper repair character, often your Warlord in vehicle-heavy lists.
- [[Redemptor Dreadnought]] - A durable heavy gun platform that loves The Flesh is Weak and Calculated Fury.
- [[Contemptor Dreadnought]] - A classic choice for March of the Ancients.
- [[Gladiator Lancer]] - A tank hunter whose laser destroyer gets +1 to hit.
- [[Desolation Squad]] - Infantry with heavy rocket launchers that stay deadly in Devastator Doctrine.
- [[Infiltrator Squad]] - Troops that hold objectives and keep enemy reinforcements from arriving within 12" of them.
