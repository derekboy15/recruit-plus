## How this army plays

The Sisters of Battle are human soldiers in power armour, carried by faith. They fight at short range with bolters, flamers and meltas, and they back that up with fierce melee units. Their signature tool is the pool of **Miracle dice**: dice you bank during the game and swap in for your own rolls at the moment that matters most.

Strengths:
- Lots of short-range firepower. Melta and flamer squads punish anything that comes close.
- Hard-hitting melee from Repentia, Zephyrim, Paragon Warsuits and Morvenn Vahl.
- Good at scoring. Cheap Troops and the Dogmata help you hold objectives and do actions.

Weaknesses:
- Most Sisters are T3 with 1 wound. They die fast to massed shooting.
- Most of your guns are short-ranged, so you have to get close.
- There is a lot to track each turn, and it is easy to waste Miracle dice.

What to expect: your first games are about getting into range in one piece. Ride in Rhinos and Immolators, and save a Miracle die for the roll that decides a fight.

## Army rules

- **Shield of Faith:** your units have a 6+ invulnerable save. Each one can also try to deny 1 psychic power in your opponent's Psychic phase. It rolls 1 die instead of 2. An unmodified 6 always denies, or a roll higher than the cast.
- **Acts of Faith:** you gain 1 Miracle die at the start of every turn, yours and your opponent's. You gain more when your units destroy enemy units and when your characters die. Once per phase, 1 of your units can perform an Act of Faith. It replaces one of its rolls with a Miracle die from your pool: an Advance, charge, hit, wound, damage or saving throw roll, or a Morale test. A Simulacrum Imperialis lets its unit perform one even if another unit already did this phase.
- **Sacred Rites:** after the mission is chosen, either pick 1 rite for the whole army, or roll for 2 different rites. Each lasts all game:
  1. Hand of the Emperor: +1 to Advance and charge rolls.
  2. Spirit of the Martyr: when a model dies in melee, roll a D6. On a 6 the attacking unit suffers 1 mortal wound.
  3. Aegis of the Emperor: Shield of Faith denies on an unmodified 5+.
  4. Divine Guidance: unmodified wound rolls of 6 with ranged attacks get 1 better AP.
  5. The Passion: unmodified hit rolls of 6 in melee score 1 extra hit.
  6. Light of the Emperor: ignore modifiers to Leadership and to Combat Attrition tests.
- **Orders:** each detachment picks an Order, such as Our Martyred Lady, Bloody Rose or Valorous Heart. If every Order unit in it shares that Order, those units gain its **Conviction**. The detachment also unlocks that Order's Warlord Trait, Relic and Stratagem.
- **Hymns of Battle:** PRIESTS chant hymns in your Command phase. The War Hymn gives 1 nearby unit +1 Attack.
- **Key stratagems:** *Battle Rites* (1CP, once per battle) swaps a Sacred Rite. *Defenders of the Faith* (2CP) makes a Battle Sisters Squad on an objective shoot more and shrug off weak wounds. *Desperate for Redemption* (2CP) lets slain Repentia fight before they are removed.

> **Tip:** older guides say you gain a Miracle die once per battle round. A 2022 update changed that to once per turn.

## Key units

- [[Morvenn Vahl]] - Your Warlord. A walker that halves incoming damage and makes nearby Sisters re-roll 1s to hit and wound.
- [[Battle Sister Squad]] - Your backbone. Troops with bolters and special weapons.
- [[Sisters Novitiate Squad]] - Cheap Troops for screens and actions.
- [[Dogmata]] - A PRIEST who chants hymns, lets a unit shoot while doing an action, and gives a unit Objective Secured.
- [[Sisters Repentia]] - Glass-cannon melee. They hit very hard and ignore wounds on a 5+.
- [[Paragon Warsuits]] - Tough walkers that reduce incoming damage by 1 and fight well up close.
- [[Retributor Squad]] - Heavy weapons that ignore cover. Multi-meltas make them your main anti-tank unit.
