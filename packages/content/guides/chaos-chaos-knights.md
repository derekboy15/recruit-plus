## How this army plays

This is one of the lowest model counts in the game: usually a handful of War Dogs, often with one or two big Knights. Every model is a Lord of War with a lot of wounds, so small arms barely scratch you.

**Strengths.** Your models are fast, tough and hit hard. Each Knight counts as several models on objectives, so one War Dog can hold a point against a whole squad. Every model is its own unit, so you can spread across the table.

**Weaknesses.** With so few bodies, every action costs you firepower and every loss hurts. Heavy anti-tank armies are your worst matchup, and cheap screens and dense terrain slow you down.

**For a new player** it is simple to command but unforgiving: a War Dog left in front of a melta gun is a heavy loss. In the final state of 9th Edition the best lists leaned on War Dogs (often House Herpetrax) backed by an Abominant or a Desecrator, and the faction won about half its games.

## Army rules

**Knight basics.** Every Knight has Ion Shields, a 5+ invulnerable save against ranged attacks. Big Knights are Super-heavy Walkers: they can charge after Falling Back, and when they make a Normal Move, Advance or Fall Back they can move over models that are not MONSTER or VEHICLE models.

**Towering Foe.** For objective control each model counts as 5 models, or 10 if it is TITANIC.

**War Dog Squadron.** When a squadron is first set up, each model must be within 6" of another model from it. After that, each War Dog is a separate unit. War Dogs also have Objective Secured.

**Harbingers of Dread.** If every unit in your army is a CHAOS KNIGHTS unit (ignoring AGENT OF CHAOS and UNALIGNED units), you choose one Harbinger ability at the start of each battle round from three columns: Despair, Doom and Darkness. Most weaken enemies within your Knights' Dread range.

**Traitoris Ambitions.** A detachment whose units share one allegiance gets a bonus:
- **Iconoclast, Conquerors Without Mercy:** when the Knight fights in a turn it charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention, it gets +1 Attack and its melee weapons improve their Armour Penetration by 1.
- **Infernal, Daemonic Surge:** in each of your Command phases, each Knight can take 1 mortal wound and roll a D3 on its surge table, or take D3 mortal wounds and pick the result. It lasts until your next Command phase.

**Households and Favours.** Each Dread Household adds a Bond, a Warlord Trait, a Relic and a Stratagem. Chosen by the Gods (1 CP) gives a Knight a Chaos god keyword so it can take a Favour of the Dark Gods.

**Signature stratagems.**
- **Diabolic Bulwark (1 CP for a War Dog, 2 CP otherwise):** when a Knight is targeted in your opponent's Shooting phase, it gets a 4+ invulnerable save against ranged attacks for the rest of that phase.
- **Pack Hunters (1 CP):** pick an enemy unit within Engagement Range of 2 or more of your War Dogs; those War Dogs get +1 Attack this Fight phase.
- **Doom and Despair (2 CP):** once per battle, in battle round 3, 4 or 5, switch on an earlier Harbinger ability for your whole army while your WARLORD is on the battlefield.

## Key units

- [[War Dog Karnivore Squadron]] - The fastest and cheapest War Dog, and the best in melee. It grabs objectives and kills what stands on them.
- [[War Dog Executioner Squadron]] - Two autocannons and a meltagun give steady shooting from a model that still holds objectives.
- [[War Dog Stalker Squadron]] - A flexible War Dog mixing a gun and a melee weapon.
- [[Knight Desecrator]] - Its laser destructor kills tanks from across the table, and its Taskmaster aura helps nearby War Dogs shoot.
- [[Knight Abominant]] - A PSYKER Knight that adds psychic powers to the army.
- [[Knight Rampager]] - A melee Knight whose Frenzied Rampage aura helps nearby War Dogs in combat.
