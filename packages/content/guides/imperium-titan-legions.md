## How this army plays

Titan Legions field the biggest war machines in the Imperium: Battle Titans, walking gun platforms many times the size of a tank. There are only four datasheets, all of them TITANIC Lords of War: the Warhound, Reaver, Warbringer Nemesis and Warlord Titans. They come from Forge World's Imperial Armour range, and there is no codex.

Strengths:
- Enormous firepower. Titan weapons have huge range, high Strength and high Damage, and several can hit targets out of sight.
- Huge wound totals, from 50 on a Warhound to 120 on a Warlord, plus void shields against shooting.
- Titans walk over enemy infantry and can still charge after Falling Back.

Weaknesses:
- A Titan costs a large share of your points, so you have very few models to hold objectives and do actions.
- Mortal wounds go straight through void shields.
- Big bases are hard to move through terrain on a normal-sized table.
- Titans were legal but rarely practical in standard-sized games. They shine in big games and Apocalypse-style battles.

Expect to use a Titan as the centrepiece of a larger Imperium force, or in a big narrative game, rather than as a whole army on its own. Check your mission pack's army-building rules, because Lords of War have their own detachment limits.

## Army rules

This catalogue has no army-wide rules or Stratagems of its own. Its rules are on the Titan datasheets.

**Void Shields.** Each Titan has a number of void shields: 2 on a Warhound, 4 on a Reaver, 6 on a Warbringer Nemesis and 8 on a Warlord. Each shield has 3 shield points.
- While the Titan has any void shields, it has a 5+ invulnerable save against ranged attacks.
- When you fail a save against a ranged attack while a shield is up, the Titan takes no damage. Instead, each point of damage removes 1 shield point.
- Once a shield has lost a point, it keeps taking the damage until it collapses. When it collapses, the rest of that attack's damage is lost.
- Shields never stop mortal wounds. Each one costs the Titan a wound as normal.
- At the start of your Command phase, each shield with fewer than 3 points goes back to 3.

**Battle Titan.** The Warhound, Reaver and Warbringer Nemesis can declare a charge in a turn they Fell Back. When they move, they can pass over models that aren't MONSTERS or VEHICLES, and can move through Engagement Range, but can't end the move there.

**War Engine.** The Warlord Titan has the same charge rule, but it can move over any models except TITANIC ones.

**Flank Speed.** When a Warhound Titan Advances, don't roll. It adds 12" to its Move instead.

**Reactor Meltdown.** When a Titan is destroyed, roll a D6 before removing it. On a 6+ it explodes. Each unit within 3D6" suffers 2D6 mortal wounds, or within 4D6" suffers 3D6 mortal wounds for a Warlord Titan. Keep your own units clear of a badly damaged Titan.

**Damage tables.** As a Titan loses wounds, its Move, Weapon Skill and Ballistic Skill get worse in four steps.

## Key units

- [[Warhound Titan]] - The smallest and fastest Titan, with Flank Speed and 2 void shields.
- [[Reaver Titan]] - The mid-sized Battle Titan, with 4 void shields and a heavy weapon on each arm.
- [[Warbringer Nemesis Titan]] - An artillery Titan with a quake cannon that can hit targets it can't see.
- [[Warlord Titan]] - The biggest Titan, with 120 wounds, 8 void shields and the biggest guns of any Titan.
