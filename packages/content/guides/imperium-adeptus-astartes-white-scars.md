## How this army plays

White Scars are the fast-attack chapter of the Space Marines. Bikes, jump packs and transports carry you across the table, and your Chapter Tactic lets units charge even after they Advance or Fall Back.

Your super-doctrine is one of the best in the game: in Assault Doctrine, melee attacks from units that charged or made a Heroic Intervention get +1 Damage. That turns lightning claws and chainswords into real threats against tough targets. Because doctrines only move one step per battle round, the earliest you can reach Assault Doctrine is battle round 3. Use the first two rounds to take objectives, set up charges and pick off targets with Assault weapons, which you can fire after Advancing without a penalty.

Expect a fast, high-risk game. You choose where the fights happen, but your units are spread out and often isolated. The weakness is staying power: once your fast units are stuck in a bad fight or shot off the table, you have little in reserve. Pick your charges carefully.

## Army rules

### Shared Space Marine rules

Almost every datasheet has **Angels of Death**:

- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** ignore modifiers to Combat Attrition tests.
- **Bolter Discipline:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire double shots at targets within half range, when the firer is INFANTRY (not Centurions) whose unit stayed still, or when the firer is a TERMINATOR or BIKER.
- **Shock Assault:** +1 Attack in a turn your unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Combat Doctrines:** if every unit in your army is ADEPTUS ASTARTES, the active doctrine improves AP by 1: Devastator for Heavy and Grenade weapons, Tactical for Rapid Fire and Assault weapons, Assault for Pistols and melee. You start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move one step on, but you never have to.

Troops have Objective Secured, and an objective they control at the end of your Command phase stays yours until your opponent takes it.

### White Scars rules

- **Lightning Assault (Chapter Tactic):** your units can charge in a turn they Advanced or Fell Back, and Assault weapons have no hit penalty after Advancing.
- **Devastating Charge (super-doctrine):** in Assault Doctrine, melee attacks from a unit that charged or made a Heroic Intervention this turn get +1 Damage.

Signature stratagems:

- **Encirclement:** set up a unit in outflank, then bring it in at the end of one of your Movement phases wholly within 6" of a battlefield edge and more than 9" from enemy models.
- **Hunters' Fusillade:** after a unit Advances, its Heavy and Rapid Fire weapons count as Assault weapons until the end of the turn.
- **Born in the Saddle:** a BIKER unit that Advances can still shoot.
- **Ride Hard, Ride Fast:** after a unit Advances, ranged attacks against it are -1 to hit until your next Movement phase.
- **Strike for the Heart:** a unit consolidates D3+3" instead of 3", or D6+6" if every model has a Move of 10" or more.
- **The Eternal Hunt:** once per battle, at the start of your Movement phase while the Assault Doctrine is active: until the next battle round, unmodified wound rolls of 6 with Pistols and melee weapons get an extra 1 AP.

## Key units

- [[Kor'sarro Khan]] - Gives nearby CORE and CHARACTER units +1 to wound in melee on the turn they charge or intervene.
- [[Khan on Bike]] - A fast biker Captain who keeps up with your bikes and leads from the front.
- [[Outrider Squad]] - Fast Primaris bikes. Their datasheet ability, also called Devastating Charge, gives +2 Attacks after charging and is separate from the super-doctrine.
- [[Attack Bike Squad]] - Quick multi-melta platforms for hunting vehicles.
- [[Vanguard Veteran Squad]] - Jump pack melee, often with lightning claws, that hits hard under the super-doctrine.
- [[Bladeguard Veteran Squad]] - Tough melee infantry with storm shields for the turns you need to hold a fight.
- [[Assault Intercessor Squad]] - Melee Troops that hold objectives and still threaten charges.
