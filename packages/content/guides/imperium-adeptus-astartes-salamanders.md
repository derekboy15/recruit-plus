## How this army plays

Salamanders are the close-range specialists of the Space Marines. Flamers and melta weapons are your signature, and almost everything in the army makes them better. You want to get close, burn through infantry and melt tanks.

Your Chapter Tactic does two jobs. Every time a unit shoots or fights it can re-roll one wound roll, which makes small units with big guns very reliable. It also makes your units tougher, because attacks with AP -1 hit them as if they had no AP at all. Your super-doctrine adds +1 to wound with flame and melta weapons in Tactical Doctrine, so you usually move into Tactical from battle round 2 and push forward.

Expect a mid-range brawl. You advance behind tough units, then unload short-ranged weapons at the right moment. The weakness is range: flamers and meltas need you to be close, and a long-range gunline can thin your army before you arrive. Use terrain and transports to get into position, and spend your stratagems on the turn you strike.

## Army rules

### Shared Space Marine rules

Almost every datasheet has **Angels of Death**:

- **And They Shall Know No Fear:** ignore modifiers to Combat Attrition tests.
- **Bolter Discipline:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire double shots at targets within half range, when the firer is INFANTRY (not Centurions) whose unit stayed still, or when the firer is a TERMINATOR or BIKER.
- **Shock Assault:** +1 Attack in a turn your unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Combat Doctrines:** if every unit in your army is ADEPTUS ASTARTES, the active doctrine improves AP by 1: Devastator for Heavy and Grenade weapons, Tactical for Rapid Fire and Assault weapons, Assault for Pistols and melee. You start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move one step on, but you never have to.

Troops have Objective Secured, and an objective they control at the end of your Command phase stays yours until your opponent takes it.

### Salamanders rules

- **Forged in Battle (Chapter Tactic):** each time your unit shoots or fights, you can re-roll one wound roll. Attacks with AP -1 against your units count as AP 0.
- **Promethean Cult (super-doctrine):** in Tactical Doctrine, +1 to wound with flame and melta weapons.

> **Tip:** From April 2022 to January 2023 the defensive half of Forged in Battle was different: it stopped enemies re-rolling wound rolls against you. The AP -1 version above is the final rule.

Signature stratagems:

- **Flamecraft:** a unit's flame weapons make their maximum number of attacks this phase.
- **The Fires of Battle:** a flame or melta weapon inflicts 1 mortal wound on each unmodified wound roll of 4+, up to 3 per phase.
- **The Crucible of Battle:** a CORE or CHARACTER unit gets +1 to wound when it shoots or fights.
- **Strength of the Primarch:** a unit gets +1 Strength in the Fight phase, and unmodified wound rolls of 6 in melee double the weapon's Damage.
- **Rise from the Ashes:** a destroyed CHARACTER comes back on a 4+ with 1 wound, once per model.
- **Rites of Vulkan:** once per battle, at the start of your Movement phase while the Tactical Doctrine is active: until the next battle round, unmodified wound rolls of 6 with Rapid Fire and Assault weapons get an extra 1 AP.

## Key units

- [[Vulkan He'stan]] - Each Command phase, one nearby CORE or CHARACTER unit re-rolls hit and wound rolls with flame and melta weapons.
- [[Adrax Agatone]] - A tough Captain whose aura gives nearby SALAMANDERS CORE units +1 to wound with melee attacks in a turn they charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- [[Bray'arth Ashmantle]] - A Dreadnought hero with heavy flamers and a 5+ invulnerable save.
- [[Eradicator Squad]] - Melta infantry that become brutal tank killers with Promethean Cult and Vulkan's re-rolls.
- [[Aggressor Squad]] - Flamestorm gauntlets turn them into a wall of flame against infantry.
- [[Attack Bike Squad]] - Fast multi-melta platforms that make good use of the Chapter Tactic's wound re-roll.
