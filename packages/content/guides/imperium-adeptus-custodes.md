## How this army plays

The Custodes are the Emperor's bodyguard: golden giants who are each worth several ordinary soldiers. You field very few models, but every one is tough, hits hard and fights well in melee and at range. The book also includes the **Sisters of Silence** (ANATHEMA PSYKANA), human warriors who are immune to psychic powers.

Strengths:
- Every model is durable. Most have a 4+ invulnerable save and several wounds.
- Strong in melee. Guardian spears, axes and blades cut through elite infantry.
- Each Custodes model counts double when you hold objectives.

Weaknesses:
- Few models. Losing even one unit hurts, and you can't cover the whole table.
- Swarms of cheap enemies and objective-heavy missions can stretch you thin.
- Mistakes are costly. One bad charge can throw away a large share of your points.

What to expect: pick your fights. Hold the centre with a few units, kill whatever comes at you, and use your command points on defensive stratagems to keep key units alive.

## Army rules

- **Aegis of the Emperor:** your INFANTRY and BIKERS have a 4+ invulnerable save. Each time one would lose a wound to a mortal wound, roll a D6. On a 6 it doesn't lose that wound.
- **Objectives:** each ADEPTUS CUSTODES model counts as 1 extra model for controlling objectives. Your units that are both CORE and INFANTRY have Objective Secured, and so do ANATHEMA PSYKANA Troops. INFANTRY characters without CORE, such as a Shield-Captain, don't get it.
- **Watchers of the Throne:** for Look Out, Sir, your Custodes INFANTRY and BIKER models count as 2 models when protecting characters from the same Shield Host.
- **From Golden Light:** many units can start in a teleportarium. They arrive in the Reinforcements step of one of your Movement phases, more than 9" from any enemy.
- **Shield Hosts:** if every unit in a detachment is from the same Shield Host, they gain its Fighting Style. Emperor's Chosen is the most common choice.
- **Martial Ka'tah:** in a pure army from one Shield Host, you choose 3 fighting forms, called ka'tahs, in an order. Each has 2 stances. In each Command phase you switch on 1 stance, moving forward through your order. You can never go back or repeat a stance.
- **Army limits:** you can take at most 1 of each kind of Shield-Captain (foot, Allarus and jetbike) per detachment. A detachment can't hold more Sisters of Silence HQ or Troops units than Custodes ones, and a Sister can't be your Warlord.
- **Daughters of the Abyss (Sisters of Silence):** psychic powers can't target or affect these units. An enemy PSYKER within 18" takes -1 to its Psychic tests for each such unit nearby, to a maximum of -3. Their attacks get +1 to wound against PSYKER or DAEMON units within 6".
- **Key stratagems:** *Arcane Genetic Alchemy* makes unmodified wound rolls of 1-3 against an INFANTRY unit fail. *The Emperor's Auspice* stops your opponent re-rolling hits, wounds and damage against one. Each costs 1CP for a small unit and 2CP for a big one. You can't use both on the same unit in the same phase.

## Key units

- [[Trajann Valoris]] - Your Warlord. A melee monster who lets nearby CORE units re-roll hit and wound rolls of 1.
- [[Shield-Captain on Dawneagle Jetbike]] - A fast, very tough leader who can hunt characters.
- [[Custodian Guard Squad]] - Troops with Objective Secured. With shields they are your best objective holders.
- [[Allarus Custodians]] - Terminator-armoured elites that can teleport in and hit hard.
- [[Venerable Contemptor Dreadnought]] - A durable walker that reduces damage and adds heavy firepower.
- [[Knight-Centura]] - A cheap Sisters of Silence leader whose Warlord Trait shuts down enemy Objective Secured nearby.
- [[Vertus Praetors]] - Jetbikes that move fast and bring salvo launchers or hurricane bolters.
