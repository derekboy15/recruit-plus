Monsters and Gribblies is a small collection of odd creatures and ancient machines, such as the tunnelling Ambull and the robotic Blackstone Fortress constructs. It isn't really an army of its own.

## How this army plays

There are only 5 datasheets here, with no army rules, Stratagems or faction bonuses. Think of them as extras. Every unit is UNALIGNED, so including them doesn't break the rule that all units in your army share a Faction keyword. They still need a slot in a detachment: the Spindle Drones are Troops and the rest are Elites.

**Strengths.** Each unit does something unusual. The Ambull and Borewyrms can burrow away and pop up elsewhere, and the Guardian Drone and Spindle Drones hit surprisingly hard together.

**Weaknesses.** These units get none of your faction's rules, auras or Stratagems, and there are too few of them to build a full army around. Each one is a bit of a novelty.

**What to expect.** These datasheets shine in narrative and casual games, or when you want to field a model you already own. Use them as distractions and flank threats rather than the heart of your plan.

## Army rules

There are no army-wide rules. These are the datasheet abilities worth knowing:

- **Rapid Tunnelling** (Ambull, Borewyrm Infestation): at the start of your Movement phase you can remove the model from the battlefield. At the end of your next Movement phase, set it up anywhere more than 9" away from all enemy models.
- **Rad-maggot Symbiosis** (Ambull): this model regains up to D3 lost wounds at the start of each battle round.
- **Hard to Hit** (Borewyrm Infestation): ranged attacks against the unit are -1 to hit.
- **Threat Level Rising** (Spindle Drones): each drone pulse gets +1 to its Strength, AP and Damage for every model in the unit that has lost wounds or been destroyed. The more you hurt them, the harder they hit.
- **Drone Commander** (Guardian Drone): a Spindle Drone unit within 6" ignores Threat Level Rising and instead adds 3 to its drone pulse's Strength, AP and Damage.
- **Hover Drones** (Guardian Drone): roll a D6 each time this model would lose a wound; on a 5+ it isn't lost.
- **Disruptive Influence** (The Archivist): every unit within 3" of this model, friend or foe, has -1 Leadership.

## Key units

- [[Ambull]] - a Toughness 6 MONSTER with 7 wounds and AP -3 claws that tunnels across the table and heals each round.
- [[Borewyrm Infestations]] - cheap units of 1-2 SWARM models that are hard to hit and can tunnel onto objectives.
- [[Spindle Drones]] - a unit of 4 drones (1 per army) whose drone pulses get deadlier as the unit takes damage.
- [[Guardian Drone]] - a tough MONSTER that boosts nearby Spindle Drones and fires an 18" destroyer pulse at Strength 9, AP -3, Damage 4.
- [[The Archivist]] - a CHARACTER MONSTER whose atomic disassembler ignores cover; it can be your WARLORD.
