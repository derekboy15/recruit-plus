## How this army plays

The Fallen are renegade Dark Angels. In 9th Edition they are a tiny faction with just 4 datasheets: Cypher, the Sorcerer, the Fallen squad and the Chaos Rhino. You will normally field them as a small extra detachment rather than as a whole army.

The Fallen squad is veteran power-armoured infantry with a wide choice of special, heavy and melee weapons. Their army ability rewards standing still: units that didn't move re-roll hit rolls of 1 when they shoot. That makes a Fallen squad a good fire base in terrain, ideally with Cypher close by.

Cypher is the star: a slippery pistol fighter who can fire his pistols even after Advancing or Falling Back and shuts down your opponent's Command Point gains.

The weakness is depth. Fallen squad models have only 1 wound each, the Rhino is your only vehicle, and you have only 3 stratagems of your own. Expect to play a supporting role.

## Army rules

### Building a Fallen detachment

The **Fallen Angels** Requisition stratagem (1 CP, before the battle) lets you include one Fallen Angels Specialist Detachment. It is a Vanguard Detachment made only of FALLEN units, Sorcerers and Chaos Rhinos, and every unit in it gains the FALLEN ANGELS keyword.

- Its Sorcerers and Chaos Rhinos swap their <MARK OF CHAOS>, HERETIC ASTARTES and <LEGION> keywords for IMPERIUM and FALLEN. Sorcerers also swap Death to the False Emperor for the Fallen Angels ability.
- Its Chaos Rhinos carry up to 10 FALLEN INFANTRY models, but no JUMP PACK models.

Cypher's **Enigmatic Ally** rule also lets him join a CHAOS SPACE MARINES Detachment that includes a CHAOS LORD without using up a Battlefield Role slot.

### Unit rules

- **Fallen Angels:** a FALLEN unit that didn't move in its last Movement phase re-rolls hit rolls of 1 when it shoots, Overwatch included. A FALLEN unit never loses more than 1 model to a single failed Morale test.
- **Hateful Assault:** +1 Attack in a turn your unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention.
- **Malicious Volleys:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire double shots at targets within half range, when the firer is INFANTRY whose whole unit stayed still, or when the firer is a TERMINATOR, BIKER or HELBRUTE.

> **Tip:** **Let the Galaxy Burn** is also printed on these datasheets, but it only works if every unit in your army has the TRAITORIS ASTARTES keyword. None of these 4 datasheets has it.

### Cypher

- **Lord Cypher:** your FALLEN units within 6" re-roll hit rolls of 1.
- **Agent of Discord:** enemy units within range can't use abilities, Warlord Traits or Relics that give or refund Command Points. The range is 12", or 18" with 10–19 other FALLEN ANGELS models within 12" of Cypher, or 24" with 20 or more.
- **Mysterious Protection:** a 4+ invulnerable save. If he is slain, on a 2+ he doesn't count as slain for mission victory conditions.
- **Escape:** once per battle, at the end of a Shooting or Fight phase, move him into Strategic Reserves. He is destroyed if he isn't on the battlefield when the battle ends.

### Stratagems

- **Without a Trace:** at the start of your opponent's Shooting phase, attacks against one FALLEN ANGELS unit entirely on or within a terrain feature are -1 to hit.
- **Ancient Enmity:** in the Fight phase, a FALLEN ANGELS unit re-rolls wound rolls against DARK ANGELS units.

> **Not confirmed:** The catalogue gives no points value for the Fallen squad, and the library could not confirm its final 9th Edition points.

## Key units

- [[Cypher]] - Your leader: re-rolls for nearby Fallen, a 4+ invulnerable save and a CP-denial aura.
- [[Fallen]] - Veteran infantry with heavy and special weapons that shoot best from a standstill.
- [[Sorcerer]] - A psyker who can carry the Caliban Steel Blade relic.
- [[Chaos Rhino]] - Carries a full squad of 10 Fallen across the table.
