## How this army plays

Imperial Knights are giant walking war machines piloted by noble warriors. A whole army might be only 4 to 8 models: a few big Questoris or Dominus Knights and a handful of smaller Armigers. Every model is a VEHICLE with lots of wounds, heavy guns and strong melee.

Strengths:
- Very hard to kill with small arms. Most enemy weapons barely scratch a Knight.
- Big Knights can walk straight over enemy infantry and still charge after Falling Back.
- Knights count as several models each when holding objectives, so a few of them can take the middle of the board.
- Few models means a small, quick army to paint and move.

Weaknesses:
- Every model you lose is a big part of your army.
- You have few units, so you can't be everywhere at once, and actions and secondary objectives are hard to spread around.
- Opponents bring lots of anti-tank weapons against you.
- Big bases make moving through ruins and narrow gaps awkward.

Expect a game of careful positioning. Pair your big Knights with Armigers, keep them close enough to share buffs, and pick fights where your size wins.

## Army rules

**Code Chivalric.** If every unit in your army is an IMPERIAL KNIGHTS unit (apart from Agents of the Imperium and Unaligned units), and all but your FREEBLADES come from the same Noble Household, you swear 2 Oaths when you write your army list. The Oaths are Protect Those in Need, Defend the Realm, Refuse No Challenge and Lay Low the Tyrants. Each Oath has a Pledge, which usually gains you Honour, and a Troth, which usually loses it.

You start with 1 Honour point, and your total stays between 0 and 6. At the start of each battle round, check your total:
- 0 points: Dishonoured. Your Oaths give no bonuses.
- 1-4 points: Honoured. The Honoured ability of each Oath works for all your IMPERIAL KNIGHTS units.
- 5-6 points: Virtuous. Both the Honoured and Virtuous abilities work.

**Bondsman abilities.** In your Command phase, each Questoris Knight with a Bondsman ability can buff one ARMIGER-CLASS model within 12". For example, a Knight Errant's Armiger can charge after Advancing, and a Knight Crusader's Armiger gets better BS. While your army is Honoured or Virtuous, an Armiger under a Bondsman ability also takes 1 less damage from each attack (to a minimum of 1).

**Exalted Court.** Upgraded Knights gain extra abilities. Their Noble Exemplar ability only works while your army is Honoured or Virtuous. If the Knight has a Bondsman ability, it can use it one extra time in each of your Command phases.

**Unyielding Knight.** Each Knight counts as 5 models when you check who controls an objective marker. A TITANIC Knight counts as 10 models instead.

**Noble Households.** In a Battle-forged army, one detachment can't mix units from two different Households, but it can include any number of FREEBLADES.

**Ion Shields.** Your Knights have a 5+ invulnerable save against ranged attacks. The Rotate Ion Shields Stratagem raises this to a 4+ for a phase.

**Questor Mechanicus.** Calculated Targeting lets a QUESTOR MECHANICUS model turn unmodified wound rolls of 6 into mortal wounds equal to the weapon's Damage.

## Key units

- [[Knight Errant]] - Melee-ready Knight whose Bondsman ability lets an Armiger Advance and still charge.
- [[Knight Crusader]] - A gun platform carrying several heavy weapons.
- [[Knight Castellan]] - A Dominus-class Knight with long-range firepower, 28 wounds and a 2+ save.
- [[Knight Paladin]] - An all-rounder whose Bondsman ability gives an Armiger re-rolls of 1 to hit and wound.
- [[Armiger Warglaives]] - Fast melee Armigers with Objective Secured.
- [[Armiger Helverins]] - Long-ranged Armigers that shoot down light vehicles and infantry.
- [[Canis Rex]] - A legendary Freeblade who can re-roll one hit, wound, charge or saving throw each battle round.
