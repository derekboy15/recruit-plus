## How this army plays

Space Wolves are a fast, close-combat chapter with their own way of doing things. Alongside much of the Space Marine range you get unique units: Thunderwolf Cavalry, packs of Fenrisian Wolves, the half-feral Wulfen and the veteran Wolf Guard. The chapter does not use Apothecaries, Tactical, Assault, Devastator, Sternguard or Vanguard units.

Your strengths are melee and counter-attacks. Your rules make every charge hit more often, and any of your units can leap into a fight when the enemy charges nearby. Thunderwolf Cavalry and Wolf Guard with storm shields are fast, tough and deadly.

Your weakness is that your best tricks need you in combat. If the enemy can stay out of reach and shoot, you lose much of your edge. Many of your stratagems cost CP, so spend it carefully.

Expect an aggressive game where positioning matters in both turns. Push forward to threaten the enemy, and leave units close enough to counter-charge anything that attacks your lines.

## Army rules

Your units have the usual **Angels of Death** rules. Combat Doctrines start in Devastator. From battle round 2 you may move on one step (Devastator → Tactical → Assault), but you never have to. Your Troops keep objectives they captured until the enemy takes them back.

**Hunters Unleashed** is your Chapter Tactic. In a turn in which the unit charged, was charged or made a Heroic Intervention, its melee attacks get +1 to hit. Every unit with this tactic can also make Heroic Interventions as if it were a CHARACTER.

**Savage Fury** is your super-doctrine for an all-Space Wolves army. While the Assault Doctrine is active, each unmodified hit roll of 6 with a melee weapon scores 1 extra hit.

Some of your units have extra rules:

- **Berserk Charge** (Blood Claws and similar): +1 Attack for models other than WOLF GUARD when the unit fights after charging.
- **Headstrong:** a unit with no WOLF GUARD model must include the closest eligible enemy unit as a target when it charges.
- **Swift Hunters** (Thunderwolf Cavalry, wolves and wolf-mounted characters): the unit can charge after Advancing, and gets an extra 1" on pile-in and consolidation moves.
- **Savage Killers** (Wulfen): the Assault Doctrine always counts as active for their attacks, and they always count as having charged for Shock Assault.

**Sagas** are your Warlord Traits. Each one has a Deed, such as killing a CHARACTER in melee. When your character completes the Deed, it gains a Saga, an aura that helps nearby SPACE WOLVES CORE units for the rest of the battle.

> **Tip:** Bestial Nature lets you use Assault Doctrine bonuses in the early turns, when your army is still in Devastator or Tactical.

Signature stratagems:

- **Bestial Nature** (1CP): in your Command phase, one INFANTRY, CAVALRY or BIKER unit gets the Assault Doctrine bonus until your next Command phase.
- **Counter Charge** (1CP, 0CP for a CHARACTER): one unit can make a Heroic Intervention from up to 6" away, moving up to 6".
- **Savage Strike** (1 or 2CP): a unit that charged gets +1 to wound in melee for that fight.
- **Cunning of the Wolf** (1CP): during deployment, one INFANTRY unit gains Outflank.

## Key units

- [[Thunderwolf Cavalry]] - your signature unit: fast, tough cavalry that hits very hard.
- [[Wolf Guard]] - flexible veterans; with jump packs and storm shields they are a fast, tough strike force.
- [[Wolf Guard Terminators]] - a hard-to-kill Terminator squad with a wide choice of weapons.
- [[Wulfen]] - savage fighters who always use the Assault Doctrine and can fight back as they die.
- [[Logan Grimnar on Stormrider]] - your Chapter Master on a fast chariot that keeps pace with the cavalry.
- [[Njal Stormcaller]] - a powerful psyker with Tempestas powers.
- [[Blood Claws]] - cheap, aggressive Troops with extra attacks on the charge.
