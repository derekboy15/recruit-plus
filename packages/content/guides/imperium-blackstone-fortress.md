## How this army plays

This catalogue holds a small group of characters from the Blackstone Fortress board game, plus the fortress's own guardian robots. It is not a full army. There are only five datasheets:
- two Rogue Traders, Janus Draik and Neyam Shai Murad;
- the Navigator Espern Locarno;
- the robot UR-025;
- Spindle Drones, the fortress's guardian constructs.

The characters are skilled individuals with good saves and special tricks. None of them can win a game alone. Think of them as a fun way to use your Blackstone Fortress models in casual or narrative games, or as a small band of heroes beside a bigger Imperium force.

Strengths:
- Two characters with 4+ invulnerable saves.
- Useful one-off tricks, such as Janus Draik's hidden weapon and Neyam Shai Murad's ignore-cover shooting.
- Spindle Drones get more dangerous as they take damage.

Weaknesses:
- Very few units, and no Stratagems or army rules to tie them together.
- Not all of these models share the same Faction keywords. Spindle Drones are UNALIGNED, and the characters come from different parts of the Imperium.
- Each model is fragile next to a real army's elite units.

Expect to field one or two of these models in a larger game rather than building a whole army from this list.

## Army rules

This catalogue has no army-wide rules or Stratagems of its own. Its rules are on the datasheets.

**Warrant of Trade.** Janus Draik and Neyam Shai Murad can embark on any IMPERIUM TRANSPORT, even one that normally only carries other Factions. All other transport limits still apply.

**Janus Draik.** He has a 4+ invulnerable save and re-rolls hit rolls of 1 for his attacks. Once per battle, at the start of the Fight phase, you can pick one enemy model within 1" of him and roll a D6. On a 4+, that model suffers D3 mortal wounds.

**Neyam Shai Murad.** Enemy units get no cover bonus to their saves against her ranged attacks. In your Shooting phase, if she did not move in your previous Movement phase, her negotiator pistols become Pistol 4.

**Espern Locarno.** He is a PSYKER with a 4+ invulnerable save. When he tries to manifest Smite, first pick an enemy unit within 12" that he can see. If none is there, he can't attempt Smite this phase. If the power works, your chosen unit takes the mortal wounds even when another enemy unit is closer.

**UR-025.** At the start of each battle round, UR-025 regains 1 lost wound. Its power claw gets -1 to hit.

**Spindle Drones (Threat Level Rising).** For each model in the unit that has lost wounds or been destroyed, the drone pulses of the surviving models get +1 Strength, 1 better AP and +1 Damage.
