The Death Guard are Nurgle's plague-ridden Space Marines: slow, stubborn and very hard to shift.

## How this army plays

Death Guard win by walking onto objectives and refusing to leave. Plague Marines are T5 with 2 wounds, and most of the army shaves damage off every hit it takes. In exchange the army is slow, has little long-range anti-tank outside its tanks and daemon engines, and cannot race anyone to a far objective.

- **An elite, medium-sized army** of Plague Marines, Terminators and a few daemon engines. Poxwalkers add cheap bodies if you want a horde.
- **Forgiving.** A bad turn rarely wipes a unit, which makes Death Guard a good first army.
- **Slow.** Plague Marines move 5", so decide during deployment which objectives you will walk to.
- **Weak spots.** Mortal wounds ignore your damage reduction, and fast or psychic-heavy armies give you trouble.

By the end of the edition Death Guard were a solid mid-tier army, built around Terminator bricks, Plague Marines, Mortarion and Plagueburst Crawlers.

## Army rules

- **Disgustingly Resilient:** most Death Guard units subtract 1 from the Damage of each attack allocated to them, to a minimum of 1.
- **Contagions of Nurgle:** if every unit in your army is DEATH GUARD (UNALIGNED units don't count), your units gain *Nurgle's Gift*: enemy units within Contagion Range of them get -1 Toughness. Contagion Range grows each battle round: 1" in round 1, 3" in round 2, 6" in round 3 and 9" in round 4. Copies of the same Contagion don't stack, and rules that affect auras don't affect Contagions.
- **Inexorable Advance:** a unit that didn't Advance or Fall Back counts as having Remained Stationary. VEHICLES don't take the hit penalty for firing Heavy weapons at enemies in Engagement Range, and INFANTRY ignore every modifier to their Move, Advance rolls and charge rolls.
- **Plague weapons** re-roll wound rolls of 1. **Malicious Volleys:** Rapid Fire bolt weapons fire twice as many shots at a target within half range, from INFANTRY whose unit Remained Stationary, or from Terminators.
- **Plague Companies:** each detachment picks one company, which gives its own Contagion Warlord Trait and stratagem: the Harbingers, the Inexorable, Mortarion's Anvil, the Wretched, the Poxmongers, the Ferrymen or Mortarion's Chosen Sons.

Signature stratagems:

- *Cloud of Flies* (2 CP, or 4 CP for TERMINATORS): in your opponent's Shooting phase, enemy models can only shoot one BUBONIC ASTARTES INFANTRY unit if it is the closest eligible target or within 12". The unit loses any Bodyguard ability for that phase.
- *The Dead Walk Again* (1 CP): in your Command phase, roll seven D6 for a POXWALKERS unit. Each 3+ brings back one destroyed model with 1 wound. Once per unit per battle.
- *Disgusting Force* (1 CP): a PLAGUEBURST CRAWLER's mortar attacks become Damage 3. After it shoots, other units within 3" of one unit it hit (except NURGLE units) take 1 mortal wound on a 4+.
- *Creeping Blight* (1 CP): in the Fight phase, a DEATH GUARD unit's plague weapon attacks with an unmodified wound roll of 6 have AP -4.

## Key units

- [[Plague Marines]] - The backbone: Objective Secured, tough, and able to carry almost any plague weapon.
- [[Blightlord Terminators]] - A 2+ save, a 4+ invulnerable save and Objective Secured. A big unit can hold the middle of the table alone.
- [[Deathshroud Terminators]] - Elite melee bodyguards who protect your characters and can stretch their Contagion Range.
- [[Mortarion]] - The Daemon Primarch: a huge flying psyker whose Contagions reach full range from the first turn.
- [[Plagueburst Crawler]] - A very tough tank with a mortar that can hit targets out of sight.
- [[Poxwalkers]] - Cheap zombies that grow back as they kill, ideal for screening and holding your home objective.
- [[Lord of Contagion]] - A Terminator leader who adds 3" to his Contagion Range and lets nearby CORE units re-roll hit rolls of 1.
