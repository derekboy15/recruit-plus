# Army builder: known rules gaps (Milestone 2)

The list builder checks the matched-play army-building rules, but a few cases are still open. Most of them need a source the research library doesn't have, or a rule the BattleScribe data doesn't hold. Each line below says what a player would see.

## May wrongly flag a legal list

- **Disciples of Be'lakor with two allied detachments.** The army of renown allows two allies, but the app allows one at Incursion and Strike Force. A legal list shows "Remove the extra detachment".
- **Fallen with an Imperial Knights or Leagues of Votann ally.** If the Fallen count as IMPERIUM, the Arks of Omen pack may allow these allies. The app only lets the Fallen take a Chaos Knights Super-Heavy Auxiliary, so these allies are flagged as not allowed.
- **Lion El'Jonson must be the Warlord.** The BattleScribe data makes this compulsory, but no source we have says so. A Lion army with another Warlord is flagged.
- **Named Chapter Masters, one per army.** The BattleScribe data allows only one named Chapter Master (or Master of Sanctity) in an army. The research library only limits the Chapter Command upgrades, so a list with two named Chapter Masters may be flagged wrongly.
- **Kill Team Strike Force at Arks of Omen sizes.** This Deathwatch army of renown is still offered at Incursion and Strike Force. It was reportedly retired in January 2023, but we can't confirm that, so the app may offer an option that isn't legal.
- **Hive Tyrant must be the Warlord.** This follows the June 2022 dataslate, as the BattleScribe data does. The April 2023 update may have lifted it; if so, a Tyranids list with a different Warlord is flagged wrongly.

## May miss a problem

- **Genestealer Cults ally.** The ally must be Brood Brothers only. The app doesn't check this.
- **Drukhari ally.** The ally must be a different Drukhari type (Kabal, Wych Cult or Haemonculus Coven). The app doesn't check this.
- **Emperor's Children ally.** A Daemons ally must be Slaanesh only. The app doesn't check this.
- **Chaos Knights Dreadblade ally.** A squadron of 2–3 War Dogs is accepted as the "single Dreadblade" ally. The rule's wording is unclear on this.
- **Agents of the Imperium as guests.** The app lets Agents join an Astra Militarum Brood Brothers detachment, which the rules forbid. It also lets several Agents join one detachment, not just one per detachment.
- **Sub-faction keywords.** Chapters, regiments and similar keywords (KRIEG, CADIAN and so on) are not checked when units share a detachment.
- **Legion of the Damned Vanguard with no HQ.** This is an 8th-edition rule that isn't on the final datasheet, so the app doesn't check it either way.
- **UNALIGNED units in a detachment.** The app ignores UNALIGNED units in its faction checks. Whether they can share a detachment with a faction isn't settled.
- **Daemonic Pact's 25%.** The app treats the 25% Power Level limit as deciding whether the Daemons gain AGENT OF CHAOS, not as a legality cap. A larger Daemons detachment isn't flagged.
- **One Inquisitor per all-Inquisition detachment.** The app doesn't check this.
- **Haemoxytes.** The data leaves the AELDARI keyword off this unit, so an army-faction problem involving it may be missed.
- **Points and Power Level disagreements.** Sergeant Telion (PL 3 here, 4 on Wahapedia), Commander Dante (PL 9 here, 8 on Wahapedia) and Kâhl (100 pts here, 90 on Wahapedia) follow the BattleScribe data. No final GW source settles them, so a total may be off by that much.
- **Fixed Warlord Traits at Combat Patrol and Onslaught.** Named Necrons Warlords (the Silent King, Anrakyr, Szeras, Trazyn, Orikan) don't have to record their automatic trait, so a list can leave it out without a warning.
